# MAPPPD increasing-network intensification descriptive v1

**Status:** post-outcome descriptive decomposition only. This branch cannot modify or delay the frozen Ecology submission.

## Question

In the three already-identified increasing MAPPPD species × region networks, where did numerical growth occur?

The regional outcome is already known: abundance increased in all three panels while final effective breeding-site number E was lower in all three. The purpose here is only to decompose that exposed result among the already-retained monitored sites.

## Frozen panels

Use exactly the retained site rosters and complete seasons from the existing
`MAPPPD_REGIONAL_CONCENTRATION_RESULT_V1` analysis. No region, site, season, threshold, or calibration may be changed.

## Descriptive quantities

For each increasing network:
1. calibrated count and relative share for every retained site in the first and last complete season;
2. absolute count change;
3. share change;
4. initial and final abundance ranks;
5. number of retained sites that increased versus decreased;
6. identity and share trajectory of the initially dominant site;
7. identity and initial rank of the final dominant site;
8. fraction of **gross positive gains** contributed by the initially largest half of retained sites (ranked by first-season counts);
9. fraction of net regional growth contributed by the initially dominant site, reported descriptively even if >1 or <0 because other sites may decline.

No p-values are computed.

## Interpretation boundary

This analysis cannot demonstrate immigration, dispersal, colonisation, or individual movement.
All retained sites were already monitored and nonzero at the relevant first complete season,
so this analysis distinguishes redistribution/intensification among occupied sites, not colonisation of empty habitat.

A result in which a minority of initially large sites absorb most gains would motivate the
expansion–intensification switching hypothesis. A diffuse gain would weaken that interpretation.
