## Prospective ecological question (not yet an effect)
**Does the presence of a physical small-island boundary alter breeding success or breeding-pair response under comparable regional forcing, rather than area alone?** A novel mechanism would require a common protocol and same-season exposure (krill/SIC/wind, before reproductive outcomes) and independent islands/mainland systems. One adjacent island-mainland pair is a natural contrast to develop, **not sufficient to prove a universal island law**. Known multi-species breeding trends, tourist impacts and South Georgia krill dependence are prior art.
