## Four authenticated Australian Antarctic Division (AADC) CC BY 4.0 source candidates (exact endpoints)
- **2023–24 same-archipelago contemporaneous source**: Turbett & Thalmann (2025), *Point data for wildlife on Macquarie Island, 2023–2024*, DOI `10.26179/98ra-0751`, original first-party direct `https://data.aad.gov.au/eds/6113/download`, source record `https://data.aad.gov.au/metadata/records/macca_wildlife_gis`. Includes penguins, flying birds, seals, **source positions explicitly INDICATIVE ONLY**: king penguin chicks surveyed around August, wandering albatross nest/courtship at different breeding phases. A positive locality point need not be measured nest XY; no common-day penguin/albatross effort or surveyed bird absence assumed.
- **Historically recorded flying seabird colonies**: Harris (2001), *Macquarie Island Flying Birds GIS Dataset*, DOI `10.26179/5c494b686370e`, first-party `https://data.aad.gov.au/eds/1379/download`, metadata `macca_flyingbirds_gis`. Source polygons for albatrosses and giant petrels were **digitized from hand sketches at 1:50,000** and incomplete for petrels. Historical source date around 2000; point or polygons are NOT centimetre-accurate field nest footprints.
- **Historical penguin breeding polygons**: Harris (2015), *Macquarie Island Penguin Colonies GIS Dataset*, `https://data.aad.gov.au/eds/4192/download`, metadata `macca_penguins_gis`. Covers gentoo, king, rockhopper, royal, reports historical start 1979 but **NOT** verified same-date as flying species. No bird abundance change from map difference.
- **Independent geographic control**: *Macquarie Island and adjacent islands 1:50000 Coastline GIS Dataset*, `https://data.aad.gov.au/eds/2812/download`, source metadata `macca_coast_ambis_gis`. This is source coastline mapping, not actual 2000 or 2023 shore-entry trail; 1:50000 scale cannot establish sub-10m nest-to-shore distances.

All four are public AADC dataset records and give exact first-party file links and reuse terms (no private credentials / no unrelated downloads). Original Macquarie mammalian eradication history is published: feral cats eradicated 2001; invasive rabbits/rodents declared eradicated in 2014 (Australian Government 2024). Those are original conservation epochs, NOT site-specific paired rat detection records. Within one Macquarie main island, before/after source GIS geometries are not an independently matched field-survey natural experiment. Bird et al. 2024, Conservation Biology DOI `10.1111/cobi.14239` published quantitative burrowing-petrel post-eradication recovery: PRIOR ART; no reanalysis of their conclusions as new ecology.
