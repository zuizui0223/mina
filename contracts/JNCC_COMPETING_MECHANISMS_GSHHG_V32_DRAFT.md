# V32 DRAFT — Competing mechanisms of seabird breeding-site share redistribution

**State: DRAFT; NOT FROZEN; NO NEW MECHANISM OUTCOMES EXAMINED OR FITTED.**
**Prepared:** 2026-10-27. **Parent:** JNCC original-data PR #198, branch research/seabird-island-generality-v1. This is a post-discovery, pre-mechanism-association analysis plan, **not a prospectively registered replication of the already viewed V30 ecological result**.

## Purpose and inferential units

The V30 result from the authentic revised JNCC comparative workbook (original SHA256 9edf05b8af0a44c3ca770f390120c4b99bcaae3ae35fd844a82982d6ad8116d4) is preserved: 14,112 matched species-site records, 1,485 original structural parent panels, 1,483 after source-aggregate guard, 1,321 count-usable panels / 17 species / 604 Country × Master site networks, and 959 declining panels. Of those declines, 62.25% exceed the finite-count concentration reference, and 14/17 species have a positive species-level mean E. These are **already observed**, and this study does not rebrand them as newly preregistered results.

A **child** is the original Site.ID × species × same count unit (AON/AOS/AOT/AOB/IND), with two matched census counts; a **parent network** is species × Country × Master site × unit, with at least 3 genuinely non-aggregate children. Site.ID and Master-site names are NOT actual physical-island IDs, spatial polygons, within-parent dispersal observations, or independent species replicates. Original JNCC first/last years differ among records; no annual time series is created.

Use **three rival explanations** only. H1 is internal social/size-dependent retention, H2 is physical-island refuge/predator accessibility, and H3 is independently measured nesting-habitat quality. Survey artifacts are an **identifiability/negative-control gate**. Marine feeding access is a deferred fourth mechanism, NOT fitted with any post hoc proxy in V32.

## Locked hypotheses, directions and estimands — proposed for freeze

### H1. Size-dependent share retention; stronger in dense colony nesters

**H1a direction:** after conditioning on the same network's later total count and original component shares, relatively larger baseline child sites retain/increase their later shares **more** than smaller children. The signed size-retention coefficient is >0 **relative to a fitted-procedure multinomial allocation null**, not just a positive correlation of original size and later size.

**H1b direction:** that H1a coefficient is **more positive** in independently trait-coded densely colonial breeding species than in dispersed/territorial nesters. Species trait rank/class and reference must be fully frozen for all eligible 17 source taxa without consulting V30 species effect rankings or this study's outcomes. If trait evidence is insufficient/ambiguous, H1b stops; no swapping traits or choosing a favored taxon subset.

Social attraction, Allee effects, and population movement **cannot be directly diagnosed by only two adjusted-count snapshots**. An H1 positive is consistent with retention advantage but also site quality, observation changes, or unmeasured local foraging. No causal sociality claim without independently timed recruitment/fidelity or individual behavior.

### H2. Physical small-island / predator-refuge hypothesis

**Signed geographic-refuge prediction, ONLY if a parent network contains distinct, verified physical landmasses:** child breeding sites on predator-inaccessible or more remote physical islands retain more relative breeding share than otherwise similar children on nearer/invaded landmasses of the **same parent**. Greater minimum water-gap distance from Great Britain or Ireland is only a provisional access *proxy* (direction + for relative share retention), never certified predator absence. If the mixed-landmass within-parent source gate fails, the signed H2 geographical-refuge test is **NOT IDENTIFIED in this JNCC cohort**. Between-parent concentration E can then be compared *descriptively* across remote/near, small/large or water-gap groups, but it is **nondirectional** and CANNOT demonstrate retreat of birds to island refuges: low predation could preserve many patches and thus LOWER excess concentration. For geometry classification retain the prior V31 small offshore landmasses (0.01–1,000 km²), large landmasses (≥50,000 km²), and explicit intermediate 1,000–<50,000 km²; Great Britain and Ireland are large offshore islands, not continental mainland.

**Predator-specific signed prediction (only if independently verified, time-appropriate invasive mammal status is acquired):** otherwise similar, predator-absent breeding landmasses have greater proportional site-share retention than known predator-occupied landmasses. A positive sea-gap effect alone **does not establish predator-free status or mammalian predation as its cause**. Freeze island-specific rodent/mink/fox presence, eradication chronology, survey years, missingness, and provenance BEFORE joining any V30 E or share change.

**Critical unit constraint:** all-child-on-one-landmass panels in V31 provide **only between-network** small/large contrasts, not within-network landmass refuges. V31's Natural Earth structural audit had 229 small-class and 157 large-class source panels, but 1,092 unresolved/ambiguous-parent panels; it did NOT show island-to-island redistribution. A within-parent cross-landmass test is permitted ONLY on a separately structurally frozen cohort with ≥2 independently identifiable physical landmasses within the SAME parent and count-unit comparability. Otherwise H2 is a matched between-network exploratory association, not evidence of movement to refuge islands. Parent fixed effects cannot identify any predictor constant for every child in that parent.

### H3. Independently measured breeding-site quality

**H3 signed prediction:** a priori externally measured more suitable nesting substrate (e.g., mapped available nestable cliff/ground area or safely classifiable nesting-habitat strata) predicts relative share retention **after controlling for baseline size**. For quantitative usable area, expected coefficient >0. Raw site count, V30 original share, post-census colony size, original source Master-site label, and GSHHG island size alone do NOT measure independent nesting-site quality.

Any slope, cliff, substrate or habitat variable must come from a separately versioned first-party source at a declared spatial support and with validation that JNCC Start.Lat/Start.Long is representative of the actual surveyed colony or nesting footprint. Freeze source year, coordinate uncertainty, true surveyed habitat denominator and covariate uncertainty before joining counts. If only low-precision site centroids or incomplete habitat layers are available, H3 stops as **not identified**; do not substitute distance to coast or observed colony size after seeing results.

## Statistical contract (mechanism fits ONLY after source and trait freeze)

- Base V30 descriptive concentration E remains **E_g = C_g,late − [C_g,early + (1−C_g,early)/N_g,late]**, with C=sum child shares squared; do not retrospectively alter the 1,321-parent/959-decline scientific result.
- **Within-parent primary H1/H3 response:** later original adjusted child counts conditional on observed later parent total, with baseline smoothed proportions p0*=(n0+0.5)/(N0+0.5k) to allow previously observed numeric zero children. Multinomial **compositional** model q_i proportional to p0*_i × exp(beta_size × centered log(1+n0_i) + beta_quality × centered independent Q_i + prespecified observation controls). The 0.5 smoothing applies **only to new mechanism analysis**; V30's E uses original unsmoothed p0. Within-parent intercept is absorbed in normalization. Beta_size>0 and beta_quality>0 are the signed tests. Report raw Δp_i=p1_i−p0_i and its zero-sum constraint; never treat child observations as independent Poisson populations.
- **Size-null calibration:** at least 1,999 fixed-seed within-parent finite-count multinomial reallocations conditional on original N1 and p0*, re-fit the SAME model/model-comparison/statistic in each replicate. Evaluate effect relative to null distribution; account for uncertainty in observed p0 by an explicitly separated observation-error sensitivity, if feasible. These are idealized arithmetic controls, **not proof of a thinning demographic process**. Adjusted source counts do not justify naïve nest-level multinomial p values.
- **H1b trait interaction:** signed size × frozen species coloniality category, with species as the replication tier (17 at most); equal-species reporting, cluster at Country × Master-site network, and species leave-one-out. No treating thousands of Site.ID rows as 14,112 independent tests of a species-level trait.
- **H2 main signed estimand ONLY IF eligible mixed-landmass parents exist:** the within-parent relative share-retention contrast (farther/verified predator-free versus nearer/invaded) between CHILD site sets belonging to different **strictly verified landmasses**, with whole-network composition and other fixed covariates held constant. Do not pool source landmass units and do not claim observed movement or colonization. **Secondary descriptive only:** compare V30 E **between strictly identified, source- and species-balanced physical-landmass groups**, controlling count unit, country/latitude, census-year gap, earlier total N0, child-site number k and source geography. Pre-fix overlap/positivity before use; uncertainty clustered at actual landmass and Country × Master network. Between-network E has NO preregistered predator-refuge sign, and cannot identify within-network redistribution or causal refuge.
- **Observation controls and falsification:** explicit original NA is never zero; sample attrition/changed boundary/effort and adjusted survey-method metadata must be audited by species, epoch and site where possible. Report unequal 1998–2005 versus 2015–2021 census years (Gannet exception), original count units, and source country imbalance (758/959 V30 declines from Scotland). Compare an observation-error/null family and exclude unknown-method strata only by frozen completeness criteria. Parent fixed effects remove shared parent-level intercepts, **not** local quality, local prey access, within-panel census timing or time-varying survey differences.
- Three-mechanism family: report all H1a, H2-geography, H3 signed estimands plus H1b/predator-status conditional extensions, effect/uncertainty/eligible sample and falsification; do not claim a unique cause merely because one association is stronger. Future truly independent census/geography is needed for mechanism confirmation.

## Outcome-blind sample gates and STOP decisions

1. **Geographic readiness:** preserve the V31 predeclared minimum before any small-vs-large comparison: ≥20 eligible small and ≥20 eligible large parent panels, ≥5 distinct small physical landmasses, ≥2 large landmasses, and ≥5 species with ≥3 parents in each extreme class, **rechecked under strict GSHHG primary assignment**. If any fails, H2 small/large comparison STOPS; previous Natural Earth support cannot rescue it.
2. **Within-parent H2:** require ≥20 structurally paired mixed-landmass parents spanning ≥5 independent physical landmasses and ≥5 species with at least 2 exact-land children of different landmass identities per parent; otherwise no within-parent island-refuge fit. Thresholds are proposed in this draft, to be frozen before geography-qualified V32 effects.
3. **H1a:** ≥100 eligible original V30 count-compatible parents across ≥8 species and ≥30 distinct Country × Master networks; ≥3 count-unit categories; otherwise STOP signed size retention fit. H1b requires independent, fully referenced species trait coding for at least 8 species spanning both a priori density categories, with ≥3 species per category; otherwise H1b STOP.
4. **H3:** ≥40 exact-geolocated original parent panels across ≥5 species and ≥15 distinct Country × Master networks with independently measured quality on ≥3 matched children each, ≥2 quality values per parent and known source footprint/accuracy; otherwise H3 STOP.
5. **Predator-specific H2:** without first-party or credibly documented period-correct predator occupancy for both exposure categories, interpret only geographic distance as an unvalidated proxy and do not fit/label a predator effect.
6. All H1/H2/H3 positivity, missing-data and geography provenance tables must be materialized **before linking to original V30 outcome E or fitting any new share-retention outcome**. Gates failing are *data support stops*, not evidence for the null.
7. No fishing through continuous island-area thresholds, alternative GSHHG/OSM matching, site-name aliases, buffers, sociality categories or favorable taxon subsets once mechanisms are viewed. Exploratory exceptions require a **new labeled branch** and no confirmatory language.
