## Taxa, substrate guild hypotheses frozen independent of area/outcome effects

Main ecological comparison concerns the SEVEN **flying** species with direct observed breeding records, not Adélie versus the other taxa as an automatic "flightlessness disadvantage" test. The publisher explicitly notes Adélie penguins cannot occupy inland continental outcrops inaccessible from sea; any apparent island-vs-mainland zero for Adélie is a known structural access constraint, not a newly discovered island-size effect. Original source species codes and preassigned nesting guilds:

- **Exposed ground** (`OPEN`): `SGP` Southern giant petrel *Macronectes giganteus*; `SK` South polar skua *Stercorarius maccormicki*.
- **Rock ledge/slope** (`LEDGE`): `AP` Antarctic petrel *Thalassoica antarctica*; `CP` Cape petrel *Daption capense*; `SF` southern fulmar *Fulmarus glacialoides*.
- **Sheltered rock crevice** (`CREVICE`): `SP` snow petrel *Pagodroma nivea*; `WSP` Wilson's storm petrel *Oceanites oceanicus*.

These are comparative coarse habitat modes; species-specific exceptions and habitat ambiguity remain. Guild membership does not infer that every reported nest at a site is on a measured substrate. Adélie (`ADP`) retained only for original source sample coverage and habitat access caution; excluded from fitted primary seven-flying-species comparison. No extrapolation to marine penguins' individual flight/swimming dispersal.

## Prespecified non-nested versus nested area/insularity comparison

Observation response `Y=1` original P, `Y=0` original directly reported A. Leave each spatial 250km block out, train on all other blocks, compare fixed `LogisticRegression(C=0.3,solver=lbfgs,max_iter=500)`, feature scaling and imputation fit only on training blocks. Missing station distance imputed to training median with a missing indicator. No post-outcome variable selection, CV tuning, threshold hunting or biological p-value.

- **B0 — observation/geographic baseline:** seven species one-hot identities, original vantage (ground/ship/air) dummies, expert-validation flags, source season-start year, latitude, sin/cos longitude, log(1+distance-to-station), and source `sub_group` accounted by geographic block holdout rather than fixed source-group indicators.
- **B1 — physical available-land/site features:** B0 plus `log10(site area)`, `island` (island vs continental outcrop), and `island × log-area`. Tests conventional colonization/terrestrial resource size; not a novel ecological law.
- **B2 — nesting-mode contrasts:** B1 plus `LEDGE × log-area`, `CREVICE × log-area`, `LEDGE × island`, `CREVICE × island`, and their respective `guild × island × log-area`. OPEN is comparison reference. Species fixed effects are retained in all models; guild main effects are redundant given species identity and not added. Tests whether substrate guild conditions the physical island-size/area response.

**Primary outcome metric:** average over eligible heldout 250km spatial blocks of site×species-weighted Brier score (mean `(Y-predicted_P)^2`); report original observation-weighted Brier and blockwise scores separately but do NOT choose a model from the latter. Support for a *tentative descriptive nesting-guild effect* requires ALL: `Δ=Brier(B1)-Brier(B2)>0`, at least **2/3 of eligible blocks improving**, largest-data block improving, direction/sign of `CREVICE×island×logarea` coefficient stable between full dataset and removal of largest block, and adequate physical area overlap between both island/continental source types within those guilds. Missing credible overlap means `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE` even if Brier improves. No confirmatory p-values.

**Sensitivities (not rescue routes):** expert-validated original occurrence records only (`occurrence_expert_validation='Y'`), and original ground vantage only (`vantage='G'`), with identical model family and no hyperparameter re-tuning. Report results even if signs reverse. Any habitat probability is conditional on the original monitored/search-recorded P/A data; it is not a causal immigration, nest success, species richness or island island area-law result. Baseline true all-potential-islands background and environmental accessibility remain unobserved as a uniform survey. We never infer unreported Pygoscelis or volant species absence.

## Source-only clarification and explicit overlap gate (before fitting V14 effects)

Actual original V13 coverage showed some published geographic features labelled `continent` are coastal outcrops and can be occupied by Adélie penguins. The original paper's warning about **inland inaccessible continental rocks** must NOT be applied to all `continent`-coded geographic sites. The main V14 comparison is restricted to the seven flying birds **because guilds are replicated by multiple species**, not because Adélies are absent from all continent-coded sites.

The physical-overlap gate left qualitative above is fixed now: each of OPEN, LEDGE, CREVICE needs **at least 8 distinct original site IDs** with direct P/A reports for `island` **and** for `continent`, and 25th–75th percentile raw `log10(area)` intervals across the two classes must have strictly positive overlap for each guild. The original source total reported-area distributions by this interaction have **not** been modelled before freezing this threshold. A missing guild×land sample or no physical-area IQR overlap means the 3-way island-specific interpretation is `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`, not a zero biological effect. This gate does not change core B0–B2 models or create an optional sign-rescue subset.
