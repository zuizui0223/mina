For Adélie and chinstrap penguins, present in all three source islands, ask whether **coastal nesting access information generalizes between real islands beyond DEM elevation and local slope**. Original V27 species held-out island DEM elevation+slope-only model improved balanced Brier on just 1/3 candidate islands, so this is an explicitly post-result mechanistic stress test. Frozen prediction for *new access information*: adding D_coast to the existing DEM elevation+slope baseline improves heldout balanced Brier on >=2/3 real source islands AND equal-island mean Brier gain >=0.005. Failure means the simple coastal access proxy does not transfer across the 3 original islands. No tuning after result.
