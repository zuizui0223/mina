**Independent source-defined measurement clarification before V31 outcome:** AADC original population_counts_BI registry DOI 10.26179/5c9bf6704cf70 explicitly defines post-1990 values as occupied nests around ~2 December at Béchervaise, Verner and Petersen, while older ANARE counts can be time-corrected and are not comparable. AADC separate *Bechervaise breeding success* dataset (DOI 10.4225/15/59829c7ed97b1) describes **repeated manual nest counts by multiple field observers, averaging sufficiently agreeing counts**, making non-integer published averaged nest estimates expected and biologically meaningful. This detailed averaging protocol is documented for Béchervaise, NOT independently established for Verner. Therefore raw numerical original source *estimates* (floating point) are not necessarily errors; numeric values are comparable only as **reported occupied-nest survey indices**, not integer numbers of separate living pairs with known measurement variance.
