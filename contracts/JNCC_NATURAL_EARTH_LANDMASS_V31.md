Natural Earth warns **large continental polygons are deliberately split into adjacent pieces** for convenience; they are not independent physical landmasses. Thus extract all original WGS84 polygons that intersect a predeclared UK and Ireland bounding envelope lon -13.5 to +3.5, latitude 48 to 63.5. Spatially UNION any edge-sharing major land shards and minor islands into connected landmasses before measuring area; do not use raw shape record area as physical island area. Record number of original fragments and merged components. Use ETRS89/LAEA EPSG3035 for true area in km² and distance to polygon.
