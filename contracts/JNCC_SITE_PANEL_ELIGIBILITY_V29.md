## Eligibility fixed before any original abundance values
- For each source main table row after original row 4, verify nonblank original species, country, county, Master site name, Site.ID original numeric positive integer, source latitude/longitude finite and UK/Ireland plausible.
- Require two source census year fields K, N to be source-integer years within 1998–2005 (earlier Gannet exception) and 2015–2021 respectively; no year invention.
- Require BOTH original L and O adjusted count fields to be a literal numeric/typed numeric **nonnegative finite candidate**, but count magnitudes are NOT parsed, summed, ranked or exposed until a separately frozen effect analysis. Zero is lexical numeric candidate; explicit NA and source blanks are *missing*, not zero. No author percent change/annual change Q or R is read as outcome or used to select panels.
- Original count unit strings in M and P must agree exactly and belong to {AON,AOS,AOT,AOB,IND}; ambiguous or numeric units excluded. Exclude mixed sources from a panel; sites with source mismatched units are not present.
- The (original species, Country, Master site, Site.ID) source key must occur EXACTLY ONCE. Entire duplicate key groups excluded. A source row already contains census aggregates for same Site.ID, NOT two separate annually repeated site observations. Duplicate site IDs across different species are expected and not two colony components for the same species.
- Parent analysis candidate: distinct species × original Country × Master site, with at least THREE unambiguously paired, matched-unit Site.ID components. Original Master site may be a geographical/administrative group and might overlap individual Site counts; this eligibility is not proof of one demographically closed colony.
- Before looking at effects, require at least 20 distinct eligible panels, 8 distinct seabird species, 15 distinct Master sites, and >=3 of the original census units represented. If any fails, stop. Passing is structural eligibility **only**, not a biological effect.
- Between-period boundaries/coverage can still change even with same numerical Site.ID; source report calls two-period records matched comparative adjusted counts but does not contain an explicit historical polygon crosswalk. No physical islandness inferred from text master-site names, UK Great Britain / Ireland are themselves island landmasses, and no island–continental comparison is possible without a separate physically anchored and independently available coast/land-polygon reference.
- Later substantive ecological test must freeze proportional-thinning calibrated N_eff change and/or two-census persistence/turnover response before number magnitudes; never select only declining panels based on observed decline after seeing results, and account for unequal census periods. A two-census dataset cannot measure annual synchrony.
- This is an independent 25-species North Atlantic seabird programme. It does NOT independently replicate the Antarctic penguin observation processes or causal mechanisms. PR197 V27 stays closed without model retuning; frozen Ecology Paper1 PR189 unchanged.
