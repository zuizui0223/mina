If any fails STOP without claiming a positive spatial mechanism; a source-support failure is not a biological null.
5. **A priori descriptive primary group is all total-count-declining panels**, conditional on both-period observed matched sites. No post-effect selecting "best" declining species. Report all positive, stable and declining cohort sizes. Species weights equal: calculate mean finite-count corrected Simpson concentration residual within each species and then average species means, including each species with >=3 declining supported panels. Species directional "support" requires equal-species mean >0 and at least 2/3 of eligible species having positive mean residual; also report fraction of actual parent panels positive, no p values.
6. Observed concentration C_t = sum_i (n_{i,t}/N_t)^2 and effective component number N_eff,t = 1/C_t, based only on SAME original child-site IDs in both snapshots. Under a fixed original site probability p_i=n_i,0/N0 and N1 independent count units allocated proportionally as multinomial draws, exactly expected concentration in the later count is **C_expected = C0 + (1-C0)/N1**. Predeclared corrected residual E = C1 - C_expected. A positive E means the observed later count is concentrated MORE than this idealized finite-count proportional-allocation benchmark; a negative E means MORE even. This is an arithmetic benchmark, NOT literal ecological breeding-attempt random survival for adjusted surveys and does not include observation error, boundary revisions or new recruitment.
7. Distinguish changing COMPOSITION from changing CONCENTRATION. Predeclare descriptive total variation T = (1/2) sum_i |p_i,late - p_i,early| across sites; it detects shifting breeding sites even if N_eff stays constant. Report median T for declining panels and fraction of declining panels with T>=0.25 but E<=0, an exploratory structural distinction (not an inferred source field extinction). Zero count transitions are recorded zeros within the comparative row, but cannot by themselves establish ecological extirpation or immigration.
8. Sensitivities frozen: all-unit declines and main annualized log count change per parent from the actual two years; per-species equal-weight mean E; leave-one-Country-out equal-species mean E. No tuning thresholds, selecting decline depth, removing inconvenient outliers, or treating nested parental panels as independent islands.
9. **No physical island versus large-island/continental mechanism yet**. A separate GIS source and calibrated site/land polygon boundary mapping are necessary; Great Britain and Ireland are large islands, not land-connected continental controls. Do not classify island from a site name such as "Clare Island" or assume every Master-site is a discrete landmass. Two multi-year census rounds cannot test yearly spatial synchrony/competitive interactions, and the source includes methodological adjusted counts and original site-boundary history unknown.
10. Prior literature already reports global species trend and source-census distributions. The only new (exploratory) question is whether **within-Master-site composition is more concentrated than exact finite-count proportional allocation permits**, and how that differs across taxa and geographic monitoring networks. Avoid claim of universal island mechanics or causal demography.
