# V26 — Fixed-baseline-colony and phenology audit of Bird–Signy period-dependent growth synchrony

**Frozen 2026-10-21 after the V25 original biological results were known**. This is a transparently post-result *robustness diagnosis* of a previously exploratory negative V23 short-epoch result, not an independent hypothesis confirmation or a new positive ecological law.

## Diagnosed ambiguity
V25 found original BAS Bird Island vs Signy Gentoo annual within-source nest/pair log1p growth r=+0.1656165 over 33 valid adjacent-year windows, +0.5466331 for 14 start years before 1997, -0.1440887 for 19 starts after 1997, and -0.3342093 in 2011–2018. But V25 compares 5, 6, or 7 changing Bird Island colony components depending on year. The pre-1997/post-1997 difference could reflect a **changing component sampling roster**, even if every compared individual annual transition internally matches its site. Signy also adopted standard CCAMLR/CEMP monitoring methods from the 1996/97 season, confounding ecological and methodological interpretation.

## Outcome-independent fixed-cohort decision
1. Reuse V25's publisher-linked source reader, actual SHA assertions, duplicate-group exclusion, original austral year rule, exact original YEAR_START=1982 / YEAR_END=2019, and recorded minimum five same-colony units per V25 transition. Before inspecting NEW fixed-roster effect coefficients, exact **reference V25 historic r** must reproduce within 1e-10 both entire period +0.165616495725728 and V23 2011–2018 -0.33420933224382615.
2. Declare the smaller constant Bird colony roster as the **whole set of names observed in both 1982 and 1983** (earliest V25 eligible adjacent-season pair). Do not choose by original nest count magnitude, correlation, Signy behavior, site size or later year outcome. This anchor is a diagnostic design chosen AFTER V25 effects; it does not retroactively make the test confirmatory.
3. Include each V25 structurally valid annual start-year ONLY if all baseline colony names have their original Bird Island records in both successive years. Entire transition excluded if any fixed-baseline colony absent, ambiguous or source-pinned numeric count missing; never let different Bird components enter the mean in different years. Signy original pairs must be actual numeric counts both years, zero permitted in log1p, missing not zero.
4. Frozen support for a descriptive FIXED-roster coefficient: at least **24** original strictly consecutive pair-season start years, with at least **8** years with starts <1997 and at least **12** with starts ≥1997. Otherwise HOLD without fixed-roster correlations; do not reduce colony count or time windows to seek a pattern.
5. Bird Island annual change is mean of ORIGINAL source log1p nest counts across precisely the baseline-anchored colony list, equally weighted. Signy annual change is original within-source log1p breeding pairs. The source pair- vs nest-count stage is still not equivalent; static scaling alone does not remove time-varying observer/stage effects.
6. Fixed endpoints: whole-history Pearson r and pre-1997/post-1997 Pearson r, the earlier-minus-modern *difference of correlations* for description only, Signy/Bird same-growth-direction counts by period, original start-year roster, and leave-one-pair-year-out r ranges within each phase. Do not claim significance from n=2 islands or repeatedly correlated annual differences.
7. **Original monitoring date QA separately, without filtering outcomes:** Bird Island median source "Date nests counted" (July–June phase) versus original Signy "DATE_PAIR_COUNT" where parseable, for all matching years; report pre-1997 and post-1997 median absolute phase gap, parsed/missing count, and >=30-day counts. Do not reselect survey dates, tune a phenology exclusion threshold, or infer same field day.
8. If the period contrast persists under fixed-baseline colony identities, **changing Bird Island component roster alone** is insufficient to explain the observed pattern; source stage/protocol and ecological regime confounding remains unresolved. If it disappears or reverses, original "nonstationarity" was panel-roster sensitive and must be demoted. Even a robust sign change is not a demonstration of mechanistic climate regime shift, fish/krill, wind, ice, competition or true long-distance ecological decoupling.

No new provider/source/weather covariates or threshold tuning in this V26. V24 original prebreeding SST test remains stopped; frozen Ecology PR189 unchanged.
