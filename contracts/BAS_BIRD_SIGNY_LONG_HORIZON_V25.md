# V25 — Fixed-roster historical Bird Island vs Signy Gentoo growth stationarity

Frozen 2026-10-21 AFTER seeing the short 2011–2018 Bird–Signy r=−0.33420933224382615 from original BAS V23 and after the exploratory NOAA ERSSTv6 winter SST V24 was stopped due to one nonfinite source grid value. An explicitly exploratory temporal GENERALITY check, not an independent pre-registered test or causal island/climate analysis. Ecology PR189 and closed V14–V17 remain unchanged.

## Ecological question
Is the apparent short-epoch anti-alignment in gentoo breeding attempts between remote South Georgia Bird Island and South Orkney Signy Island **persistent across the longer independently reported BAS monitoring histories**, or does it vary with time and observation protocol? This is a biological regional-variation description, NOT direct immigration, predation, krill or wind causation.

## Freeze unit, time scale, and stop gates before opening historical effects
1. Use entire ORIGINAL overlap years **1982 through 2019** inclusive, without post-result timespan selection. Strictly parse original Bird DD-Mon-YY nesting dates and classify July–June austral season from calendar month; accept only source Gentoo and fixed seven listed colonies, and exclude ALL duplicate Colony×Season source groups.
2. Signy use exact original consecutive YYYY-YY or YYYY-YYYY season year, no guessed correction to malformed 1998-8895 source row; use only Gentoo. Exclude all duplicate original season-year source groups in full.
3. Source structure gate requires >=20 distinct **consecutive year-to-next-year Bird/Signy paired intervals** with >=5 of the SAME original Bird colonies recorded in each consecutive year and one Signy row for each year. Both years must be present, no >1-year gaps disguised as annual.
4. Only after structure gate passes, parse nonnegative finite numerical original nests/pairs, preserve true 0 in log1p and exclude invalid source observations (never recode NA). Numeric support must still contain >=20 annual intervals with >=5 same Bird colonies or stop before any outcome correlation.
5. Bird Island annual growth is equal-weight mean of log(1+nest[t+1])−log(1+nest[t]) across each interval's **same named colonies** (>=5), not raw colony sums or a changing number of independent islands. Signy annual growth is log(1+pairs[t+1])−log(1+pairs[t]). This is a **within-series** change comparison with different field-stage protocols; fixed population-size scaling does not automatically cancel after log1p for tiny counts.
6. Pre-selected descriptive endpoints: (i) all-eligible-historical Pearson r Bird–Signy, (ii) after-1996/97 standard CCAMLR period (intervals starting >=1997) Pearson r if >=12 annual windows, (iii) older pre-standardization segment intervals <1997 if >=8 annual windows, (iv) leave-one-window-out r min/max and growth-sign concordance. No p-values, no tuned lags/windows, no new stage thresholds, no testing different colony groups after outcomes.
7. Internal V23 consistency: reproduce the exact previously observed **2011–2018** Bird–Signy coefficient −0.33420933224382615 using the *same 8 year-to-year windows* (even if historical longer series has gaps) or fail-closed before publishing new historical effects. Historic pre/post splits are transparent protocol-sensitivity checks, not independent hypothesis confirmation.
8. If historical r differs sharply from the fixed short-epoch r, identify nonstationarity/period dependence as an important scientific limitation, not evidence of changing marine environmental causes. If it agrees, call a descriptively persistent remote-region contrast, still no physical island-distance, breeding climate or demographic rescue mechanism. Bird and Signy are only two independent physical islands no matter how many colony/years are measured.
