# V23 — Gentoo same-provider near-versus-far annual nest-growth contrasts

Frozen on 2026-10-21 after the positive V22 Bird Island–Maiviken original result was known, but BEFORE fitting either Signy far-region effect. This is an EXPLORATORY, prospectively specified follow-up, not a confirmatory universal island law or post hoc rescue of the closed V14–V17 series. No change to Ecology PR #189.

## Competing descriptive predictions
V20 showed King George–Livingston gentoo census-growth correlation +0.6349 (both NOAA) but weak correlations with distant BAS Signy; provider and geography were confounded. V22 showed +0.5621534427494942 between Bird Island (a separate offshore island) and Maiviken on South Georgia main island (both broadly BAS-monitored), across ten 2009–2018 year-to-year windows. The known positive V22 effect motivated this comparison. Within the BAS system, a new Signy comparator allows a directional descriptive hypothesis: SAME-REGION Bird–Maiviken synchrony is stronger than the mean correlation of Bird–Signy and Maiviken–Signy. The opposite result or a near-zero gradient would not support geographical regionalization. Distinct BAS stations and ecological regions may still use different observers/protocols.

## Outcome-independent cohort and fixed analysis
- Download each exact original BAS publisher URL via published V21/V18 official routes; stop on SHA256 drift, missing original headings or nonpublisher redirects.
- Reconstruct the frozen V22 2009–2019 Bird–Maiviken cohort with the same original dates, seven equal-weighted colonies, 30-day seasonal stage gate and year matching. The full original V22 r must reproduce +0.5621534427494942 to <=1e-10, or STOP. Do not choose a different year or adjust a colony for a positive Signy contrast.
- Signy original SEASON label must be a valid consecutive austral YYYY–YY/YYYY-YYYY, no guessed or repaired malformed season (including 1998-8895). Require original Species explicitly Gentoo, valid single original row per season; whole duplicated years excluded. Original literal missing/NA/nonnumeric TOTAL_NUMBER_OF_PAIRS is unknown, never a zero.
- Use EXACT common annual growth windows with numeric-valid source records at all THREE physical monitored island units. Required at least EIGHT common complete adjacent-year windows, out of the ten V22 windows. No pair-specific partial years to inflate near versus far differences.
- For each common window, compute within-original-series log(1+nests/pairs) difference; Bird is the arithmetic mean of seven colony log-change values, not log of sum and not 7 independent islands. Use original V22 V1/ Maiviken signals without modification. Between-dataset level/size comparisons forbidden. Stage-dependent and time-varying measurement bias does NOT necessarily cancel.
- Primary predeclared three Pearson correlations on the SAME windows: r_near Bird–Maiviken, r_far1 Bird–Signy, r_far2 Maiviken–Signy. Descriptive gradient DELTA = r_near – 0.5*(r_far1+r_far2). Report exact common window roster, three signs and same-direction counts, leave-one-window-out DELTA range (no p-value, no significance claim, no split chosen after results).
- If DELTA > 0, label a POSITIVE DESCRIPTIVE within-BAS near-vs-far gradient, not an effect of water barrier, sea-ice, weather, prey, island area or migration. If <=0, label THIS direction unsupported. If fewer than eight windows, zero variance or any verification failure, stop before interpreting a gradient. No threshold/radius/source/response/model rescue on seeing outcome.
- These three sites are three physical islands/landmasses; the two South Georgia sites do not form a multi-island experimental replicate set. Same-provider does not imply same field observer. Year-to-year changes can be autocorrelated and closely sampled region effects cannot be disentangled with one within-region physical pair. Do not average with NOAA V20 as statistically independent replication.

Required next mechanistic evidence: >= several replicated coast/island pairs with harmonized census stage and independently dated weather, marine access and breeding habitat, adjusted for observer and source. Frozen Paper1 Ecology PR189 remains unchanged.
