Date 2026-10-21. A separate prospective data-source branch, not part of the Antarctic penguin PR197 or frozen Ecology PR189. Original JNCC revised 8 July 2024 comparative file, 1998–2002 Seabird 2000 versus 2015–2021 Seabirds Count, 25 seabird species and >10,000 sites in the latter complete census:
https://jncc.gov.uk/resources/63f0ea40-485d-46dd-b967-150df90a7b2b
Actual publisher original XLSX URL:
https://data.jncc.gov.uk/data/63f0ea40-485d-46dd-b967-150df90a7b2b/comparative-seabirds-count-dataset-revised-20240708.xlsx

Biological motivation developed AFTER source-limited V27 Antarctic nest-change spatial-sign test. Ask whether an independently monitored bird system reveals colony persistence/turnover and island/mainland dependence across two censuses, not whether the UK census independently replicates Antarctic annual synchrony. Two cross-sectional census periods **cannot** test annual population synchrony or colony contraction without compatible persistent component identities.

## Source-only V28 gate
1. Download the EXACT official 2024 revision HTTPS from data.jncc.gov.uk, no alternate versions, bounded file <=12 MiB, reject foreign redirects, corrupt ZIP, malicious paths or oversized uncompressed content. Record publisher original SHA256 and file bytes.
2. Inspect original XLSX workbook/sheet XML only for sheets, declared source dimensions, first 12 original row header TEXT labels, nonempty cell counts, typed numeric-cell counts and source sheet sizes. Do not inspect or reproduce abundance magnitudes, infer site loss or zero abundance, calculate rates, select species based on outcome, or fit an ecological model at this source-inventory stage.
3. Do not assume that a colony site from Seabird 2000 corresponds to an identically named site in Seabirds Count. Before any cohort is frozen, verify a stable site code or documented polygon/crosswalk and whether the source contains explicit true-zero observations, missingness, count-unit methods and coverage.
4. Even if 25 species and >10,000 source sites are advertised, original census fields may represent aggregated regional totals or differently bounded units. These are NOT 10,000 longitudinal island populations. Do not assume every original record has both census periods.
5. Compare island versus mainland only after independent JNCC physical coastline/land polygon evidence, with geographically matched offshore and mainland sites and species stage-matched count units. Consider site-survey effort and length of exposure between 1998–2002 and 2015–2021. Do NOT pool with Antarctic records as equivalent habitat or sampling environment.
6. A macro-test design can proceed only after verifying species, source site ID and both-period count columns, plus an explicit source-study linkage/validity rule; freeze subsequent ecological hypotheses and holdouts BEFORE looking at counts. If original file inaccessible, record the failed acquisition rather than relying on JNCC's published 25-species/10,000-site summary.
7. OGL 3.0 source licensing is reported in JNCC Resource Hub; preserve attribution and cite JNCC. This is a geographically non-Antarctic generality exercise, kept distinct from Penguin Paper1/PR197.
