## Precisely frozen response and predictors

For site \(i\), species \(s\), breeding start year \(t\):
- Response \(g_{ist}=\log(1+N_{ist})-\log(1+N_{is,t-1})\); using observed nest counts, so it measures *reported breeding counts*, not survival, immigration, recruitment, population growth of adults or successful offspring.
- Primary marine exposure \(\Delta I_{it} = SIC_{25-75km,i,t} - SIC_{25-75km,i,t-1}\); secondary marine exposure \(\Delta I^{far}_{it} = SIC_{75-150km,i,t} - SIC_{75-150km,i,t-1}\). Those are *interannual differences of independently standardized Oct–Nov sea-ice concentrations*; static 1981–2010 baseline cancels. It is neither empirically observed prey nor colony fast ice.
- Static \(\log(1+R_i)\) where \(R_i\) is 2025 ADD medium mapped exposed-rock km² inside 2km source site center. Island \(A_i=1\) if high-res source site is linked to a disjoint standalone 2026 land polygon, 0 for the largest contiguous Antarctic mainland polygon. Both are at most preliminary, season-invariant site covariates; no annual ice-free habitat variation is observed.
- Observation controls are **original** current/previous precision (1–5; smaller is better), \(\log(1+N_{is,t-1})\), species and breeding year, latitude and Antarctic longitude represented cyclically as \(\sin(lon), \cos(lon)\). These are observational adjustments, not causal randomization.

## Frozen nested predictor comparisons, with independent geographic holdouts

Compare **three prespecified ridge regressions**, \(\alpha=20\) in z-scaled features, intercept unpenalized, using no fitted ecological effects to choose variables:
1. **B0 geographical/timing observation baseline:** centered year and year², previous log count, precision grades for original two source observations, latitude, sin/cos longitude, categorical species. These controls are required in all subsequent models.
2. **B1 marine-only predictor:** B0 plus near/far interannual sea-ice differences and species×near, species×far interactions. This tests species differences in marine response, without land effects.
3. **B2 land×sea-island candidate:** B1 plus island indicator, logrock, island×logrock, near×island, near×logrock and **near×island×logrock**. Only the full three-way interaction is proposed as specifically island-boundary-dependent in the original V1 contract. No fishing over additional radii, lags, thresholds or arbitrary polynomials after outcomes.

**Prediction gate**: exclusive **leave-one-spatial-250-km-block-out (12 blocks)** training and heldout prediction, each block receives *equal weight in final macro RMSE/MSE*, so the dominant block cannot vote 217 times. Standardize continuous features in the training fold only; impute missing original precision with training-fold median and add missingness indicator if needed; fixed \(\alpha=20\) no tuning. Report aggregate observation-weighted MSE separately but do NOT choose the best by that metric. Primary result \(Δ_{land} = macroMSE(B1)-macroMSE(B2)\) where macroMSE is mean of block MSEs, with positive indicating land-island features improved heldout prediction. Also \(Δ_{marine} = macroMSE(B0)-macroMSE(B1)\). Report per-block signed gains, mean/median, number of blocks improved, **largest-block heldout MSE** and (for B2) original fit coefficient on fully interaction near×island×logrock. **Do not attach confirmatory p-values** to this original exploratory model.
- **Minimum substantive criterion** for a *tentative* land×sea pattern (NOT proof): positive equal-block Δland, at least **8/12 blocks improving**, the *largest* heldout block improving, and interaction coefficient sign consistent when all data vs training **excluding that largest block**. If any fail, label the full H1–H3 as **NOT_SUPPORTED_BY_THIS_DATA**, even if a naive all-record p-value could be small. A small but positive CV score is not causally identified.
- **Additional negative checks, not rescue routes**: leave-one-species-out (no relabeling species) and leave-largest-block-out coefficient sign and magnitude. No method to claim land-specificity when mainland coverage or spatial blocks have poor overlap.
- Credibility limits: 61 sites and 12 spatial regions, overlapping publisher sources, coastal location error, contemporaneous long-term gradual sea-ice/habitat changes not observed, 25km marine proxy, origin years 1980–2020, and recorded nest counts sensitive to phenological survey effort. Do not equate 406 pairs to 406 independent islands.
