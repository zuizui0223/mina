# V20: Within-series annual gentoo growth synchrony on three physical islands

Frozen 2026-10-20 before any source count magnitude was read, separate from closed V14–V17 and frozen Ecology PR189.

Original source pinned NOAA NCEI accession 0185113 XLSX SHA256 a57370f82adf82b53da11ce5d85dada1ae3f3750ef246312b59d0f168763eef4 and BAS Signy Gentoo original CSV SHA256 53fe3821f313ed35840376fcf4d11c8e0e8fbbd63f01ba84a5e21c2db4df6d6b. NOAA columns Copa_Gentoo and CapeShirreff_Gentoo are generic seasonal penguin census counts, whereas BAS TOTAL_NUMBER_OF_PAIRS is a nesting-stage breeding-pair index from ten fixed colonies. Their levels, colony scales and count stages are not automatically equivalent. Exact original V19 joint lexical census years 1998–2009 and 2011–2014 are fixed; one malformed BAS label 1998-8895 remains excluded. Missing source cells are NOT biological zero.

Algebra: for a time-invariant local positive measurement multiplier, log(count[t+1]/count[t]) removes that factor. Hence limit biological exploration to positive *within-source* annual log changes, never the raw pooled counts. Time-varying effort, survey extent and phenology may still bias this estimand and are not corrected by a log ratio.

Predefined minimum: 8 consecutive year windows with positive original count at all three monitored island sites in both years (out of at most 14 original V19 windows). If fewer, stop before correlations; preserve real zeros and missing separately. No addition of 2010 or any new species, rescaled thresholds, cherry-picked time subset or log1p.

Primary descriptive outputs: pairwise Pearson r for King George–Livingston, King George–Signy and Livingston–Signy annual gentoo log changes; average r across three pairs; exact fraction of eligible windows with all three growth rates strictly positive or strictly negative (zeros excluded from sign denominator). Report yearly derived log changes, not original source magnitudes. Sensitivities frozen: sign concordance and mean pairwise r in 1998–2005 versus 2006–2014 starts; leave-one-window-out min/max r; within-original-NOAA monitored site Gentoo/Chinstrap growth r at Copacabana and Cape Shirreff in the SAME V19 common-year roster (even though extra same-site data may exist). No biological p-values or causal environment inference.

The original NOAA category is only species census count as defined by https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/55436; BAS gentoo methodological denominator is ground-survey breeding pair index https://data.bas.ac.uk/full-record.php?id=GB%2FNERC%2FBAS%2FPDC%2F01540. Three geographically close island sources cannot establish general Antarctic island biogeographic law, predator mechanism, recruitment, or krill/ice cause, and original series may repeat values in other published products. A mechanistic test requires independent island-resolved contemporaneous environmental covariates and more independent island populations.
