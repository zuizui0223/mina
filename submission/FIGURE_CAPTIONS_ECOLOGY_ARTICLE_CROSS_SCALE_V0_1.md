## Figure 1. Declining penguin populations lose effective monitored breeding components

Annual breeding-pair abundance and effective breeding-component number for the five within-system population trajectories, each indexed to 100 in its first eligible season. Palmer panels are Cormorant, Humble and Litchfield Adélie penguins; Signy panels are the prospectively frozen Adélie and chinstrap replications. Effective component number is (E=1/\sum p_j^2). Palmer uses unchanged colony-code rosters; Signy uses the frozen monitored-unit panels. The figure is descriptive; inferential support comes from the fixed-composition count-error nulls summarized in Figure 2.

## Figure 2. Breeding-space concentration recurs when the spatial component is moved one level up

**A. Within breeding systems.** First-to-last percentage change in effective monitored breeding-component number for the five Palmer and Signy trajectories. Labels report one-sided probabilities under the most severe frozen 20%-CV count-error sensitivity. All five within-system populations show supported contraction beyond proportional thinning.

**B. Regional monitored site networks.** Observation-error-calibrated regional concentration effect, (Delta\kappa=\kappa_{obs}-\mathrm{median}(\kappa_{null})), for the four structurally estimable declining species × APBP-region networks. Positive values indicate stronger abundance-linked concentration than expected under the panel-specific fixed-composition null after the frozen observation-error sensitivity. Circles denote networks individually supported under both frozen regional nulls; squares denote networks with the same positive direction but without individual support. All four effects are positive; the South Shetland Adélie and chinstrap networks are individually supported.

## Figure 3. Numerical recovery does not necessarily reconstruct the regional breeding-site distribution

First-to-last percentage change in adjusted regional abundance against first-to-last percentage change in effective breeding-site number for all seven eligible MAPPPD species × region networks. Circles are declining networks and triangles are increasing networks. All three increasing networks end with lower effective breeding-site number than in their first retained complete season, despite higher abundance. This panel is descriptive context only: no regional ratchet or hysteresis test was frozen before the increasing-network outcomes were inspected.

## Figure S1. The same concentration endpoint arose through different nominal component-level routes

Post-hoc descriptive trajectories of the initially dominant and ultimately dominant monitored breeding units in the five within-system populations. At Palmer, the component that was largest initially had zero breeding pairs by the final eligible census in all three populations and dominance shifted to another component. At Signy, the initially dominant component remained dominant and increased its share in both Adélie and chinstrap penguins. These summaries carry no inferential p-values and do not alter the concentration classifications.
