# Figure captions — Ecology Article cross-scale v0.1

## Figure 1. Declining penguin populations lose effective monitored breeding components

Annual summed monitored breeding-pair abundance and effective breeding-component number for the five within-system monitoring trajectories, each indexed to 100 in its first eligible season. Palmer panels are Cormorant, Humble and Litchfield Adélie penguins; Signy panels are the prospectively frozen Adélie and chinstrap replications. Effective breeding-component number (E) is defined in the Methods. Palmer uses unchanged colony-code rosters from the historical fixed sample-colony monitoring panel; Signy uses the frozen monitored-unit panels. The figure is descriptive; inferential support comes from the fixed-composition count-error nulls summarized in Figure 2.

## Figure 2. Decline-associated concentration is strong within breeding systems and directionally recurrent in declining regional networks

**A. Within breeding systems.** First-to-last percentage change in effective monitored breeding-component number for the five Palmer and Signy trajectories. Labels report one-sided probabilities under the most severe frozen 20%-CV count-error sensitivity. All five within-system monitoring trajectories show supported contraction beyond proportional thinning.

**B. Declining regional monitored site networks.** Observation-error-calibrated regional abundance–concentration effect Δκ (defined in the Methods) for the four structurally estimable declining species × APBP-region networks. Positive values indicate stronger abundance-linked concentration than expected under the panel-specific fixed-composition null after the frozen observation-error sensitivity. Circles denote networks individually supported under both frozen regional nulls; squares denote networks with the same positive direction but without individual support. All four effects are positive; South Shetland Adélie and chinstrap are individually supported. This panel describes only the declining regional subset and is not a claim that regional concentration is decline-specific.

## Figure 3. Regional breeding-site concentration is not restricted to declining abundance trajectories

First-to-last percentage change in adjusted regional abundance against first-to-last percentage change in effective breeding-site number for all seven eligible MAPPPD species × region networks. Circles are declining networks and triangles are increasing networks. All three increasing networks end with lower effective breeding-site number than in their first retained complete season; Victoria Land Adélie and Central-west Antarctic Peninsula gentoo show substantial E declines despite abundance growth. This is a main interpretive boundary on the decline-specific regional narrative, but not a confirmatory trend-asymmetry or hysteresis test because no such regional endpoint was frozen before these outcomes were inspected.
