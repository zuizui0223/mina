## Figure 1. Declining penguin populations lose effective monitored breeding components

Annual breeding-pair abundance and effective breeding-component number for the five within-system population trajectories, each indexed to 100 in its first eligible season. Palmer panels are Cormorant, Humble and Litchfield Adélie penguins; Signy panels are the prospectively frozen Adélie and chinstrap replications. Effective breeding-component number (E) is defined in the Methods. Palmer uses unchanged colony-code rosters; Signy uses the frozen monitored-unit panels. The figure is descriptive; inferential support comes from the fixed-composition count-error nulls summarized in Figure 2.

## Figure 2. Breeding-space concentration recurs when the spatial component is moved one level up

**A. Within breeding systems.** First-to-last percentage change in effective monitored breeding-component number for the five Palmer and Signy trajectories. Labels report one-sided probabilities under the most severe frozen 20%-CV count-error sensitivity. All five within-system populations show supported contraction beyond proportional thinning.

**B. Regional monitored site networks.** Observation-error-calibrated regional concentration effect Δκ (defined in the Methods), for the four structurally estimable declining species × APBP-region networks. Positive values indicate stronger abundance-linked concentration than expected under the panel-specific fixed-composition null after the frozen observation-error sensitivity. Circles denote networks individually supported under both frozen regional nulls; squares denote networks with the same positive direction but without individual support. All four effects are positive; the South Shetland Adélie and chinstrap networks are individually supported.

## Figure 3. Numerical recovery does not necessarily reconstruct the regional breeding-site distribution

First-to-last percentage change in adjusted regional abundance against first-to-last percentage change in effective breeding-site number for all seven eligible MAPPPD species × region networks. Circles are declining networks and triangles are increasing networks. All three increasing networks end with lower effective breeding-site number than in their first retained complete season, despite higher abundance. This panel is descriptive context only: no regional ratchet or hysteresis test was frozen before the increasing-network outcomes were inspected.
