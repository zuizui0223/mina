## Section S1: Evidence provenance

The manuscript combines evidence with distinct inferential roles. Palmer Adélie concentration is the discovery analysis. Signy Adélie was frozen prospectively after the Palmer result and provides the independent geographic replication. Signy chinstrap was separately frozen and provides the cross-species replication. The MAPPPD regional analysis was then frozen before regional concentration outcomes and tests whether the concentration **direction** persists when monitored breeding sites within published regions replace within-system colony components.

The five-trajectory abundance–(E) scaling analysis and component-dominance trajectories were bounded post-hoc descriptions. They do not alter the primary concentration decisions.

## Section S3: Prospectively frozen Signy panels

The primary Signy years were 1996, 1998–2009 and 2011–2019, giving 22 complete seasons.

The Adélie panel used five canonical monitored units: A1+A60, A2, A3, A4 and A64. A1, A60 and pooled forms were harmonized before effect inspection because the source changed reporting convention. Stable-roster abundance declined from 2,342 to 1,217 breeding pairs, while (E) declined from 3.081 to 1.936 (−37.2%; slope −0.04396 yr⁻¹). The one-sided probability was 0.000010 under Poisson, CV10% and CV20% nulls.

The chinstrap panel used nine literal units: C15, C16, C17, C18, C46, C47, C79, C80 and C81. Stable-roster abundance declined from 1,642 to 581 pairs, while (E) declined from 4.438 to 2.191 (−50.6%; slope −0.07179 yr⁻¹). Under CV20, one of 100,000 simulations was at least as negative as observed (plus-one p = 0.000020).

All four declining networks had positive observation-error-calibrated (Delta\kappa). Two were individually supported under the frozen panel-level rule. No multiplicity-adjusted family-wide regional rejection criterion was prespecified, and the nominal 4/4 positive sign probability (0.0625) is descriptive because species × region panels within the same region are not independent geographic replicates.

The Central-west Antarctic Peninsula retained rosters include BISC (Biscoe Point), which belongs to the broader Palmer-area APBP context but is not one of the three Palmer discovery populations. MAPPPD is therefore interpreted as a scale-transfer analysis rather than an additional independent geographic replication.

## Section S5: Direction rather than a universal exponent

The five local Palmer/Signy trajectories had post-hoc log–log abundance–(E) elasticities of 0.069, 0.203, 0.465, 0.289 and 0.366. The descriptive fixed-effect estimate was (\kappa=0.250). Regional declining networks had raw κ values of 0.080, 0.088, 0.022 and 0.445. These values do not support a universal quarter-power exponent. The cross-scale claim is therefore ordinal: the concentration direction recurs more consistently than its magnitude.

## Section S6: Increasing regional networks

All three eligible increasing networks ended with lower (E) than in their first retained complete season:

| Network | Abundance change | E change |
|---|---:|---:|
| Adélie — Victoria Land | +33.1% | −26.6% |
| Gentoo — Central-west Antarctic Peninsula | +21.8% | −16.3% |
| Gentoo — South Shetland Islands | +64.1% | −1.1% |

These observations are descriptive only. No regional ratchet or hysteresis endpoint was frozen before the increasing-network outcomes were inspected.

## Section S7: Component-level route boundary

The initially dominant monitored component had zero breeding pairs by the final eligible census in all three Palmer discovery populations. At Signy, the initially dominant component remained dominant and increased its share in both species: from 47.5% to 69.4% in Adélie and from 40.0% to 65.6% in chinstrap. Thus the replicated decrease in effective component number does not require a universal “largest colony survives” mechanism.

## Section S8: Claim boundary

The analyses support cross-scale directional recurrence of abundance-conditioned breeding-space concentration within Antarctic *Pygoscelis*. They do not support a seabird-wide law, a universal κ, a universal refuge mechanism, formal regional hysteresis, a closed-population interpretation of APBP regions, or causal attribution to habitat, site fidelity, recruitment, movement or predation.

No alternate regional definitions, thresholds, lags, transformations, nonlinear models or trait screens were opened in response to the regional result.
