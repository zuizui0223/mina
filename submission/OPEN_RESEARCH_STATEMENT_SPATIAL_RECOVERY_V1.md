# Open Research statement — spatial recovery Ecology Article v1

**Status:** scientifically complete; permanent archive DOI pending.

## Public source data

The manuscript uses public long-term penguin monitoring data and published natural-history evidence.

Primary analyzed datasets include:

- **Ross Sea Adélie aerial census:** DOI `10.7931/kf06-x745`
- **Palmer Station LTER Adélie census:** DOI `10.6073/pasta/89dd52217ca37e3a72a67f7a9bc3c82e`
- **Signy Island Adélie monitoring:** DOI `10.5285/daf2c4fd-c1e3-4e65-851f-d11f02c5b69d`

The Beaufort capacity-release interpretation also uses published band/resighting evidence from LaRue et al. (2013), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0060568`, together with the public Ross Sea aerial census.
