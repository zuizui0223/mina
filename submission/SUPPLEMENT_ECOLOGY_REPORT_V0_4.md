# Supplementary figure caption — Ecology Report v0.4

## Figure S1. Post-hoc nominal census-unit trajectories

Descriptive first-to-last shares of the initially dominant and finally dominant monitored census units. On all three Palmer islands, the code that was initially dominant had zero breeding pairs by the final eligible census and another code became dominant. At Signy, the initially dominant unit remained dominant in both species. These summaries were specified after the primary concentration outcomes were known and carry no p-values or inferential weight. Palmer colony codes are official island-specific census identifiers, but the public archive does not provide a versioned spatial-boundary history for every code through 1991–2017; Figure S1 therefore describes **nominal census-unit trajectories**, not verified turnover among fixed physical polygons.
