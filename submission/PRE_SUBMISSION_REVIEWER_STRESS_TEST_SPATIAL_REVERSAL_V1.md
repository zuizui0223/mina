# Pre-submission reviewer stress test — PR189 spatial reversibility v1

**Purpose:** identify the strongest likely editorial/reviewer objections to the Ecology Report before formatting and submission. No new ecological effects are authorized from this document.

## 1. “Exact reversal is a straw-man; of course noisy ecological data will not return exactly”

**Risk:** **Very high** under the original v0.1 framing.

**Resolution:** The preferred v0.2 manuscript no longer treats failure of exact equality as the discovery. The positive result is the **degree of reversibility**:

- aggregate restoration = 96.97%;
- loss/rebound cosine = 0.99695;
- inverse-path mismatch = 9.41%.

Exact reversal is used only as a reference axis that quantifies spatial slippage.

**Required wording:** “largely retraced,” “highly reversible,” “moderate residual mismatch.”

**Avoid:** “recovery failed spatially,” “exact reversal rejected.”

**Status:** Addressed in submission v0.2.

## 2. “The 9.41% mismatch is not exceptional, so why is this publishable?”

**Risk:** **High.**

The two other complete Ross down→up triplets have mismatches of 9.12% and 10.63%.

**Response:** Do not sell effect-size extremeness. The focal episode is informative because it combines:

1. independently documented external disturbance;
2. >50% breeding-abundance trough;
3. 96.97% aggregate restoration in the immediate rebound;
4. a known pre-disturbance spatial vector;
5. published colony-specific access/demographic context.

The ecological result is that even a severe disturbance left **ordinary-scale compositional slippage rather than spatial disorganization**.

**Status:** Addressed.

## 3. “Maybe the more interesting result is that the network is spatially resilient, not that it mismatches”

**Risk:** **High if ignored; opportunity if embraced.**

Loss and rebound are almost collinear. Preferred framing now emphasizes that a severe perturbation was followed by rapid spatial reconstruction.

The 9.41% mismatch calibrates imperfection around that dominant signal.

**Status:** Addressed in v0.2 title/abstract.

## 4. “The focal 1999–2001–2002 decomposition was selected after seeing the failed Ross recovery result”

**Risk:** **Very high inferentially.**

**Response:** This cannot be converted into a confirmatory test.

The manuscript must state prominently:

- the frozen 2001–2012 deconcentration prediction failed;
- the 1999–2002 natural-experiment decomposition is a transparent post-result refinement;
- the focal effect size is descriptive;
- external sign tests were frozen prospectively and serve a different role.

**Status:** Addressed; must remain visible in Introduction/Methods.

## 5. “2001 could be a census-method artifact rather than a biological disturbance trough”

**Risk:** Moderate.

**Response:** 1999, 2001 and 2002 all use the same broad pre-2006 film/manual aerial workflow. Published Ross studies independently identify the iceberg-era breeding disturbance and severe access/sea-ice effects.

Counts represent occupied breeding territories, not total adults.

**Status:** Addressed.

## 6. “You have no uncertainty on the Ross 9.41% effect”

**Risk:** **High.**

**Response:** Correct. Do not imply statistical significance.

Main text:
- 9.41% = descriptive allocation effect size;
- source-specific component count SEs are unavailable.

Supplement:
- bounded perturbation thresholds of 22.7% with observed rebound scale fixed and 13.8% with scale floating;
- explicitly not estimates of count error.

**Status:** Addressed.

## 7. “The six units mix colonies and subcolonies”

**Risk:** Moderate.

Royds is a colony; Bird and Crozier are split into census components.

**Response:** Call them **breeding components**, not six independent colonies. Show three-colony summaries separately where relevant. E6 is a fixed monitoring-resolution endpoint, not a claim about six equivalent biological colonies.

**Status:** Addressed.

## 8. “The rebound could reflect breeding participation rather than population recovery”

**Risk:** **High if ‘population’ is used carelessly.**

**Response:** The response is breeding abundance/occupied territories. Annual changes can contain participation, abandonment, survival, recruitment and movement.

Use “breeding rebound,” not “adult population doubled.”

**Status:** Addressed in title and Methods.

## 9. “Bird Island is not a recovery experiment”

**Risk:** High.

**Response:** Correct. Bird is a prospectively frozen **positive-net-change sign test**, not a disturbance–recovery replication.

Do not call 1981→2024 a recovery phase.

**Status:** Addressed.

## 10. “Emperor results are only posterior medians, not uncertainty-propagated spatial inference”

**Risk:** Moderate to high.

**Response:** State explicitly:
- 50-colony endpoint uses posterior medians;
- public output lacks joint indexed colony draws for a posterior E distribution;
- regional quadrant result is descriptive point-estimate evidence.

Do not claim posterior probability of E decline.

**Status:** Addressed.

## 11. “All four quadrants are not evidence of statistical independence”

**Risk:** Moderate.

**Response:** Use “not sign-locked” or “not deterministically fixed.” Bird annual continuous changes are moderately correlated; emperor regional correlation is low-n descriptive.

**Status:** Addressed.

## 12. “The external systems are apples and oranges”

**Risk:** Moderate.

Bird = direct nest counts at one island; Emperor = model-based posterior median colony indices at continental scale.

**Response:** They are not pooled into a common effect size. Their only role is directional falsification of a universal sign rule. The paper does not estimate a cross-system mean.

**Status:** Addressed.

## 13. “The mathematical point that a sum does not determine a vector is trivial”

**Risk:** **Very high editorially.**

**Response:** Do not sell the algebra.

Sell the empirical question:

> how reversible was a real breeding network after a documented >50% disturbance?

The algebra only defines aggregate versus compositional coordinates.

External tests show why scalar abundance cannot substitute for measuring the spatial state.

**Status:** Addressed in v0.2; keep mathematics visually secondary.

## 14. “No general mechanism was identified”

**Risk:** Moderate editorially, low inferentially.

**Response:** Mechanism routes were prospectively attempted and closed:

- Bird ratio: measurement-sensitive;
- Port Lockroy: support fail;
- Signy ratio-free reproductive output: terminal fail;
- Ross subcolony perimeter/area: terminal fail.

This is a state/reversibility Report, not a mechanism paper.

**Status:** Addressed. Do not add another mechanism screen.

## 15. “If the focal mismatch is ordinary, external tests seem bolted on”

**Risk:** Moderate.

**Response:** Make roles explicit:

- Ross: **how reversible was one known severe disturbance?**
- Bird/Emperor: **can spatial direction be inferred from aggregate direction in general?**

They answer different, nested questions.

Do not present them as replications of the Ross effect size.

**Status:** Addressed in cover letter v0.2; verify Results transitions.

## 16. “Crozier West is singled out post hoc”

**Risk:** Moderate.

**Response:** It is reported because the exact inverse-path residual vector has only one positive component in the focal episode. Do not elevate this to a hypothesis test or unique mechanism.

Published access geometry may be discussed only as case-specific context.

**Status:** Addressed.

## 17. “Why Ecology Report rather than a note or case study?”

**Risk:** Editorial.

**Response:** The Report has:
- a documented natural experiment;
- an explicit quantitative reversibility metric;
- strong calibration of effect magnitude;
- two prospective external sign tests;
- transparent failed predictions/mechanism routes.

The contribution is compact by design and fits the Report format better than a full mechanism article.

**Status:** Reasonable.

## 18. “What would make the paper materially stronger?”

Only a genuinely prospective predictor of the contrast mode measured independently of current abundance and opened in an independent system.

Current opened datasets no longer permit a defensible upgrade.

**Stop rule:** do not add new covariate screens, lags, species, or metrics to PR189.

## Final editorial risk assessment

### Strongest asset
A severe, independently documented breeding disturbance followed by **96.97% aggregate restoration and 0.997 spatial-vector alignment** in the immediate rebound.

### Strongest caution
The residual 9.41% mismatch is descriptive, ordinary-scale within Ross, and lacks a source-specific sampling-error CI.

### Strongest generality
Prospectively frozen Bird/Emperor tests show that aggregate direction does not dictate spatial direction.

### Remaining publication risk
An editor may still judge the empirical contribution too modest because the broad abundance/composition distinction is established and the focal residual is not exceptional.

The best defense is **not** to amplify the mismatch. It is to emphasize the quantified spatial reversibility of a real severe disturbance and the audit-disciplined external boundary tests.
