## Broad ecological relevance

The general problem is a scale transition:

> **regional recovery is a weighted aggregate of local recovery.**

A dominant local component can restore enough abundance to make the total look recovered while:
- smaller local units remain below prior losses;
- dominance increases;
- effective spatial redundancy declines;
- endpoint composition remains different from baseline.

## Why the focal result is not a metric artifact

Three independent descriptions agree:

1. local restoration ratios vary from 10.8% to 109.8%;
2. endpoint composition remains far from exact-inverse expectation;
3. effective-number summaries cross past baseline toward greater dominance.

The exact inverse mismatch is algebraically determined by heterogeneity in local restoration ratios.
