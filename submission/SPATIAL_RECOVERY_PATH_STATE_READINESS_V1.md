## Exact local-recovery decomposition

Canonical interpretation note:

    docs/ROSS_LOCAL_RECOVERY_RATIO_DECOMPOSITION_V1.md

Aggregate restoration is exactly the loss-weighted mean local restoration ratio. In the focal Ross episode, local restoration ranged from 10.8% to 109.8%, with a median of 51.8%. Cape Crozier West supplied 80.6% of rebound; excluding it, the other five components restored 65.3% of combined loss.

This makes the focal result independent of reliance on cosine similarity.

## Central result

Ross 1999→2001→2002 gives three different recovery answers:

- aggregate loss restored: **96.97%**;
- inverse-path fidelity: **90.59%**;
- baseline compositional displacement erased: **2.4%**.

Therefore:

> **recovery occurred largely in the same breeding components that had lost abundance, but the previous spatial composition was not restored.**

The 2001→2002 compositional shift (TV = 9.75%) was the largest of 25 complete adjacent Ross transitions.

## Exact inverse counterfactual

Given the observed 96.97% aggregate rebound, exact proportional inverse reversal would have produced:

- endpoint TV from 1999 = **0.0717%**;
- E6 = **2.0589**, essentially equal to the 1999 E6 = **2.0558**.

Observed 2002:

- endpoint TV from 1999 = **4.967%**;
- E6 = **1.8190**.

Thus the structured 9.41% rebound-allocation mismatch is state-consequential.

## Within-Ross calibration

All three complete six-component down→up episodes have:

- inverse-path fidelity: **89–91%**;
- state restoration: **+2.4%, −29.2%, −53.4%**.

This is descriptive within-system calibration, not independent replication.

## Main claim boundary

- individual-level movement or identity inference;
