# Ecology Report figure captions — cross-scale v1

## Figure 1

**Declining penguin populations lose effective monitored breeding components.** Annual breeding-pair abundance and effective breeding-component number (E; defined in the Methods) for the five within-system population trajectories, each indexed to 100 in its first eligible season. Palmer panels are Cormorant, Humble and Litchfield Adélie penguins; Signy panels are the prospectively frozen Adélie and chinstrap replications. The figure is descriptive; inferential support comes from the fixed-composition count-error nulls reported in the text.

## Figure 2

**Breeding-space concentration recurs when the spatial component is moved one level upward.** **A.** First-to-last change in effective monitored breeding-component number for the five Palmer and Signy within-system trajectories. Labels report one-sided probabilities under the severe frozen 20%-CV count-error sensitivity. **B.** Observation-error-calibrated regional concentration effect Δκ (defined in the Methods), for the four structurally estimable declining species × APBP-region networks. Positive values indicate stronger abundance-linked concentration than expected under the panel-specific fixed-composition null. Circles denote networks individually supported under both frozen regional nulls; squares denote networks with the same positive direction but without individual support. Local and regional statistics are scale-specific and are not pooled as a common effect size.
