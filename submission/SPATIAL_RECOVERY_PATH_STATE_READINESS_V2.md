## Focal result

Ross Island Adélie penguins, 1999→2001→2002:

- breeding-abundance decline: **54.3%**;
- aggregate loss restored: **96.97%**;
- local restoration range: **10.8–109.8%**;
- median local restoration: **51.8%**;
- Cape Crozier West share of rebound: **80.6%**.

Path reversal remained high at both natural grains:

- six components: **90.59%** inverse-path fidelity;
- three colonies: **93.27%**.

State restoration was incomplete and grain dependent:

- six components: **2.4%** of baseline→trough TV displacement erased;
- three colonies: **28.3%** erased.

Exact proportional reversal would have placed the endpoint ~**0.07% TV** from baseline at either grain. Observed endpoint distances were **4.97%** and **3.54%**.

## Licensed interpretation

> **Near-complete aggregate recovery was produced by strongly unequal local recovery: abundance returned largely where it had been lost, while spatial structure remained altered at both biologically natural grains.**

Short form:

> **The population recovered where it had declined, but not in the same proportions.**
