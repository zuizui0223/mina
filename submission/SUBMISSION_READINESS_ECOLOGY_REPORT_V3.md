## General ecological principle

Positive abundance–occupancy relationships already establish that declining populations often lose occupied sites. This manuscript tests a stricter conditional statement.

For a spatially structured population:
- N(t) describes total abundance;
- p(t) describes relative spatial composition.

The proportional-thinning null sets the latent N(t) trajectory from the observed annual totals while holding p(t) constant; simulated counts then vary under the frozen observation-error model. Rejection therefore identifies directional spatial redistribution beyond the contraction generated by lower abundance plus that observation error.

**Editorial shorthand:** abundance decline constrains, but does not determine, the spatial trajectory of a structured population.
