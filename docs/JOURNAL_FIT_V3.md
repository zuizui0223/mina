# Journal fit v3 — after serial-structure-preserving N_eff diagnostics

Checked: 27 September 2026.

## Decision

**First shot remains Ecosphere — Article, Animal Ecology track.**

**Fallback remains Ecology and Evolution.**

The circular-shift diagnostics materially strengthen the only positive local
association but do not change its epistemic category.

## What strengthened

Effective colony number has a standardized conditional coefficient of +0.1168.

That coefficient remains extreme under:

- 100,000 independent island-wise circular shifts preserving each island's
  cyclic temporal structure (p < 1e-5);
- an exact 416-member shift group preserving cross-island N_eff covariance among
  the four persistent islands (p = 0.00240);
- fixed circular-topology count-error coupling nulls, with the largest coupled
  p-value = 0.00780.

Thus the association is not plausibly dismissed as an artifact of the original
year-block permutation destroying serial structure, nor is it readily generated
by the fixed stylized shared-count-error models.

## What did not strengthen

The held-out predictive result remains unsupported:

- MSE gain = +0.00102884;
- synchronized year-block permutation p = 0.26224.

Therefore the manuscript must not call N_eff:

- a robust predictor;
- an early-warning indicator;
- a demonstrated resilience mechanism.

## Why Ecosphere remains appropriate

The paper's contribution is still multiscale ecological synthesis rather than a
single validated mechanism:

1. common regional decline;
2. heterogeneous local ecological endpoints;
3. bounded negative environmental mechanism tests;
4. one conditional within-patch association that survives structured surrogate
   nulls but does not transfer robustly to held-out years;
5. external spatial triangulation without an identifier crosswalk.

That evidence structure fits a broad quantitative island/animal-ecology paper
better than a higher-risk JAE framing built around predictive novelty.

## What would genuinely change the ceiling

A one-to-one LTER `colony_code` ↔ GIS polygon crosswalk for Torgersen, or an
independent replication in another monitored archipelago, would test whether the
N_eff association corresponds to physical breeding-habitat contraction. That is
the next evidence capable of changing the paper's inferential level.
