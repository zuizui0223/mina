**Date:** 2026-10-08. **Route:** PR195 exploratory source-backed physical-context extension. Not a PR189 submission amendment, hypothesis test, or confirmed successful breeding analysis.

### What the 2014 seasonal comparison actually shows

During the **11 nominal 15-day windows May→last preimage** around the LaRue / Fretwell historic published centroids, **3** windows contain class4 in both 3km neighborhoods, **2** only around Fretwell, **0** only around LaRue, and **6** neither. This is a **nested physical-support relationship**, not complementary temporal buffering: the secondary published centroid *always* has any class4 when the primary does, and on two additional periods. The maximum observed contiguous class4 run in either center during that comparison is 2 time bins; 2010 (source `yes`) has 4 consecutive ice-class bins at both points. This descriptive difference **does not identify breeding success** or a causal continuity threshold; indeed a strict condition requiring every May→image bin to show mapped class4 would mark even raw bird-present years as nonqualifying.

2014 September 13–27 and September 28–October 12 both show **0 class4/28** near LaRue versus **30/30** near Fretwell. Thus the qualitative discrepancy is not confined to only the one immediately preceding 15-day bin. The earlier bin is NOT necessarily a real-time prospective measurement because Fraser/Massom retrospective manual edge validation can consult the immediately following period when cloud cover prevents direct classification.

### Critical negative control

The original 2013-11-30 `bpresent=yes` observation has 0 class4 in both 3-km neighborhoods in the last completed preceding composite and no class4 in either site in the last four intervals; this is a **stage/footprint/measurement counterexample to treating 'bird yes' and 'fixed-centroid class4 present on matching nominal ice bin' as identical labels**. Raw bird detection alone does not verify completed breeding in that physical patch. October and November observations are not biologically exchangeable.

## Novelty veto from existing Antarctic literature

**Already known, NOT new:**
- Emperor penguin reliance on stable ice through the breeding year and breeding failure after early breakout (e.g. Fretwell et al. 2023, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43247-023-00927-x).
- Antarctic-wide fast-ice persistence, volatility, annual phase, extent and colony habitat differences at roughly 1-km / 15-day resolution, including 3-km colony-location neighborhoods (2023 *Science Advances* study **'Where to live? Landfast sea ice shapes emperor penguin habitat around Antarctica'**, https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adg8340). That paper also reports **no significant environmental-habitat difference between occupied and absent sites**, limiting novelty of simply finding ice at an unoccupied published coordinate.
- Moving emperor colonies within season and access to multiple physical platforms have already been observed with winter SAR and optical imagery (Macdonald et al. 2026, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43247-026-03906-0).
- Ledda Bay was previously described as re-forming and undergoing frequent early fast-ice breakup by Fretwell et al. 2021, https://doi.org/10.1002/rse2.176.

**Potentially incremental empirical contribution from this analysis:** Cross-publication representations of *one named colony* can be far enough apart to reverse independent annual local habitat classification even at the same 1/3/5-km-compatible spatial scale. This is an audit of **measurement support and colony site identity**, not a new Antarctic causation law.

**Open causal question:** Does changing physical opportunity, independently dated and measured at true colony-year nesting footprints, change first breeding settlement conditional on past occupancy and immigration pressure? No suitable georeferenced annual nest polygons with repeated confirmed viable-vacant years and independent first-breeding source/offspring success have been demonstrated here. Without this, 'social memory', Allee effect, source-rescue and time-integrated effective island capacity are **hypotheses only**.

## Conservative decision

- Preserve frozen scientific [Ecology manuscript PR189](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina/pull/189), unchanged.
- Keep PR195 as exploratory source audit with two published centroids, not a positive ecological-mechanism paper.
- Do not tune ice-duration thresholds to classify the six already-read bird codes, do not calculate naive p-values from six year-clustered points, and do not interpret source 2013 guano/area/yes as year-round successful breeding.
- The source evidence currently favors **spatial-georeference and temporal-observation ambiguity** over any new demonstrated social behavioral process. It does **not** prove that ice instability is the cause of absence.
- Further same-site literature mining will be valuable only with an independently dated actual breeding footprint or reproductive success record; otherwise stop rather than extrapolating causation.

Status: **EXPLORATORY PHYSICAL CLASS SUCCESS / CAUSAL MECHANISM HOLD**.
