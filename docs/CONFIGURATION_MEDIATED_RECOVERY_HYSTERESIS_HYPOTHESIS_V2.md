# Configuration-mediated recovery hysteresis v2 — residual-memory hypothesis

**Status:** post-result generated secondary mechanism. Supersedes V1.

## Revision

The immediate Ross shock/rebound has a strong exogenous explanation in colony-specific iceberg and sea-ice access geometry.

Therefore configuration-mediated hysteresis is no longer used as the primary explanation for the 2001 -> 2002 re-concentration.

Its proper role is narrower:

> **after contemporaneous access and environmental conditions are accounted for, does prior spatial fragmentation/configuration explain residual differences in local recovery?**

## Prior evidence

Already established:

- McDowall & Lynch: Adélie subcolony spatial configuration can respond hysteretically to abundance because nest-site fidelity and edge-biased predation impede reorganization.
- Schmidt et al.: Royds has higher edge exposure and lower/more variable reproductive success than Crozier; slow Royds recovery is consistent with a fragmented, suboptimal configuration.
- Dugger et al.: iceberg years alter breeding dispersal, and Royds differs from Bird/Crozier in apparent survival and movement.

None of these are new PR189 results.

## Generated residual-memory model

For node i:

    observed restoration_i
      = f(current environment_i,
          access cost_i,
          current abundance_i,
          usable capacity_i,
          movement_i,
          prior configuration_i).

The configuration-memory hypothesis is specifically:

    prior configuration_i

adds predictive information **after** the other current-state variables are controlled.

## Prospective prediction

At matched:

- current breeding abundance;
- current ocean-access conditions;
- local breeding capacity;
- major regional forcing;

nodes with lower prior fragmentation, lower perimeter-to-area ratio, and lower edge exposure should recover breeding participation and reproductive success more strongly.

## Falsification

The configuration-memory scale-up is weakened if prior configuration adds no recovery information after current access/environment and current abundance are controlled.

## Relation to Ross

Ross currently supplies:

- a branch-dependent spatial state;
- known access differences;
- known demographic differences;
- prior evidence that Royds configuration may be suboptimal.

It does not separate these explanations.

Therefore Ross motivates the hypothesis but does not confirm it.
