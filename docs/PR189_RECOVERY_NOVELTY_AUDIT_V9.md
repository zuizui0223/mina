# PR189 novelty audit v9 — final ceiling after mechanism closure

**Status:** final novelty ceiling after the independent Signy reproductive-output and Ross subcolony-configuration mechanism routes both failed prospectively.

## What the new calibration changes

The focal Ross natural experiment has:

    aggregate shock loss restored = 96.97%
    inverse-path allocation mismatch = 9.41%
    loss/rebound cosine = 0.99695.

However, the 9.41% mismatch is not exceptional within the same Ross series.

Other complete six-component down→up episodes have:

    1989→1990→1991  mismatch 9.12%
    2002→2003→2004  mismatch 10.63%.

Therefore novelty cannot rest on an unusually large spatial mismatch.

The focal episode is valuable because:

1. the disturbance is independently identified;
2. aggregate restoration is nearly complete;
3. the pre-disturbance reference vector is known;
4. the spatial mismatch can be interpreted against published colony-specific access geometry.

## Strongest Ross statement

> **A documented shared disturbance was followed by near-complete numerical restoration and a moderate, structured failure of exact spatial reversal.**

Do not write:

> an exceptional spatial reorganization.

The rebound largely retraces the loss.

Approximately one tenth of rebound allocation differs from exact proportional reversal.

## Broad prior art

Already established:

- sums do not determine compositions;
- abundance recovery can differ from community-composition recovery;
- metapopulation aggregate recovery can mask local nonrecovery;
- spatial synchrony can break under local heterogeneity;
- common and contrast modes are standard mathematical ideas;
- path dependence and hysteresis are established ecological concepts.

Therefore the state/allocation paper is an empirical refinement, not a new universal framework.

## Claim ceiling

Safe:

- near-complete aggregate rebound does not require exact spatial reversal;
- Ross mismatch is moderate, not exceptional;
- aggregate and spatial directions are not sign-locked in prospective external tests.

Not safe:

- Ross effect size is unusually large;
- a general penguin recovery law;
- a confirmed reproductive-success mechanism;
- a confirmed configuration-memory mechanism;
- statistical independence of aggregate and spatial modes.
