## Why the macro test remains blocked

- Previous MAPPPD-only source had 32 nominal colonies but **6 conservative 200-km geographic blocks** and only **14 constant-vantage/accuracy-code<=2 sites across 4 blocks** when survey quality was standardized.
- Signy adds **1 source-verified independent block** and 13 observed breeding-pair winters with real coastal-polynya variation: **7 geographic blocks nominally, high-quality subset 4+1=5**. Signy North Point is an observational index for five monitored subcolonies, not a census of all island colonies. BAS's pairs and original MAPPPD nests require measurement comparability crosswalk before merging.
- The preregistered feasibility gate was >=8 physically independent, method-comparable blocks; seven fails it even if the Signy local physical signal is strong. Mac. Robertson Land source would at most add another independent geographical block **after** its annual nest counts, methods, actual years, and actual DEEP-AA/NSIDC support are verified. Do not claim it has already done so.
- No p-value for the climatic or demographic response, no selection of convenient alternative 100 km radius/years/lag, and no causal claim from this source-only step.
