## Scientific claim lock

Do not strengthen the manuscript during formatting. In particular:

- do not say sea ice is unimportant;
- do not call effective colony number a causal protective mechanism;
- do not say large breeding groups are harmful;
- do not claim a colony-code-to-GIS polygon validation;
- do not say the modest colony-network increment explains the regional decline;
- do not reopen climate windows, weather months, colony thresholds or topology metrics.
