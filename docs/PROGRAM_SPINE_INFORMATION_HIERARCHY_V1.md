# Program spine v1 — where is the ecological information?

**Frozen evidence basis:** `docs/EVIDENCE_LEDGER_66_ENDPOINTS_V1.md`, built from all 66 JSON result files on `main@7764523c5f1e536b86084de8583cdd6262ee8fa7`.

## One-sentence story

> **Decline direction is shared regionally, but the reproducible spatial information lies in breeding configuration: the tested static island traits, simple environmental mechanisms and apparent island phenotypes do not transfer as general rules, whereas breeding effort repeatedly concentrates into fewer effective monitored components beyond proportional thinning.**

This wording is intentionally weaker than “the region determines the direction” and “configuration predicts population fate.” The five Palmer islands share a long-term decline, but the tested annual and smoothed sea-ice and snowfall models do not identify its cause. Likewise, effective component number has a robust conditional association with growth, but the held-out predictive gain fails the synchronized year-block permutation test.

## The information hierarchy

### 1. Regional scale — shared direction, unresolved cause

The strongest regional observation is the common Palmer decline: the five island abundance trajectories have a dominant shared long-term component (PC1 ≈ 96.4%). This is ecological context rather than a causal mechanism.

Three finite environmental routes were tested and did not explain the pattern:

- preceding-year sea-ice duration;
- 3/5/7-year smoothed sea-ice duration;
- October snowfall × published snow-prone habitat.

Therefore the defensible conclusion is:

> **The direction of decline is regionally coherent, but the common driver is not identified by the simple environmental formulations tested here.**

Do not write that the regional driver “must act at long timescales”; the explicit low-frequency sea-ice rescue also failed.

### 2. Static place — extensive testing, no confirmatory transferable rule

This layer generated the largest number of result files because it required extensive outcome-blind support, observation, forcing-scale and recovery gates before real inference.

The evidence ledger contains **28 static-place files**, but their strength distribution is:

- A confirmatory: 0
- B bounded/robust: 2
- C scope-limiting negative: 5
- D data/identifiability gate: 16
- E technical audit: 5

The real-data A×H point estimates were directionally negative in all three Pygoscelis species, but the frozen permutation endpoint did not reject. A-only buffering also failed, scale/trait sensitivity was not invariant, and the radius sign-switch diagnostic was null-compatible.

Thus:

> **Static habitat amount, habitat diversity and their interaction do not provide a confirmed transferable rule for demographic response in the tested Antarctic data.**

The detectable-effect audit narrows rather than proves absence: effects around |0.5| should have been detectable, whereas effects around |0.3| need not have been.

### 3. Species / phenotype — apparent island identity is largely compositional

The ODSP provenance and Palmer phenotype analyses point in the same direction.

Across the full Palmer penguin assemblage, island-associated morphology is strongly explained by species composition. Once restricted to Adélie penguins:

- fixed island partial effects are small;
- pairwise island contrasts frequently reverse by year;
- cross-year island classification from structural morphology and isotopes is approximately chance.

Thus:

> **A visible “island phenotype” is not a stable transferable Adélie island identity over the observed three-year window.**

This is bounded exploratory evidence, not proof against local adaptation over evolutionary timescales.

### 4. Within-system breeding configuration — the only layer reaching positive prospective replication

This is where the evidence hierarchy changes.

**Discovery:** Palmer stable-roster Adélie populations show effective-component contraction beyond a fixed-composition thinning null under the complete frozen count-error family.

**Independent geographic replication:** Signy Adélie was frozen after the Palmer discovery and independently supports the same concentration endpoint.

**Cross-species replication:** a separately frozen Signy chinstrap panel also supports concentration.

These are the only two **class-A positive replication units** in the 66-file ledger.

The strict Signy Adélie roster/window result is a robustness analysis within the same replication family and is not counted as a third vote.

The endpoint is also mechanistically non-unique:

- Palmer: the initially dominant monitored component disappears and dominance turns over;
- Signy: the initially dominant monitored component persists and strengthens.

The bounded scaling search further shows that the transferable feature is not a universal exponent or threshold:

- all five local trajectories have positive raw abundance–E coupling;
- common descriptive κ is near 0.25;
- population-specific κ varies widely;
- 50/25/10% abundance hinges do not improve held-out prediction;
- the frozen ratchet criterion fails.

Thus the core result is directional:

> **Decline repeatedly reorganizes breeding space toward fewer effective components beyond proportional thinning, but the rate and internal route of that reorganization are contingent.**

### 5. Mechanistic traces — informative, but none reaches the same evidential tier

#### Effective component number and subsequent growth

The original leave-one-year-out model found a small positive gain from N_eff. That predictive interpretation does **not** survive the later synchronized year-block permutation.

What does survive is narrower:

- the positive coefficient is unusual under circular-shift nulls;
- it survives same-census mechanical-coupling simulations;
- it remains after lagged-growth controls.

Therefore:

> **Breeding configuration carries a robust conditional demographic association, but not a demonstrated forecasting signal.**

#### Reproductive performance and redistribution

A fixed Palmer lag-2 specification supports a performance→redistribution association after denominator separation, but the provenance repair occurred after coefficients had already been exposed. It is therefore nonconfirmatory.

The prospectively frozen Signy replication fails.

Thus:

> **Performance-linked redistribution is compatible with the Palmer data but is not transferable enough to identify a general mechanism.**

#### Reproductive collapse / Allee-like mechanism

The denominator audit rejects a Palmer-wide reproductive law and finds no pre-extinction breeding collapse.

#### Win-stay / lose-switch

The broad redistribution pattern is compatible with within-island switching, but the specific asymmetric win-stay/lose-switch prediction is not supported.

### 6. Individual process — the remaining information gap

The public mark-resight audit did not locate a qualifying Palmer table containing colony-scale repeated individual locations. Therefore the existing program cannot directly distinguish:

- adult breeding dispersal;
- prospecting;
- immigration;
- temporary nonbreeding;
- mortality;
- public-information use.

This is a data limit, not a negative biological result.

## Manuscript architecture implied by the ledger

### Main claim

Use only:

1. Palmer concentration as discovery;
2. Signy Adélie as prospective independent-system replication;
3. Signy chinstrap as prospective cross-species replication.

### Context that motivates the claim

Use:

- regional common decline;
- phenotype compositionality / temporal reassembly;
- static-place macro nulls.

These show why the successful configuration endpoint is biologically informative, but they do not become additional confirmation of concentration.

### Mechanistic boundary

Use:

- robust conditional N_eff association but failed predictive permutation;
- Palmer-only lag-2 performance association plus failed Signy replication;
- failed reproductive-collapse and win-stay/lose-switch routes.

The message is that the **state variable is reproducible before the generating process is identified**.

### Data-limit ending

End the evidence hierarchy with the mark-resight gate:

> The repeated spatial endpoint is visible, but the individual decisions that generate it are not observed in the available public data.

That creates the natural next question without pretending it was already answered.

## Selection-bias safeguard

The paper should not narrate all 66 files sequentially. Instead:

- put a compact evidence-class × ecological-layer table in the main text or a central schematic;
- place the full 66-row ledger in Supporting Information;
- state explicitly that only class A supplies independent positive confirmation;
- make class-C negatives visible as claim boundaries;
- report class-D gates as unresolved questions rather than failed biological hypotheses.

This converts the analysis history from a liability into the logic of the paper: **the program searched multiple information layers under increasingly explicit gates, and prospective replication survived only for breeding-space concentration.**
