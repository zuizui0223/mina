# Journal of Biogeography cover letter — integrated v0.5

Dear Senior Editors,

Please consider our Research Article, **“Dynamic colony history and limits of static island architecture for Antarctic penguin demographic coupling.”** We ask a biogeographical transferability question across nested scales: when local breeding populations contain measurable demographic history, do static island descriptors capture how population time series respond to shared forcing across species and Antarctica? Palmer yields a fixed-specification association consistent with short-lived information in a late-season colony state, while an independent nest-level endpoint is non-confirmatory and a predeclared breeder-arrival test stops at its information gate. The Antarctic-wide architecture test is also non-confirmatory. By distinguishing colony distribution, long-term trend and temporal coupling, the manuscript defines the evidentiary limits on which forms of island information transfer across scale.
