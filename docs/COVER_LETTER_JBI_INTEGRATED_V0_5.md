# Journal of Biogeography cover letter — integrated v0.5

Dear Senior Editors,

Please consider our Research Article, **“Dynamic colony history and limits of static island architecture for Antarctic penguin demographic coupling.”** We ask a biogeographical transferability question across nested scales: when local breeding populations contain measurable demographic history, do static island descriptors capture how population time series respond to shared forcing across species and Antarctica? Palmer data reveal short-lived history in a late-season colony state, whereas the preregistered Antarctic-wide architecture test is non-confirmatory. By distinguishing colony distribution, long-term trend and temporal coupling, the manuscript defines which forms of island information do—and do not—transfer across scale.
