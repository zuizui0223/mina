# Reviewer audit of manuscript v0.1

## Major issue 1 — title strength versus effect size

The original title said that colony-network structure "predicts local resilience." The underlying held-out gain is real but small: MSE declines from 0.07311 to 0.07208, about a 1.4% relative reduction. Version 0.2 therefore uses **"indexes local vulnerability"** and states the relative error reduction in the abstract and Discussion.

## Major issue 2 — island-ecology generality

Version 0.1 explained why penguins are marine central-place foragers but did not connect the study strongly enough to the established seabird-island literature. Version 0.2 explicitly frames seabird islands as land-sea coupled systems and distinguishes the conventional question—how seabirds transform recipient islands—from mina's reciprocal demographic question: how terrestrial breeding-patch state conditions a population whose main trophic resources are external.

## Major issue 3 — negative mechanism tests

The annual sea-ice, 3/5/7-year duration and snowfall-by-habitat tests are useful falsifications, but wording must not imply that sea ice, prey or snow are unimportant. Version 0.2 consistently limits inference to the frozen linear predictive formulations.

## Major issue 4 — external spatial evidence

The Torgersen GIS reconstruction is genuinely independent evidence that breeding-patch attrition is spatially structured. It is **not** an identifier-level validation of the LTER effective-colony metric because the colony-code/polygon crosswalk remains unresolved. Version 0.2 changes "strengthens" language to bounded phenomenon-level triangulation.

## Major issue 5 — novelty statement

The strongest general contribution is scale separation in an externally subsidized island system:

1. a near-common regional demographic direction;
2. only moderate annual synchrony;
3. heterogeneous local extinction/replacement endpoints;
4. a small but specific within-island breeding-organization signal.

This is more defensible than positioning the paper around a new climate correlate or a causal fragmentation mechanism.

## Recommendation

Proceed with the ecological story as a multi-scale island-demography paper. Do not reopen climate windows, precipitation months, colony-size thresholds or topology-index searches. The next scientific advance beyond v0.2 should be a true colony-code-to-GIS crosswalk or replication in another archipelago.
