### What V46 really tests

Under an independent *constant underlying source-site composition* hypothesis, V46 estimates each site's null p from the pooled original counts `(n0_i+n1_i)/(N0+N1)`, generates 1,999 independent simulated old and new multinomial counts with exactly the original N0,N1 and source child roster, recomputes leader and concentration, and recalculates the size S from each **simulated** earlier size—not a fixed observed first-epoch score. Tied simulated leader rows are excluded from simulated event denominators (5/50/95% eligible old-source cohorts **755/768/780** versus 846 originally eligible), not treated as "no switch". The test is a **post-outcome** model-sensitivity analysis; the null probability `p` is fitted from the two observed snapshots.

**Under that particular counting model**, original leader replacement and initial large-site failure remain far larger than the measurement artifact generated by both census epochs. This is not a likelihood of actual dispersal, fidelity loss, or actual site boundary switching and is not an estimate of field sampling/observer error.

## What can be said about island and Antarctic ecology

The repeated *form* of breeding component reconfiguration across source species is a useful nontrivial source-level macroecology observation. It is **not yet a causal island biogeography result**. Original `Master site` is often a single mainland coast or single physical island, the same `Site.ID` can involve historical boundary/effort changes, and no within-parent verified alternative island refuges were available. Only source-relative counts at two epochs were observed, not individual movement or demographic replacement.

Frozen original Antarctic Penguin Paper 1 independently found effective nesting-component contractions (Palmer & Signy Adélie/chinstrap). Phenomenological correspondence may be discussed carefully, but **no common causal mechanism or cross-system predator-free contrast is established** (Antarctica also has avian predators and other marine/terrestrial controls).

**Recommended manuscript claim ceiling:** "Declining colonial seabird monitoring assemblages exhibit non-proportional shifts and frequent changes of their dominant original components beyond two-snapshot proportional-count nulls" (UK 17-species original-data study). Interpret why this happens as unresolved; no mammalian predation, Allee effect, competition, island access or physical recolonization claim unless independent measured source supports the mechanism. Keep frozen short Penguin Ecology Report a separate valid submission.
