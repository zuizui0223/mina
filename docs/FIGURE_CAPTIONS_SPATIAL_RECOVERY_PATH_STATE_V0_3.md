# Spatial recovery path-versus-state manuscript — main figure captions v0.3

## Figure 1. One disturbance-rebound episode gives different answers to whether the population recovered

Ross Island Adélie penguins, 1999→2001→2002. Aggregate loss restored was 96.97%. Inverse-path fidelity remained high at six-component (90.59%) and three-colony (93.27%) grains. State restoration—the fraction of baseline→trough total-variation displacement erased by the endpoint—was 2.4% and 28.3%, respectively. Thus the path-versus-state discrepancy survives coarsening, while the magnitude of state restoration is spatial-grain dependent.

## Figure 2. Near-complete aggregate recovery masked strongly unequal local restoration

(A) Fraction of each component's 1999→2001 loss restored in 2001→2002. Local restoration ranged from 10.8% to 109.8%; the dashed line is aggregate restoration (96.97%), the loss-weighted mean, whereas median local restoration was 51.8%. Cape Crozier West supplied 80.6% of rebound. (B) Relative breeding composition in 1999, 2001, and 2002. Differential recovery increased Crozier West dominance; total-variation distances were 5.09% (1999→2001), 4.97% (1999→2002), and 9.75% (2001→2002).
