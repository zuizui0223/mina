- V2 original-frame/absence-labelled precision check: the bird source programmes remain distinct. Earlier-map density and lean source-controls preserve positive direction, but **31 site-level within-250km×class×frame shuffles** challenge the local signal. Adélie observed improvement is **smaller than 30/31 shuffles**, flying seabird improvement exceeds **31/31 shuffled arrangements**. This is a post-result local-information diagnostic, *not* a causal contrast of flight capability. `results/AADC_SITE_OPTION_V2_FALSIFICATION.json`; [CI](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina/actions/runs/40209742219).
- V4 original AEI **matched-sample** spatial models: 338 Adélie sites (11 blocks), 305 flying sites (8 blocks). Adding independently mapped 2km non-bird labelled AEI icefree area/richness **does not improve** macro held-out Brier beyond source geography/site area and/or candidate site density. The earlier flying candidate-density source effect attenuates to Brier gain +0.00731 in only 5/8 blocks, below fixed 6/8 criterion. `results/AADC_AEI_VS_SITE_OPTION_V4_EXPLORATORY.json`; [CI](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina/actions/runs/40211049151).
- V5 **diagnosis of the missingness mechanism**: V3 original sites fall into 6,863 nonanimal-habitat-mapped, 252 only bird-linked AEI classes, 670 no mapped raster cells. Among 10,723 original direct P/A survey rows, mapped **nonanimal habitat is available** for Adélie **80.2% P vs 63.9% A**, and flying seabirds **89.5% P vs 97.6% A**. At the independent site-ID level, Adélie ever-P sites are covered 74.9% vs only-A 22.4%; flyers ever-P 71.9% vs only-A 99.4%. These asymmetric denominators demonstrate strong **response-associated sample selection** in the eligible habitat comparison (not causal effect of animals on map coverage). `results/AADC_AEI_V5_SOURCE_MISSINGNESS.json`; [CI](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina/actions/runs/40211474380).

## Scientific decision

**The independent Antarctic island-geometry/habitat atlas is a real original-data product. The evidence that its topology or AEI habitat amount drives penguin or flying-seabird site occupancy is NOT established.** Source-selected positive candidate-point density prediction is of ecological interest but not a finding about physical island boundaries, ice-free reproductive capacity or direct flight/dispersal traits. The matched AEI score is weakened by response-associated source missingness.

**STOP rerunning the same AADC P/A outcomes with adjusted environmental buffers, site matches, likelihoods or guild selections.** Reframe as an independent data-base-building programme, not a collection of favorable reanalyses. No attempt to 'rescue' Paper1/PR189, Paper2/V14, strict V41 or V4. No Nature-level causality claim.

## Next independently falsifiable source need

The next biological study needs original, externally verified breeding observation sites (or imagery) **with positional accuracy, repeated breeding seasons, explicit survey effort and confirmed A/P/NR or stage-matched nest counts**, plus season-appropriate breeding substrate measured independently and a versioned island/ice-gap topology. Original site features, physical land polygons and potential-site reference points must stay separate. The test of land continuity versus breeding-habitat continuity must use the same actual species, years and survey programme or explicitly adjust source effects; a penguin–flying-seabird interaction is a *future hypothesis*, not yet a conclusion.

Candidate actual source domains: independent original NZ Ross Sea aerial survey; BAS original bird datasets; NOAA US-AML R and AADC field programmes; MAPPPD only after original citation/vantage genealogy deduplication; satellite imagery nests or stable fast ice for emperor as a **separate outcome domain**. Do not invent independent replication from overlapping NZ and MAPPPD records (316 source-census overlaps already verified in PR197).

Proceed with **source-first coverage/precision gates** before animal effect estimates, and permit a clean STOP if original observation support is insufficient. The prior strong paper contribution (PR189) remains independently frozen and not submitted without author signoff.


## ADDENDUM 2026-10-26: original 122k nests, external terrain and historical multi-guild source gate

**Independent original spatial animal records acquired:** BAS/Hart 2019–20 four sub-Antarctic localities, 10 source species-labelled CSVs, 122,170 literal original nest-number records, 31 unlocated, and **122,139 located nests** over at most **three named candidate islands** (Saunders, Bellingshausen, Thule). V21 original 10m nearest-nest heterospecific fractions were **0–0.167%** against ~44–50% under indiscriminate within-locality source species label exchange. V22 revealed strong nesting spatial patch composition; within-100m source grid controls gave smaller residual conspecific enrichment than V21, and magnitudes were grid-phase-sensitive. This is a genuine first-party individual-nest original-data result, not a demonstrated competition/social mechanism.

**Independent environment actually acquired:** official Copernicus DEM GLO-30 public original 2021 georeferenced elevation and 3×3 terrain slope for **all 122,139** nest point positions. New fixed original source-island-held-out Adélie/Chinstrap two-variable classifier V27: class-balanced Brier 0.25 baseline vs 0.24585 Saunders, 0.25208 Bellingshausen, **0.37372 Thule**. **Only 1/3 candidate islands improved**, equal-island Brier gain **−0.04055**. Strong source-specific terrain sorting does NOT transfer across islands. This rejects a simple universally portable elevation/slope predictor in these original data; it does NOT prove island-contingent causal habitat choice, species competition or actual response of unoccupied substrate. Sources: `results/BAS_HART_INDEPENDENT_COPDEM_TERRAIN_V26.json` and `results/BAS_HART_ORIGINAL_COPDEM_TERRAIN_TRANSFER_V27.json`, [real-source actions](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina/actions/runs/40280146558).

Therefore: **STOP attempts to fit island extinction/colonisation, quantitative penguin-vs-flying guild ecology or independently verified nest-number trends from this old archive** until original count stage/citation/landmass/effort lineage can be recovered. **Current original-data-backed ecological verdict:** Antarctic/sub-Antarctic breeding spatial configuration is strongly nested/hierarchical, local species segregation observed, but a universal simple topographic filter was not supported and **a novel causal effect of physical island P vs exposed breeding substrate F vs dynamic fast ice T is still NOT identified**. Re-running the same old outcomes against arbitrary shoreline buffers, georeference aliases, terrain models or historical presence-only counts is not an approved rescue. Retain the complete original atlas and versioned source receipts; prioritize direct authentic multi-island repeated actual nest surveys with explicit visited-zero and substrate/fast-ice history. Ecology PR189 and paused Zavodovski PR196 remain untouched.
