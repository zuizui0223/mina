# Manuscript spine v5 — hierarchical variability promoted

## Working title

**Common decline, divergent endpoints: hierarchical demography across Antarctic penguin breeding islands**

## Central ecological contribution

The manuscript now distinguishes two spatial hierarchies that need not coincide.

1. **State hierarchy:** islands retain persistent differences in abundance,
   extinction endpoint and broader assembly outcome.
2. **Buffering hierarchy:** temporal compensation is substantially stronger
   among breeding subcolonies within islands than among neighbouring islands.

The upper-level common decline remains context. The positive ecological result
is where temporal variability is reduced when the same population system is
aggregated across nested spatial levels.

## Frozen hierarchy result

Primary subcolony-level inference is restricted to Cormorant, Humble and
Litchfield because these are the only three islands with unchanged reported
colony-code rosters throughout 1991–2017.

Raw Wang–Loreau decomposition:

- beta within islands = **1.0737**
- beta among islands = **1.0112**
- beta total subcolony to archipelago = **1.0858**
- within-island contribution on the additive log-beta scale = **86.4%**

The hierarchy ordering is retained:

- before Litchfield extinction (1991–2006);
- after removing Litchfield entirely (COR/HUM only);
- for linearly detrended log1p abundance in every eligible island;
- for annual log1p growth in every eligible island.

Across all five island totals, raw beta is only **1.0137** (phi = 0.9864), so
the shared long-term decline leaves little raw temporal compensation among
islands.

## Relationship to N_eff

N_eff remains:

> **conditional association, not prediction, not causality**

Frozen coefficient:

- primary beta = **+0.1168**
- after adding previous-year growth = **+0.1185**
- year-block held-out-gain test p = **0.262**
- circular-shift coefficient null remains extreme
- fixed circular count-error sensitivities remain below p = 0.01

The hierarchical result does not prove that temporal compensation causes the
N_eff coefficient. It instead shows why internal breeding-patch organization
is an ecologically appropriate level to examine.

## Context rather than novelty

- PC1 = 96.4% remains regional background.
- median annual-growth correlation = 0.373 remains timescale context.
- year/island variance partition remains descriptive.
- failed sea-ice-duration and snowfall formulations remain bounded negative
  mechanism tests.
- Biscoe/Dream community trajectories remain endpoint context, not the core
  hierarchy test.

## Claim boundary

Allowed:

- temporal compensation is stronger within eligible islands than among islands;
- the state hierarchy and buffering hierarchy differ;
- island totals are strongly synchronous in their long-term decline;
- internal breeding components retain more asynchronous dynamics.

Not allowed:

- beta variability proves causal spatial insurance;
- dispersal among colony codes produced rescue;
- 86.4% is a causal mechanism fraction;
- N_eff is a supported early-warning indicator;
- census colony codes are equivalent to mapped GIS polygons.

## Discussion order

1. Established timescale-dependent synchrony is context.
2. Introduce state hierarchy versus buffering hierarchy.
3. Show that raw island-to-archipelago compensation is weak.
4. Show that the larger nested beta transition occurs within islands and is
   robust to pre-extinction, Litchfield-removal, detrending and growth checks.
5. Report bounded environmental mechanism failures.
6. Position N_eff as a small structured-null-robust conditional association,
   including the previous-growth sensitivity.
7. Use Torgersen only as phenomenon-level spatial triangulation.
8. End with colony-code/GIS crosswalk and external-archipelago replication as
   the decisive next tests.

## Journal position

**Ecosphere remains the first-shot target.**

The hierarchy result materially improves the ecological contribution because
the manuscript now contains a positive, scale-explicit ecological result that
does not depend on restoring the failed N_eff predictive claim.

## Terminal rule

No additional same-census hierarchy metric, alternate beta definition,
post-hoc colony-roster reconciliation, topology metric, climate window, weather
month or threshold search is permitted for v0.6.

New positive evidence must come from:

- external colony-code/GIS spatial crosswalk;
- another monitored archipelago;
- an independently defined mechanistic covariate.
