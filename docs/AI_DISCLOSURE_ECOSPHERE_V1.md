# AI-use disclosure template — Ecosphere

ESA's current policy requires disclosure when generative-AI tools contribute to
manuscript writing, image production, data collection, or analysis beyond
ordinary spelling/grammar editing.

For this project, a suitable factual disclosure is:

> OpenAI ChatGPT was used during software development, analysis-workflow
> implementation, diagnostic design, and manuscript drafting/revision. All
> code, numerical results, statistical decisions, interpretation, citations,
> and final manuscript text were reviewed and approved by the authors, who take
> full responsibility for the work.

Placement:

1. Include an AI-use disclosure in the manuscript Acknowledgments if required by
   the final author-approved submission.
2. Repeat the disclosure in the ScholarOne submission form as requested by ESA.
3. Do not list any AI system as an author.

The final authors should review this wording for completeness before submission.
