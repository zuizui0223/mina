**Date:** 2026-10-07

## Established prior art

Do not claim novelty for:

- ecological recovery having multiple dimensions;
- trajectory-versus-state or hysteresis concepts;
- aggregate recovery differing from compositional or patch recovery;
- pulse-disturbance impact versus return-time frameworks;
- site fidelity or temporary nonbreeding;
- uneven local demographic rates changing spatial composition.

Relevant prior art includes Lamothe et al. (2019), Hillebrand et al. (2020), White et al. (2022), Wilson et al. (2023), and the broader resilience literature.

## Primary empirical contribution

The focal Ross natural experiment gives strongly discordant answers to three recovery questions:

- **amount:** 96.97% of aggregate loss restored;
- **path:** 90.59% inverse-path fidelity;
- **state:** only 2.4% of baseline compositional displacement erased.

The 2001→2002 compositional shift was the largest of 25 complete adjacent Ross transitions.

Given the observed 96.97% aggregate rebound, exact proportional inverse reversal would have produced an endpoint only 0.0717% TV from baseline and E6 ≈ baseline. The observed endpoint was 4.97% TV from baseline with substantially lower E6.

Therefore:

> **recovery can occur in the same places as loss without restoring the same spatial population state.**

## Why the structured residual matters

The 9.41% inverse-path mismatch is not treated as intrinsically large or statistically exceptional.

Its ecological importance is that it is strongly structured:

- Cape Crozier West overshot its exact inverse allocation by ~10,271 pairs;
- the other five components under-recovered relative to that counterfactual.

This residual was sufficient to turn an almost perfectly restored counterfactual state into an observed state with materially different composition and dominance.

## Process contrasts

Palmer/Signy and Beaufort are not independent replications of the Ross path/state result.

They provide biological contrasts showing how unequal local multipliers reweight spatial state:

- persistent decline: unequal attrition beyond proportional thinning;
- capacity release: unequal expansion beyond proportional growth.

## Forbidden claims

Do not write that:
- spatial memory was restored;
- high path fidelity implies state restoration;
- the same individuals returned;
- the path/state distinction is a new theory;
- the focal 9.41% mismatch is exceptional within Ross;
- the three Ross episodes are independent replicates;
- Palmer/Signy/Beaufort constitute a pooled process-class test.

## Strongest novelty sentence

> **The population recovered where it had declined, but not in the same proportions.**
