# Program spine v1 — where is the information in penguin decline?

**Source:** 66-result evidence ledger on `main` at `7764523c5f1e536b86084de8583cdd6262ee8fa7`.

## One-sentence story

> **Penguin decline shares a regional direction, but its most reproducible local form is written in the changing spatial allocation of breeders: static place traits, snapshot phenotypes, and simple environmental responses do not yield a confirmed transferable rule, whereas breeding-space concentration survives independent geographic and cross-species replication.**

This wording is deliberately weaker than “the region determines direction.” The data establish a shared regional-scale direction, not causal identification of the regional driver.

## The hierarchy as an elimination argument

### Level 1 — Regional direction is real

The five Palmer Adélie island populations share an exceptionally strong long-term same-sign component (PC1 = 96.4%), despite only moderate annual synchrony.

What survives:
- a common multi-decadal decline;
- heterogeneous island fates and annual deviations.

What fails:
- a simple positive annual sea-ice-duration response;
- 3/5/7-year smoothed sea-ice-duration rescue;
- October snowfall × snow-prone-habitat amplification.

**Interpretation:** regional-scale information exists, but the tested simple annual/low-frequency predictors do not identify the regional driver.

### Level 2 — Static place does not become a confirmed transferable rule

Paper 2 contains extensive outcome-blind atlas, coverage, observation, forcing and recoverability work, but those D0 receipts are design infrastructure, not ecological support.

On real outcomes:
- all three species have negative A × H point estimates;
- the frozen cross-species permutation test is non-confirmatory (p = 0.0947);
- no species interaction survives Holm correction;
- the sign weakens at 1 km and reverses at 5 km;
- the observed radius sign switch is itself null-compatible;
- MDE analysis shows good power only for very large common effects (~0.50–0.55), not moderate effects around 0.30.

**Interpretation:** the tested static island-architecture formulation is not a confirmed Antarctic-wide rule. Very large common effects are constrained; moderate effects are not excluded.

### Level 3 — Snapshot island phenotype is not a stable within-species state

Assemblage-level morphological differentiation is largely species sorting. Within Adélie:
- fixed island effects are weak over the Palmer Penguins window;
- pairwise island contrasts reverse across years;
- same-year island classification exceeds cross-year transfer.

**Interpretation:** much of the apparent “island phenotype” is borrowed from species composition or rebuilt through time rather than expressing a persistent local ecotype.

This remains C2 exploratory evidence and should not carry the main program claim.

### Level 4 — Breeder configuration is the strongest replicated local signal

Palmer discovery:
- Cormorant: E declines ~19%;
- Humble: ~51%;
- Litchfield: ~83% before extinction;
- all exceed proportional-thinning + frozen Poisson/CV10/CV20 count-error expectations.

Prospective external replication:
- Signy Adélie passes the same concentration logic.

Prospective cross-species replication:
- Signy chinstrap passes independently.

What does **not** transfer:
- component identity: Palmer loses initial dominants, Signy retains them;
- effect magnitude;
- a universal κ ≈ 0.25 law;
- a common threshold;
- a ratchet rule.

**Interpretation:** the transferable quantity is the **direction of spatial reorganization beyond proportional thinning**.

This is the only positive ecological phenomenon in the current program with both independent geographic and cross-species prospective replication.

### Level 5 — Mechanistic traces exist but do not identify the process

#### N_eff and near-term demography

The N_eff family should be read as one chain:
- positive conditional coefficient;
- survives circular-shift, mechanical-coupling, count-error and demographic-momentum diagnostics;
- but the held-out predictive gain is null-compatible under synchronized year-block permutation (p = 0.262).

**Final status:** robust conditional association, not a predictive mechanism.

#### Reproductive performance and redistribution

Palmer:
- short-lag performance-linked redistribution persists to lag 2–3;
- 4–5 year delayed recruitment echo fails;
- win-stay/lose-switch asymmetry fails;
- island closure is inconclusive.

Signy:
- frozen primary lag-2 replication fails (p = 0.279);
- a favorable sensitivity cannot rescue the null primary result.

**Final status:** locally plausible dynamic process, not a transferable Antarctic mechanism.

#### Reproductive mechanism

The denominator audit blocks a simple story that larger colonies universally produce more chicks per pair or that reproductive collapse precedes extinction.

**Final status:** no Palmer-wide Allee-like reproductive mechanism.

### Level 6 — Individual process remains below public-data resolution

The mark–resight audit finds:
- individual banding exists;
- standardized searches for returning birds are documented;
- but the public ID → later breeding-site / breeding-status bridge is absent.

**Interpretation:** the program reaches a genuine information boundary. Counts can reveal redistribution; they cannot distinguish adult retention, breeding dispersal, prospecting, mortality, temporary nonbreeding or immigration at the individual level.

## Manuscript logic

The program should not be narrated as:

> “We tried 66 endpoints and one happened to work.”

It should be narrated as:

> “We moved down a hierarchy of candidate information levels. Regional synchrony established a shared background. Static place and phenotype did not yield a transferable rule. Within-population breeder configuration produced the only positive signal that survived prospective geographic and cross-species replication. Mechanistic analyses then narrowed but did not identify the process, and individual-level data were insufficient to go further.”

The negative and stopped routes therefore have a positive logical role: they define **where the information is not demonstrably located**.

## Strongest allowed claim

> **Across the current Antarctic Pygoscelis evidence, the most reproducible population-level information about how decline is spatially expressed lies in breeder allocation among monitored breeding components, not in a confirmed common static island trait or snapshot phenotype.**

Important qualifiers:
- “most reproducible” is comparative within this research program, not a universal theorem;
- “information” means an empirically supported population-level association/endpoint, not Shannon information;
- the regional cause is not identified;
- the individual redistribution mechanism is not identified.

## Natural next test

The next independent test is not another same-data mechanism search.

It is:

> **Does abundance-conditioned breeding-space concentration recur in component-resolved colonial breeders outside Pygoscelis?**

That is the correct route to taxonomic generality. If external data require permission or are unavailable, the current paper should stop at Pygoscelis rather than substituting another post-hoc search in the same penguin records.
