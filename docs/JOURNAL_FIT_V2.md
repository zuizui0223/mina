# Journal fit v2 — after N_eff uncertainty diagnostics

Checked: 27 September 2026.

## Decision

**First shot: Ecosphere — Article, Animal Ecology or Coastal and Marine Ecology track.**

**Fallback: Ecology and Evolution — Research Article.**

The previous Journal of Animal Ecology submission package remains archived but is paused. Its only positive held-out predictive pillar (effective colony number) does not survive the frozen synchronized year-block permutation null (p=0.262), so JAE is no longer the recommended first shot without genuinely independent validation.

## Why Ecosphere now fits best

The revised paper is a multiscale ecological case study rather than a high-strength single-mechanism animal-ecology result.

Its defensible contribution is:

1. a 27-year synchronized multi-island demographic record;
2. scale-aware interpretation of regional coherence versus annual synchrony;
3. heterogeneous local endpoints under shared regional decline: persistence, local extinction/vacancy and species replacement;
4. finite prospective falsification of simple sea-ice-duration and snowfall formulations;
5. a conditional colony-organization association with explicit permutation and count-error diagnostics;
6. independent Torgersen spatial triangulation with a clearly unresolved colony-code/GIS crosswalk.

Ecosphere's current scope is deliberately broad across ecological subdisciplines. It includes Animal Ecology and Coastal and Marine Ecology subject tracks and accepts quantitative work addressing questions of broad ecological relevance. That is a better match for the revised “externally subsidized breeding islands” synthesis than a manuscript whose acceptance depends on one strong predictive effect.

## Why Ecology and Evolution is the fallback

Ecology and Evolution explicitly maintains a broad scope and states that descriptive and preliminary ecological studies are acceptable. That makes it robust to the revised evidence structure: several bounded negative mechanism tests, strong descriptive long-term data, heterogeneous ecological endpoints and one conditional association that does not support robust held-out prediction.

## Why not lead with JAE now

The frozen N_eff uncertainty result changes the risk profile:

- observed held-out MSE gain: +0.00102884;
- synchronized year-block permutation p: 0.26224;
- observed gain percentile: 73.78%.

The standardized coefficient remains unusual under year permutation and fixed count-error coupling simulations, but that is an association result, not the robust predictive result described in the old JAE draft.

Without an independent archipelago replication or a resolved colony-code-to-GIS habitat crosswalk, keeping JAE as the first shot would rely too heavily on procedural strength and negative tests rather than on a strong positive ecological advance.

## Manuscript positioning

Lead with **divergent local ecological fates under a common regional decline in an externally subsidized island system**.

Treat as context, not novelty:
- PC1 = 96.4%;
- long-term versus annual synchrony contrast;
- Moran/timescale synchrony theory.

Treat as bounded secondary evidence:
- failed sea-ice-duration and snowfall formulations;
- N_eff beta +0.1168;
- N_eff held-out gain (report permutation p=0.262);
- count-error simulations;
- Torgersen spatial triangulation.

Do not use Palmer Penguins morphology as Introduction motivation. It may remain repository provenance or Supplementary exploratory history but is not part of the core scientific rationale.
