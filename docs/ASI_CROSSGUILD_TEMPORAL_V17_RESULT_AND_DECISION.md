### Implications for the research question

Across verified V14 AADC and V15/V16/V17 original data sources, only descriptive multi-species breeding sample coverage, not generalizable cross-guild breeding-change mechanisms, has been identified. V16 supports matched-date cross-sectional comparability at the *source survey* level; it does not identify annual population growth mechanisms, colony-level habitat effects, marine prey interactions or causal penguin-driven subsidies.

The next ecological information gain is **not** to re-fit the same 25 two-region transitions or relax the third-region gate. Prioritize a different source: genuine annual census, survival or breeding success at physical island units for independent penguin guilds, with known observation protocols. NOAA NCEI accession **0185113** (1977–2015, Copacabana on King George Island and Cape Shirreff on Livingston Island, 3 penguin species; DOI 10.25921/58yq-7g68) and BAS Signy 1978–2020 stage-resolved census source families are candidate observations. A new source cannot be treated as an independent replication if it copies ASI/APBP values or if nest/chick abundance estimands differ.
