# Evidence hierarchy for the spatial-recovery paper v1

**Status:** synthesis frozen after Ross, prospective Bird Island, and prospective emperor endpoints were opened.

## Tier 1 — focal ecological result

### Ross Island Adélie natural experiment

Role:

    mechanistic/natural-history centerpiece

Key comparison:

    1999 pre-shock
    -> 2001 mega-iceberg breeding trough
    -> 2002 immediate rebound.

Aggregate loss restored by 2002:

    96.97%.

Yet local restoration fractions span:

    10.8% to 109.8%

across the six fixed breeding components.

The biological result is:

> near-complete aggregate breeding rebound does not imply restoration of the local abundance vector.

Important status boundary:

- the original 2001–2012 Ross V2 prediction was frozen before effects;
- the sharper 1999–2002 restoration decomposition was generated post-result after identifying 2001 as a documented disturbance trough;
- mechanism interpretation is supported by independent published iceberg/access studies but is not a prospectively tested causal mediation.

## Tier 2 — prospective external sign tests

### Bird Island Gentoo

Role:

    prospective positive-net-change counterexample

Frozen endpoint rule:

    earliest complete six-unit season -> latest complete six-unit season
    1981 -> 2024.

Result:

    N +34.2%
    E +15.7%.

Use:

> positive aggregate change can accompany spatial equalization.

Do not call the full interval a preidentified recovery phase.

Secondary preallowed temporal audit:

    all four annual delta-N / delta-E sign combinations occur.

### Global emperor penguin network

Role:

    prospective decline-side external test

Frozen:

    50 paired colonies
    2009 -> 2018
    posterior median colony abundance index.

Result:

    N -12.4%
    E -12.8%
    30 colonies down
    20 up.

Predeclared regional sensitivity:

    all four N/E sign quadrants occur across the eight eligible ice regions.

Use:

> decline can concentrate at global scale without universal local decline, while regional aggregate and spatial directions remain decoupled.

## Tier 3 — discovery/supporting systems

### Palmer/Signy Adélie

Role:

    discovery of loss-driven concentration arithmetic.

Key result:

    0/39 frozen components increase first-to-last;
    gross gains tiny relative to losses.

Use:

    differential attrition as a decline-side mechanism example.

### Heard Island King

Role:

    post-result historical triangulation.

Use:

    continuous local positive change can coexist with non-monotonic E and permanent dominance reversal.

Do not call confirmatory.

### Beaufort Adélie

Role:

    post-result/corrected mechanism contrast.

Use:

    new/small subcolony gains disproportionate share after habitat release; spatial spreading is possible.

Do not treat as a replication.

## Paper-level evidence logic

The paper should not require all systems to share one direction.

Instead:

1. Ross shows scalar restoration can fail to restore the spatial vector.
2. Bird prospectively shows positive aggregate change can move E upward.
3. Emperor prospectively shows global decline can move E downward while regions span all four directions.
4. Supporting cases illustrate concrete demographic paths that generate those states.

The general claim is therefore about **separability**, not a universal sign.

## What belongs in the main text

Main Results:
- Ross restoration decomposition;
- Bird prospective endpoint;
- emperor prospective global endpoint + compact regional four-quadrant result.

Main Discussion:
- common versus contrast demographic modes;
- Ross disturbance/access mechanism;
- monitoring implications.

Supplement or brief Discussion:
- Palmer/Signy arithmetic;
- Heard;
- Beaufort;
- branch-dependence/hysteresis diagnostics.

This hierarchy prevents a selected-case collage from becoming the paper's evidentiary structure.
