# Anonymous cover letter draft — Journal of Animal Ecology

Dear Editors,

Please consider our Research Article, **“Common decline, divergent collapse: breeding-colony organization indexes local vulnerability across Antarctic penguin islands,”** for publication in *Journal of Animal Ecology*.

The manuscript uses a complete 27-year synchronized census of five neighbouring Adélie penguin breeding islands to address a general problem in animal ecology: how strongly shared regional population change can coexist with patch-specific demographic dynamics and local extinction. The five islands share an exceptionally strong common long-term component, yet their annual growth is only moderately synchronous and their endpoints differ substantially.

A key feature of the study is that we do not infer mechanism from synchrony alone. We prospectively bounded and then falsified simple annual and low-frequency sea-ice-duration formulations and an annual snowfall-by-habitat interaction. We then tested one predeclared within-island state variable. Effective colony number provided a small positive held-out-year increment beyond current abundance, island identity and secular time, whereas simple occupied-colony count did not. An externally fixed large-group threshold also failed its directional hypothesis. Independent Torgersen mapping provides phenomenon-level evidence that real subcolony attrition is spatially structured, while an unresolved colony-code-to-GIS crosswalk prevents us from making a causal fragmentation claim.

We think the manuscript fits *Journal of Animal Ecology* because it uses a long-term animal population system to separate regional demographic direction, local annual dynamics and within-patch population organization. More broadly, it develops the idea of externally subsidized breeding islands: marine central-place foragers obtain most trophic resources outside the terrestrial patch, allowing breeding-patch state to emerge as a distinct axis of local vulnerability.

All core ecological endpoints were closed before manuscript preparation. The repository retains prospectively frozen contracts, result receipts and automated numerical/claim guards. Public source data are identified by DOI in the manuscript, and an anonymized reproducibility snapshot can be supplied for review.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.

Sincerely,
