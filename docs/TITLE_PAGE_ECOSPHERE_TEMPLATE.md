## Open Research Statement

The primary Adélie penguin breeding-pair census used in this study is publicly
archived by Palmer Station Antarctica LTER at DOI
10.6073/pasta/89dd52217ca37e3a72a67f7a9bc3c82e. The seasonal sea-ice
indices and Palmer Station daily weather data used in the bounded environmental
tests are publicly archived at DOI
10.6073/pasta/4207e529832840db2282498d9f4f4f05 and DOI
10.6073/pasta/3eefb45dbfb784c3cabe3690ea46fe9e, respectively. Broader
penguin abundance and distribution records are available through the Antarctic
Penguin Biogeography Project/MAPPPD and are cited in the manuscript. Novel
analysis code, frozen analysis contracts, result receipts, tests, and figure
generation code are publicly available for peer review at
https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina. If the manuscript is accepted, the exact
code and derived-data snapshot supporting the accepted version will be archived
in a permanent repository (Dryad or Zenodo) and the permanent identifier will
replace this prospective statement.
