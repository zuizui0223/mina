# Bird Island gentoo prospective recovery-allocation result v1

**Status:** external prospective result. Endpoint rule, six-unit roster and recovery gate were frozen before nest-count magnitudes were opened.

## Frozen endpoints

Outcome-blind support fixed:

    1981 -> 2024

as the earliest and latest complete six-unit nest-count seasons.

## Recovery gate

Total incubating nests:

    1981: 3,331
    2024: 4,470
    change: +34.2%.

Therefore the frozen recovery gate passes.

## Primary result

Effective breeding-unit number:

    E1981 = 3.569
    E2024 = 4.128
    change = +15.7%
    delta log(E) = +0.1454.

This is **spreading/equalizing breeding recovery**, not recovery concentration.

## Proportional-growth reference

Aggregate multiplication:

    G_bar = 1.34194.

Starting-dominance-weighted RMS multiplication:

    G_D = 1.24781.

Exactly:

    E1/E0 = (G_bar/G_D)^2
          = 1.15655.

The main positive proportional residual is at Square Pond:

    observed-minus-proportional expectation = +610 nests.

Johnson, both Upper units and Lower Mountain Cwm receive less growth than exact proportional allocation would predict.

## Composition turnover

Total-variation distance between endpoint compositions:

    TV = 0.1611.

Dominant breeding unit:

    1981: Johnson
    2024: Johnson.

So the system becomes more even without changing the identity of its largest monitored breeding unit.

## Confirmatory decision

Under the frozen decision logic this is:

    Outcome B — N up, E up.

Therefore Bird Island is a **prospective external counterexample** to any universal recovery-concentration rule.

It supports the conditional/path-dependent model:

> breeding recovery can either concentrate or spread depending on how positive local change is distributed across the existing breeding-node network.

## Why this matters for PR189

Before this test, the evidence set contained:

- Ross: shock trough -> unequal rebound / reconcentration;
- Heard: non-monotonic recovery with dominance reversal;
- Beaufort: small/new unit gains share after habitat release.

Bird Island is stronger than those as a generality check because:

1. the six-unit roster was fixed before counts were opened;
2. endpoints were determined from missingness only;
3. the system is geographically independent of Ross/Palmer/Signy;
4. the focal species is Gentoo rather than Adélie;
5. the response is directly comparable incubating/occupied nest abundance.

The result says the paper must **not** argue that numerical recovery tends to concentrate.

The stronger general result is:

> **the spatial direction of breeding recovery is not determined by the sign of aggregate recovery.**
