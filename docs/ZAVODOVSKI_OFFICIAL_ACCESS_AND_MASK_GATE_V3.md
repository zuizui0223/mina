### Stop decision

No temporally causal spatial succession analysis can be done solely from polygons whose cloud-obscured pieces may have been copied from different acquisition years. Even if the species differ in the published census, a two-map intersection can be an artifact of temporal substitution. Do not infer competition, legacy, dispersal, or site turnover from raw overlay.

### Mechanistic inference warning

Even with authentic same-year masks, a high succession overlap is a *pattern*, not evidence of guano legacy or competitive release. Those require independent environmental or experimental evidence and an eligible-habitat matched comparison.
