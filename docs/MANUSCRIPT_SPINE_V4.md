# Manuscript spine v4 — final N_eff null reconciliation

## Working title

**Common decline, divergent endpoints: multiscale demography across Antarctic penguin breeding islands**

## Central ecological contribution

The paper is about how a shared regional demographic decline resolves into
different local ecological states in an externally subsidized island system.

The main local outcomes are:

- persistence at very low abundance;
- local extinction/vacancy;
- species replacement at the Biscoe Point benchmark;
- habitat-structured subcolony attrition on Torgersen.

## What is contextual rather than novel

- PC1 = 96.4% is descriptive regional background.
- The weaker annual synchrony is interpreted through established
  timescale-dependent synchrony/Moran-effect theory.
- Failed sea-ice-duration and snowfall formulations are bounded falsifications,
  not evidence that climate or terrestrial habitat are unimportant.

> **conditional association, not prediction, not causality**

The coefficient is not readily reproduced by the predeclared temporal-surrogate
