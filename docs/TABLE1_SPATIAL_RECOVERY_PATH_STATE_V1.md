# Table 1. Evidence architecture for spatial recovery and reweighting

| Evidence role | System / interval | Aggregate change | Spatially neutral counterfactual | Observed spatial result | Inferential boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Focal natural experiment | Ross Island Adélie, 1999→2001→2002 | −54.3% to trough; 96.97% of loss restored | Rebound allocated in proportion to preceding local losses | Local restoration 10.8–109.8%; inverse-path fidelity 90.59% (6 components) / 93.27% (3 colonies); state restoration incomplete at both grains | Path/state and grain audits are post-result; no individual identity |
| Within-series calibration | Ross complete down→up episodes | Aggregate restoration 34.8%, 97.0%, 122.1% | Same proportional inverse-path baseline | Inverse-path fidelity 89–91%; six-component state restoration +2.4%, −29.2%, −53.4% | Three episodes are not independent replicates |
| Persistent-decline discovery | Palmer Adélie, 1991→2017 | Regional decline | Fixed starting composition thinned proportionally | Effective breeding-unit number −19.1%, −50.6%, −82.7% on stable-roster islands beyond frozen nulls | Census codes are not mapped polygons; causal vital rates unidentified |
| Persistent-decline replication | Signy Adélie, 1998→2009 | 2,688→901 breeding pairs | Fixed starting composition thinned proportionally | Effective breeding-unit number −29.7%; frozen replication supported | No separate survival/recruitment/movement decomposition |
| Capacity-change contrast | Beaufort Adélie, 2004→2010 | Island total +34.3% | Island growth allocated in proportion to 2004 composition | Small/new unit gain 3.15× proportional expectation; share 0.95%→1.48%; independent export toward Ross declined | Census and movement are independent evidence streams, not mediation |

**Common biological logic.** Aggregate abundance is a weighted result of local changes. Spatial state is preserved only when local changes are sufficiently proportional to their counterfactual expectation.

**Claim ceiling.** Ross is the focal path-versus-state natural experiment. Palmer, Signy, and Beaufort are process contrasts showing other forms of unequal local change; they are not treated as independent replications of Ross.
