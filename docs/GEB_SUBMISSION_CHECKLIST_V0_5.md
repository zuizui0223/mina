## Claim checks before every export

The submission must continue to state that:

1. Palmer lag 2 is fixed-specification validation after provenance repair.
2. The one-year association is not the novelty and is mechanically coupled here.
3. Independent REPRO mean nest success does not replicate the primary association.
4. HUMPOP is stopped/non-estimable, not a null effect.
5. Part II is directionally concordant but non-confirmatory.
6. Radius variation does not establish biological scale dependence.
7. Parts I and II are not a head-to-head predictive model comparison.
8. State does not replace physical habitat.
9. Public-information use is not demonstrated.
10. Ross unpublished behavioral outcomes are absent.
