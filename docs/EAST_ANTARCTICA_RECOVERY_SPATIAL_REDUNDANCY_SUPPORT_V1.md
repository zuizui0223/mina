# East Antarctica 99-site route: support gate result

**Status:** SUPPORT FAIL under the source frozen in V1.

The S1 file was downloaded only after the V1 contract was committed.

S1 contains:
- the 99-site coordinate roster and regional membership;
- the number of sites by region;
- regional 30-year growth-rate summaries;
- regional abundance time series for the 72-site higher-frequency subset.

It does **not** contain the paired historical and recent standardized abundance point estimates for each of the 99 sites required by the frozen E endpoint.

Therefore:

> the V1 East Antarctic effective-site-number test is not opened and receives no biological result.

No alternative statistic is substituted from the same S1 after seeing its contents.

The publication-level facts remain useful context: all five regions increased, 84/99 local populations increased, 15 declined, and local growth rates were heterogeneous. But those facts were known before V1 and are not treated as a new test.

A new route is eligible only if another source is identified that exposes site-level abundance and its endpoint is frozen before those values are inspected.
