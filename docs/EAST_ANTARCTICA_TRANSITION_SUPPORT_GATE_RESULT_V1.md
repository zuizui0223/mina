The ecological conclusion is a **data boundary**, not a negative colonization result: the archive is broad in space and time but sparse in consecutive annual revisits of explicit absent sites. The route is therefore closed for annual transition inference. No NR pseudoabsence, nonconsecutive interval, alternate geographic aggregation, or selective period may rescue it.
