# V25 circum-Antarctic coastal rock opportunity frame and marine niche pilot
2026-11-27 JST. Parent 12d478e8cd616ed0730257c32ae246f3215eac77. Fixed source-based descriptive opportunity analysis, not fitted SDM, occupancy, future population prediction or preregistration of independent outcomes. Prior V17–24 and main untouched.
## Independent denominator
Use entire original BAS automatically extracted rock outcrop v7.3 (DOI 10.5285/178ec50d-1ffb-42a4-a4a3-1145419da2bb, publisher linked zipped shapefile 41 MB), and original high-res coastline polylines v7.12 (DOI 10.5285/dbaf29f5-c8dc-4e79-aeb1-1df316e951ec, publisher linked ZIP 47.2 MB). Deliberate pinned editions, not claims of latest. Open coast defined exclusively by literal source surface rock coastline and ice coastline; exclude grounding lines, shelf front, rock against shelf, rumple. Original Plantarctica union classes as V22/23 (exact SHA256), 2017–2023 composite. Verify source URL, ZIP CRC, original layer/CRS, geometry types and source counts; report invalid polygons, stop if invalid geometry area exceeds 0.5% of summed source polygon area; otherwise exclude and report number/area rather than silent repair. All distances/areas in EPSG3031 projected approximation. Fixed 1km raster [-3500km,+3500km] axes, all_touched source rasterization, source polygons/lines not fractional cover. Use rasterized coastal distance from cell centers to coast-hit cell centers, primary <=5km, fixed sensitivity <=1 and <=10 km; exclude raster cell centers north of 60S. Raster coast approximation and no walking-path/slope accessibility. "Coastal mapped-rock opportunity pixels" are pixels intersecting mapped rock near mapped open coast, not square kilometres of exposed rock, guaranteed accessible habitat, or true absence. Aggregate each into 10km fixed grid tiles with at least one such 1km rock-hit pixel; count rock-hit pixels and terrestrial vegetation/lichen-hit pixels sharing those coastal-rock pixels. Report area-independent cell numbers and geographic block counts. Independent rock and plant map detection incomplete, shared spatial/mapping bias. All 594 eligible MAPPPD v5 known focal sites, 212 modern-positive 2017–23 (41/137/57 species) reproduced from V23 source pin; use exact 10km tile containing original site center, no snapping. Report points outside frame, species-specific distinct observed cells and how many have vegetation in coastal rock-hit pixels. Unrecorded cells NOT absences. 
## Marine comparison
First fixed 12-scene OSI SAF native 25km monthly pilot (1993,2008,2018 Aug–Nov, as original 595 pilot, no outcome selection). Download original publisher netCDFs, compare each SHA to original 120-scene archived receipt 13671496095. Same native grid fingerprint, reference 1993-09 wet pixels, geodesic within 100km, >=3 pixels and >=80% valid per month. At each 10km candidate tile use centroid of included rock-hit pixel centers (not claimed nest center), exact same field for recorded and background tiles. Require all 12 months, report missing separately. Average 12 equally; this is NOT 30-year climatology or future forecast. Compare fixed SIC bins [0,20),[20,40),[40,60),[60,80),[80,100] for all coastal candidate cells, rock-hit-pixel weighted opportunity and recorded modern species cells. Primary 5km, repeat 1/10km opportunity sensitivity for terrestrial support; marine primary only. For each species, compare observed cell mean SIC and map-hit fraction to expected means in the same 250km EPSG3031 blocks weighted by observed cell count (within-block opportunity weighted by rock-hit pixel counts). Keep unmatched and missing separate; no p-values, no background true absence. Report block counts, number of observed tiles, total denominator, differences within-block and across entire continent. No ecological causal inference or novelty guarantee. 
## Prior art
Gallagher et al. 2025 DOI 10.1002/ecs2.70091 already models marine + terrestrial suitability WAP using logistic regression and assumed true absences; this work's opportunity inventory does not make that assumption. Burton-Johnson et al. 2016 DOI 10.5194/tc-10-1665-2016 already produced rock map, no new remote sensing classification. V25 only builds continental opportunity frame and a pilot marine exposure comparison; need full multi-decade climate, terrain/snow, source QA, independent survey effort before dynamic habitat model. No future nests projected. No further local source fishing.
