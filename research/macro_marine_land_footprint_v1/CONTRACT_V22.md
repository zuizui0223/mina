# Pan-Antarctic marine-to-land footprint — V22 macroecology
2026-11-26 JST. Change direction from local mechanism v17–21 to a geographic macroecology main question. Post-outcome exploratory, not a causal model or preregistration. Prior manuscripts/main untouched. Source ref c67e55c9f4f657ef308c02a85c6ff366dce8e1f4.
## Question
How much of the mapped Antarctic terrestrial photosynthetic landscape is near known breeding sources, and does a historical change in the allocation of observed nesting counts change that geographic association beyond a proportional total change? This is distinct from the published static moss SDM and previous 173-site count-versus-vegetation regressions. Not a claim of new nutrient measurement, range-wide census, or actual animal effects. Main target is spatial source/receptor geography, not another locality-level correlation.
