New data lane: observed same-day marine co-use opportunity, not breeding success or ecological competition. Prior art Lowther et al. (2020), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-75148-9, explicitly established overlap of male fur seal and krill fishery ranges and late chinstrap breeding in South Orkneys. Gate remains conditional on timezone, deployment identity and life-stage metadata review. These are design counts only, not proof of actual foraging.
