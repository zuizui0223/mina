# Fixed one-step likelihood audit: Gaussian log-count GAM versus negative-binomial GAM

**2026-11-09 after initial source-pinned Gaussian GAM results were seen.** This is an explicitly **post-result, single-family sensitivity check** to resolve a distributional mismatch, not another search for a significant climate effect. No new response, dates, regions, β, lags or covariates are introduced. Retain draft PR #213 separately from climate PR #206/main/Paper1/2.

## Why

The existing working non-MaxEnt SDM on 661 original MAPPPD count observations uses Gaussian `log1p(nests)`. It found an Adélie within-site SIC association with nominal mgcv approximate p≈6.66×10^-5, but **removing Victoria Land** reduced it to p≈0.151. Before ecological interpretation, determine whether this pattern is robust to the **actual nonnegative integer count sampling family**. This test is not a fix for preferential sampling or unequal detection.

## Frozen support and exact models

- Reuse the **unchanged 661 original observations / 56 site×species groups / 40 surveyed sites** from `nonmaxent_sdm/outputs/marine_observed_site_species_year_661.csv` generated by the original source Git blob-hash gates. No simulated zero, unobserved species/year or novel effort proxy. Original observed zeros (44) remain.
- Use exactly the same design basis as the Gaussian source model: species factor, `s(year_c,by=species,k=6)`, `s(sphere_x,sphere_y,sphere_z,k=12)`, `s(site_species,bs="re")`, plus **only** `s(sic_site_anomaly_per10pp,by=species,k=4)` in environmental model.
- Fit `mgcv::gam(family=mgcv::nb(), method="REML", select=TRUE)` on the **actual integer `observed_nest_count`**; smooth `k` unchanged; fixed `set.seed(20261109)`, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=OMP_NUM_THREADS=MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`. Estimate θ by mgcv; do **not** manually fix θ after looking at significance. Record `converged`, θ, per-species SIC EDF, approximate p, AIC and deviance explained.
- Fit **both** no-SIC and +SIC negative-binomial baseline models, then exactly **one** post-result geographic sensitivity: +SIC fitted on all observed sites except Victoria Land (353 counts, 14 sites) with the same basis and no changing k. The sample selection is fixed by pre-existing geographic sensitivity, not new outcome review.
- Require R fits converged and numerical outputs finite; if not, explicitly mark MODEL_HOLD rather than changing family, smooth basis or dropping species.
- **Terrestrial mapped vegetation sample stays 197 current site×species medians**, which can be noninteger. A negative-binomial count likelihood is **not valid for these median outcomes**; preserve previously tested Gaussian land results rather than force this family on medians.

## What this test can and cannot decide

- A positive/adverse ΔAIC or per-species approximate p measures conditional *in-sample* fit under an assumed NB distribution. It does not validate the raw species count detection/effort, extrapolate a probability to unobserved islands, or predict future SSP abundance.
- NB p-values for 661 **repeated** records (only 40 sites, 6 broad region labels, 26 Victoria Land sites) remain **nominal**—not robust region-block p, ecological causal estimates or FDR-adjusted after all previous exploration.
- The required decision compares the **direction of evidence** from Gaussian log counts and NB counts and whether the Adélie climate smooth survives exclusion of Victoria Land. Report **all** numbers including an ecological negative/unstable result.
- If NB matches the earlier geographic fragility, **stop this SDM model-search branch** and treat it only as evidence that observed breeding counts show region-specific conditional environment associations. Do not pursue new lags, habitat limits or alternative k to rescue p.

Outputs: frozen model receipt, three NB fit metrics and smooth diagnostics, source/identity checks, Japanese result readout. In CI, store source summary and no raw animal IDs.
