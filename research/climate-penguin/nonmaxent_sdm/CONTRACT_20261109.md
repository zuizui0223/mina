# Non-MaxEnt Antarctic breeding-abundance SDM pilot — analysis contract (2026-11-09)

**Branch:** `codex/penguin-nonmaxent-abundance-sdm-20261109`; Draft, targeting climate PR #206, not main. This was designed after seeing earlier ice/vegetation positive correlations, so it is **exploratory and outcome-aware**, not preregistered. It must not be used to search for new significant variants.

## Scientific estimand and why not MaxEnt

- Response is **observed breeding nest-count size at a site where the focal species was counted**, not occurrence probability, habitat suitability at unvisited locations, species absence, colonization or true population size.
- An alternative MaxEnt-style presence-only IPP with background data is close to MaxEnt mathematically (Renner & Warton 2013, DOI 10.1111/j.1541-0420.2012.01824.x). The distinctive value here is making use of **actual counts, observation methods and time-varying original SIC**.
- Use a **penalized generalized additive model (GAM)** on `log1p(observed nest count)` and a separate contemporary land-abundance GAM. Gaussian log-count GAM is a conditional observed-count model; it is **not** a zero-inflated/hurdle or detection-calibrated ecological abundance model. Do not claim actual abundance or absolute suitability predictions without effort/detection data.

## Fixed marine historical cohort and smooth model

From original 1991–2020 season records, join each site×year to exactly four complete nearshore (0–100km) SIC monthly means, August–November, and require `method!="unknown"`, exactly one `n_methods` and nonnegative integer original observed count. For each **site×species** select the **most frequent known method** over those 30 years, breaking any frequency ties by lexical name **before looking at counts**, and retain all original observed zero counts for that method. Expected source support: **661 observations, 56 site×species groups, 40 unique sites (ADPE 482/40, CHPE 84/8, GEPE 95/8)**. Spatial region coverage **6 original broad labels**; 26 of 40 source sites in Victoria Land. This is a cohort of *known surveyed penguin sites*, not a random habitat sample.

For each site, use the mean SIC over the complete 1991–2020 time series to construct `SIC_anomaly = SIC_augNov_year − 1991–2020_site_mean`; this is a retrospective environmental **within-site** variable, and its mean uses future years relative to an earlier census, so it is **not an operational forecast feature**.

Primary GAM on log1p(observed_nests):
- Null: `species + s(year,by=species,k=6) + s(sphere_x,sphere_y,sphere_z,k=12) + s(site_species,bs="re")`.
- Climate: the same + `s(SIC_anomaly/10,by=species,k=4)`.
- `mgcv::gam(method="REML")` Gaussian; compare AIC and adjusted R² as purely descriptive in-sample diagnostics (not external predictive validation). Approximate GAM smooth p-values *not* valid as independent site/region or project-wide FDR evidence. Extract species-specific EDF, parametric/smooth summaries, and support coverage. Spatial smooth uses the unit sphere to avoid longitude seam. Site×species intercepts control repeated counts; their coexistence with a shared spatial smooth limits interpretation of the separate spatial field.

## Fixed contemporary terrestrial companion (different population and time window)

From source `vegetation_response_v1/site_species_cohort.csv`, 2017–2023 season coverage, retain original **positive mapped terrestrial vegetation/lichen detection** only; expected 197 observed site×species rows out of 246 total. This **composite** vegetation map is one cross-sectional covariate, not annual greening, and values of zero map detection are not biological absence.

A separate site/species cross-sectional GAM on `log1p_count_median`:
- Null: `species + region + log1p(observed_seasons) + s(sphere_x,sphere_y,sphere_z,k=12)`.
- Vegetation: same + `s(log1p_mapped_area_m2,by=species,k=4)`. For rare species in this cohort, report support and extrapolation warning. Only descriptive AIC difference/conditional smooth shape; no joint time-varying climate-vegetation mechanism inferred.
- Both models use entirely **different response grains** (historical site/species/observed season count vs current 2017–2023 median site/species count). Do not stack these into one 3-axis regression or claim the same individuals/years.

## Inference/decision gate

1. Source SHA/record grains, counts, species, site IDs, ice completeness and cross-sectional vegetation support must pass; any drift → STOP.
2. Refit each explicit null and environmental model exactly as specified. If splines fail due small sample or identifiability, report MODEL_HOLD and keep source cohort rather than posthoc changing k or selecting better regions.
3. Output GAM fit metrics and **nominal approximate** p-values with clearly labeled model assumptions; do not infer independent positive climate effect, 2050/2100 future nest abundance, vegetation greening, species displacement, or habitat occupancy beyond observed colonies.
4. Prefer physical interpretability to optimizing predictive scores; *no new β or environmental threshold search*. Do not compare absolute response units or fit a joint model with static 2017–2023 vegetation on 1991–2020 seasonal counts.
5. A scientifically defensible future **dynamic multi-species joint SDM with occupancy and separate detection submodel** requires synchronized multi-colony species-resolved survey zeros, effort, time-aligned vegetation series and coastal ocean covariates; present data do not meet that gate.

**Deliverables:** source-verified 661-row marine panel, source-verified 197-row contemporary land panel, two fixed R GAM fits and technical output, Japanese bounded interpretation, exact replay GitHub workflow.
