## Target and sensor pinned before imagery outcomes

- Obtain publicly available **USGS Landsat 8 Collection 2 Level 2 surface reflectance**, STAC collection `landsat-c2-l2`, Planetary Computer's public API. Do **not** substitute Google Earth thumbnail RGB, pseudo-NDVI or classified land pixels for botanical vegetation.
- Only **observed breeding survey site-years** that appear in the frozen `nonmaxent_sdm/joint_support_outputs/site_25_environmental_context.csv` and `matched_63_observed_rows.csv`: expected **50 unique site-years, 25 site IDs, 2017–2020 breeding seasons**. Do not add unobserved site-years to raise apparent sample size. Source coordinates from the same Git-blob-pinned `contemporary_vegetation_site_species_197.csv` (one coordinate per site). Record original source cloud, Landsat asset metadata and returned STAC item IDs.
- Pre-fixed **austral breeding summer**: December 1 of survey season Y through February 28 of Y+1. Some scenes may occur *after* a penguin census (and some census month unknown); their reflectance cannot be interpreted as a pre-census habitat predictor. Observation images 2017–2020 seasons thus cover **2017–2021 calendar** images. Require Landsat 8 `LC08`, valid Level-2 `red`, `nir08`, `qa_pixel`, `qa_radsat`.
- For each site-year, query all STAC scenes intersecting the site center; record full returned scene count and catalog/query errors. If provider return/pagination incomplete, mark `INCOMPLETE_CATALOG`, not zero imagery.
- Preselect at most **three scenes on distinct observation dates**, ordered by scene-level `eo:cloud_cover` then date then item ID, **before examining any local QA or reflectance/NDVI**. Scene-level cloud cover is not local clear land; keep local failures. Do not replace low spectral values with additional scenes after seeing NDVI.
- Pre-fixed identical **81 EPSG:3031 geographic sampling points at 400 m grid spacing**, offsets −1600...+1600m x/y, only points whose euclidean distance to site center is ≤2000m. Native raster values are sampled in each scene's own CRS after coordinate transformation. A physical point persists across years, but Landsat 30 m pixels can differ at registration boundaries; the same geographic point is not a verified same biological vegetation patch.
- **QA-only gate:** Landsat-8 `QA_PIXEL` flags fill (0), cloud dilation (1), high-confidence cirrus (2), cloud (3), shadow (4), snow (5), water (7) must all be clear. `QA_RADSAT` must be zero. No-date or masked source pixels are not zero NDVI. USGS C2 L2 `SR=DN*0.0000275−0.2`; accept physically plausible red and NIR in [0,1.2], positive, sum>0.01; NDVI=(NIR−red)/(NIR+red). These rules remain **spectral QA**, not vegetation cover truth.
- Annual site-point value = median valid original NDVI across sampled scenes in that breeding summer, and the number of distinct valid dates. **Two-year same-geographic-point support** requires at least **two distinct QA-valid scene dates within each compared season**; a site-year comparison needs ≥6 common geography points meeting that requirement and >0 catalog/reflectance errors are explicitly recorded. Report 2-year pairs only for actual census years at the site. Do not choose seasonal date windows after seeing values.
- Summarize all **50 expected source site-years**; low-image, snow-covered, off-land, QA, latency, and provider failures are explicit `NO_DATA` statuses. No fabricated annual greening or climate causal effect if the supported sample is small.
- Compute site-year NDVI deltas **only as spectral differences** if paired support passes; no `vegetation_greening` label, no positive p-value search, no correlation with penguin nests or sea ice in this stage. Snow contamination, guano, algal stains, soil, rock and phenology are alternative causes. Original source COG bands must not be committed; preserve compact provider item IDs, per-point QA outcome and source URL provenance (not signed ephemeral SAS tokens) in CI artifact.
- Retain separate common-map vegetation as a **static site covariate**. Do not back-fill 1991–2020 with 2017–2023 composite, or convert missing species surveys into absences.
