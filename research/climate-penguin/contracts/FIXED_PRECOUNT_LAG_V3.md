Primary: the original 45 matched ADPE|GEPE site/year comparisons within the 381-source cohort, same original citation/vantage. Response is GEPE minus ADPE annual change in log(1+nests). One fixed exposure window only: for each breeding-season label s, the mean August-November ERA5 temperature of s-1. Compare the exposure difference across the two endpoints. This changes the estimand to prior-season covariation; it does not repair or validate the earlier contemporaneous effect. No optimal-lag, month, direction or sample search.
