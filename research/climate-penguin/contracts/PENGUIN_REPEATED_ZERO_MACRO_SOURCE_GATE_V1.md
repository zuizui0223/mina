# Antarctic penguin source-repeated-zero and greening-transfer gate

Frozen 2026-10-29, **explicitly post-outcome support audit**. Existing MAPPPD `mapppd_zero_episodes.csv` has 38 apparent first-zero episodes across five taxa, but most may be one-off observed zero after a long census gap. No same-island georeferenced historic colony footprint nor historical vegetation outcome is in this file. It must not serve as a dataset of 38 independent island extinctions.

Eligible repeated-zero source candidate: for the exact same original site/species ID, preserve **at least two distinct actually surveyed seasons with numeric zero after a documented positive**, no recorded later positive reappearance, and no interpolation of unsurveyed years. Require **≥5 distinct site IDs in ≥3 named geographic regions within a single species** before discussing a continent-wide replicated event. This is a minimal exploratory support threshold, NOT claim of formal statistical power, independent site geography, or biogeographical causality. The user-directed Adélie hypothesis is analyzed separately, not rescued by pooling unrelated species after observing their directions.

The complementary original 2024-published Plantarctica map is **static** even when features are present around a historical zero; no before/after vegetation cover or ecological nutrient transfer is measured. Its original 500m/2km point-buffer features can overlap unrelated physical islands. Source geography, field survey effort, original dated colony polygons, never-breeding controls, and seal trajectories remain necessary for causal colonization/greening comparison.

Previously recovered Litchfield official 2014 ASPA 113 table contains six explicit zero seasons 2007–2012, whereas the pinned MAPPPD original snapshot contains only two; keep those original data lines separate. Historical verified colony location lies in the EAST of Litchfield, and source MAPPPD registry center is **NOT** original nest polygon: existing fixed 25 pixel grid does not measure the abandoned colony. The previous same-sensor Landsat 7 2003–2007 vs 2009–2013 source QA gate passed **Litchfield alone (9 fixed positions)** but failed HUMB/TORG controls and thus is **STOP**. Do not loosen thresholds or infer vegetation change from an unverified nest location.
