# Palmer L7 same-sensor multi-year vegetation-source eligibility gate V1

Date 2026-10-29. **Post-exploration support audit**, not a confirmatory greening test. Earlier 2007/2008 January NDVI at 7 Litchfield and 3 Torgersen site-centroid grid positions was negative or mixed, not vegetation classification. This new independent **February–March Landsat 7 only** period is never adjusted to recover a favorable NDVI direction.

## Original data, time and space

- Reuse original 1986–2026 **Landsat C2L2 QA_PIXEL** annual scene-level source artifact from successful mina workflow [40863960933](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina/actions/runs/40863960933). Its sample is the same predeclared 5×5 EPSG:3031 fixed grid offsets ±80m, ±40m, 0m at **LITC, HUMB, TORG**, NOT physical geolocated historic nesting plots. No original reflectance or vegetation outcome is opened by this audit.
- For a single *same sensor* comparison, keep only original source IDs beginning **LE07** (Landsat 7 ETM+, Collection 2 L2 SR) and independently original QA-clear **February–March** scenes.
- **Pre**: image years 2003–2007 inclusive; **post**: image years 2009–2013 inclusive. Calendar 2007 is the post-summer of breeding season 2006 (last original positive Litchfield count), calendar 2008 the post-summer of 2007 (first zero); 2008 is excluded because the earlier entire February–March original fixed-position QA found no Litchfield clear observation. This exclusion is explicitly after observing QA, so the window is prospective for new reflectance only, not for original seasonal support. No changes after opening spectral output.
- Read the independent original SCAR v7.12 high-resolution coastline SHA256 `66d82f...`, and exact original fixed island land IDs Litchfield 5475, Humble 5496, Torgersen 5504. No nearest-island snapping. Only source QA fixed points whose centers are strictly covered by the original physically separate island polygon survive. Do not treat non-water QA alone as a physical island assignment.
- Within each site×year, select one source scene using ONLY maximum number of physical-island points clear of fill/cloud/shadow/snow/water; tie by least difference to **March 1** on a non-leap day-of-year scale and then lexicographic scene ID. Do not choose after seeing NDVI. Retain scene IDs, dates, original bitmasks and rejected count.
- A **paired fixed position** qualifies if it has at least **3 distinct pre years** and **3 distinct post years** with original QA-clear land in that exact 5×5 source point location. A site passes temporal sample support with >=5 such fixed positions and >=3 distinct valid pre and post image years. Main biological contrast requires Litchfield **and at least one originally named still-breeding control** (Humble or Torgersen) to pass. No recovery by moving offsets, shortening windows or switching sensors. Full colony polygon and exact 30m native pixel area matching remain separate gates.

## Interpretation & STOP

This only establishes *where fixed sample points are physically mapped to a single island and remain seasonally observable on the same sensor*. Same point ≠ identical native 30m pixel footprint across different orbital scenes, and QA-clear non-water/non-snow ≠ vegetated. The mapped 2024 Plantarctica polygons provide a separate static context only and may not be used to select favorable positions. Until image reflectance, radiometric QA, native-pixel independence, spatial extent and original nest polygons are validated, **do not claim vegetation area change, greening, penguin nutrient effect, sea-ice mediation, or warming attribution**.

If the predeclared support gate fails, record missing opportunity and STOP without threshold relaxation. If it passes, a new explicitly exploratory whole-sample source-read plan may be implemented **without altering selection**, saving raw DN/reflectance/QA and within-site annual variation. Dates are field IMAGE dates not original PENGUIN census dates.
