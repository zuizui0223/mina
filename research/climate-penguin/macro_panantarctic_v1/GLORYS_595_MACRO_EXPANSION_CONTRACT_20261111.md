## Central question

The previous original source-verified 34 site / 6 source-region analysis found pooled Aug–Nov SIC/salinity/thetao PC1 explaining 70.94% of standardized marine climate variability, but its variance was 85.91% BETWEEN source regions, and within-site temporal PC1 explained only 47.59%. **Can the geographic pattern extend to the true 595-site/18-region coastal source atlas, and is its decade-by-decade temperature/salinity/sea-ice movement really one reproducible axis?**
