# Frozen source-only optical δ15N×polynya spectral attribution of same 439 and 358 historical records

**2026-11-21 JST**, separate exploratory measurement diagnostic. **No unseen 2013–21 Y and no independent dietary/trophic-level or ocean baseline measurement.** Do not introduce new radii, years, covariates, outcomes or site exclusions.

## Question and prespecified operation

Is the already estimated negative winter-polynya association numerically concentrated in one or a few L4/L5/L7 reflectance bands, in sensor mixing, or a cancellation across multiple spectral channels?

The isotope optical proxy was built with original Youngflesh 2026 six-band PLSR5. The original R `oscorespls`, 1000-stratified-fold source L4/L5/L7 coefficient ensemble was reconstructed with original 103 laboratory guano samples and already locked in successful GitHub Actions [#44838806193](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina/actions/runs/44838806193), artifact **14680080746** ZIP sha256 `be0234fef11a11b5b19129c8a84db0ab90dbe80b7cd6a71a3e733e36eaa1e342`; L7 coefficient original artifact **14602063283**, sha256 `ac1199ad312df63173f7be73452ccbb5e01b7c55f29d3413ede63e1a1b665669`. Original raw L4/L5/L7 RDS source selected positive pixels and site×season 860 membership must be identical to previous fixed analysis.

**Fixed identity:** For a site×season's day-equal optical proxy `Y = sum_{sensor in L4,L5,L7} [sensor_fraction_on_day × intercept_sensor + sum_b(mean_day_band_reflectance_sensor × coefficient_{sensor,b})]` averaged across exact recorded days. With 21 `sensor_intercept+6_sensor_band` linear mean features, same two-way site and region×season FE and unchanged environmental X, the 439-point winter polynya slope is exactly sum of 21 regressions of features on residualized P, each multiplied by the published-method PLSR coefficient. Same for 358-point conditional P0−P1 difference. Validate reconstructed sum against existing `−1.02334031992733`, `−0.9411358938647068`, `−0.6932686855748454` per **+10 percentage points of winter100km coastal polynya** (tolerance 1e−6).

**Output:** 21 signed additive contributions to each β, 7 channel group summaries (`intercept_sensor_fraction` separately; six optical bands summed over three sensors), and all coefficients' signs. Show original band contribution sign and possible cancellation. If contributions are large and cancel, **do not report unstable percentages of the net as physical importance**. This is exactly linear algebra of the measurement estimator, not a new statistical ecological hypothesis and not evidence that a specific molecule or pathway controls optical reflectance. No p-value or feature importance inference from decomposed contributions. The same 1,000 training-fold coefficients can give sensitivity distribution for these contributions, but those folds are not independent biological replicates.

**Controls and source:** Same old439 M_P: controls DOY, DOY², summer temperature, ice, polynya P, two-way FE. Old439 M_SP adds summer S. Same 358 time-contrast P0/P1 simultaneously +salinity, same site×region-year FEs; contrast includes full P0−P1 covariance in already published result. No variants or band exclusion selected by outcome; computations on original frozen candidate 131,014 old guano pixels and 2,659 acquisition days, original 860 rows.

**Interpretive guardrail:** If effect comes mainly from SWIR band(s), the proxy's sensitivity to water/snow/rock/guano mixture remains an alternative to changing trophic position; if it is broadly distributed across wavelengths, that alone does **not** validate direct diet. Baseline isotope (source AA phenylalanine) vs trophic AA is not estimable from optical bands alone; a truly independent baseline/AA dataset is needed. The independent 2026 USAP dataset 602094 source is currently not automatable because official endpoint returned HTML not original CSV (MD5 mismatch).
