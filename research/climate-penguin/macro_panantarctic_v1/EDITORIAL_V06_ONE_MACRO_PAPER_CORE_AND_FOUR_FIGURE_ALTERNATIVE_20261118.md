# One macroecological paper: multidimensional marine change, penguin nesting, and isotope responses

**Editorial candidate v0.6 | 2026-11-18 JST | mina draft PR #214 | not a submitted paper.** The frozen, source-reviewed biology-first manuscript v0.5 and its four figures remain **unchanged**. This is a proposed integrated alternative structure for later author selection, **not automatic merging or an assertion that one causal chain has been demonstrated**.

## One central question

**「南極ペンギンの気候に関連した生態・繁殖変化を、海氷の後退という一つの環境軸と一つの応答係数で要約できるのか？」**

**Hypothesis framing (not established causal mechanism):** Coastal marine environments have at least a thermal–ice and a salinity dimension, while observable biological responses occur at different spatial/time grains (colony reweighting, co-surveyed species ratios, satellite-derived isotope records). A universal mapping of one ice gradient to these multiple responses should **fail spatial transfer**; physically projected exposure can be mapped without claiming future biological abundance.

The **positive salinity–isotope result must stay in the story**; failure to identify fish–krill diet or future nests is a limitation of mechanisms, **not a failed salinity observation**.

## Candidate title

**Sea ice alone does not capture Antarctic penguin coastal environments or their spatially uneven ecological associations**

This is **an observational/predictive-scope title**, not a claim that climate causes penguin population change.

## Candidate abstract (~265 words)

Antarctic penguin population change is often interpreted through sea-ice loss, yet near-colony marine conditions and available biological observations are structured along different environmental, spatial and temporal dimensions. We combined 2,456 method-consistent nest-count records from 595 catalogued sites for three *Pygoscelis* species, historical nearshore sea ice and reanalysis, satellite-derived Adélie isotope records, and selected CMIP6 model scenarios while retaining the true sampling and model grid units. Across 588 marine-matched sites and 1993–2020, the thermal–sea-ice axis explained 60.4% of standardized physical variation, while a predominantly salinity axis explained another 33.4%. In 860 original source-matched Adélie colony-season satellite optical δ¹⁵N records (88 sites), salinity covaried positively with the isotope proxy after accounting for sea ice, temperature, observation timing, site and region–season effects (β=+0.834‰ per salinity unit; partial residual R²=2.88%), although the conservative 13-region interval included zero and regional slopes differed. Within repeatedly counted fixed Adélie colony rosters, nest-count-weighted latitude shifted 88–107 km south between the 1990s and 2010s, while corresponding nearshore sea-ice exposure changed by only 0.02–0.63 percentage points. At six strictly matched two-species sites, gentoo-to-Adélie nest ratios increased throughout, although the combined recorded two-species nesting subtotal increased at five sites. In late-century SSP5-8.5 projections, three selected CMIP6 models all projected local surface freshening at 464 of 595 known sites, but agreement on joint freshening and ice loss at only 384. Spatial correlations between projected ice and salinity changes differed in sign across those models, even after accounting for source region and shared ocean cells. These results demonstrate the ecological importance of distinguishing environmental axes, observational scales, and physical future exposure; they do not establish climate-driven diet switching, individual dispersal, or future breeding abundance.

## Evidence ranking and novelty (avoid overclaiming)

1. **Firmly demonstrated as source measurements / arithmetic**: 588×28 marine two-axis PCA; original fixed-site nest-count reweighting; 6/6 co-survey ratios; physical three-model sign concordance; original future source-model coupling disagreement. The 3-model historical within-region salinity pattern matched GLORYS poorly compared with source sea ice (within region site r_sos 0.204/0.208/0.248 vs r_sic 0.676/0.367/0.610), which limits local attribution but is not future skill validation.
2. **Exploratory positive biological association**: 860 source optical isotope×salinity β=+0.834, partial R²2.88%. Significant under cell cluster but not conservative 13-region t interval. Region-subset beta only +0.080 among 227 records/4 co-census regions; isotope and exact co-census same source site×season overlap 0. Retain as positive environmental association with strong geographic qualifications.
3. **Not established**: iso→fish/krill trophic shift, warming→migration, salinity→nest growth, plant greening/snow→island recruitment, future local extinct/new colony model. Eight repeated colonies/29 co-observed nest/isotope seasons yielded within-site observed +0.134 direction but t(7) interval [−0.149,+0.418]; leaving Victoria Land ≈+0.004. Do not promote this underpowered bridge to a main figure.
4. **Prior-art boundary**: Sea ice–Adélie abundance nonlinear association (Iles et al. 2020), long-term regional species turnover (Trivelpiece et al. 2011; Lynch & LaRue 2014), penguin satellite diet/sea ice/population study (Youngflesh et al. 2026), South Ocean freshening/salinification physical literature (Silvano et al. 2025) predate components of this manuscript. Novel framing is **joint source-checked quantitative alignment of different environmental dimensions and strictly different response domains**, not first discovery that warming affects penguins, salinity affects food webs, or Southern Ocean freshens.

## Publication gates

- Do not call the site-equal within-region future model coupling or the isotope coefficient a **single** climate response pathway. They use different seasons, different predictors and populations.
- The original 265 site×species historical warming-regression β−0.80 [−1.49,−0.41] is not reproducible from the frozen original 2456-count source; exclude from this paper unless its exact publisher/analysis cohort is recovered.
- Physical source risk/exposure metrics are not real population forecasts. Include separate row for original 271 counted sites and lost nesting-weight coverage at 50km ocean model grid gate.
- A main text statement of geographic direction, model uncertainty and the **limits on transfer** is more defensible than a pre-defined claim that climate has no effect.
- **Freeze editorial alternative only.** Do not modify or merge existing original v0.5 source/figures. Candidate main text / figures are a proposal until independent source visualization and review are complete.
