# Before results: one between-site versus within-site polynya comparison

**Frozen at 2026-11-22 JST, before inspecting any new between-site isotope slopes.** Same post-selected observational panel, 2004–2010, 439 original colony-season optical δ15N and direct actual June–October DEEP-AA coastal-polynya area fractions within 100 km. Existing within-site adjusted M_P slope (−1.003109‰ per +10 percentage-point polynya fraction) is known. The new between-site slopes and sign are **not yet inspected**. No use of Reaves data as substitute matched observations.

## Ecological question and three distinct estimands

1. **Within site, original frozen (reference, not independent):** original M_P slope of colony-season δ15N proxy versus winter polynya area fraction, adjusted for colony fixed effect, reporting region×season fixed effect, summer sea-ice concentration, temperature and the two original image-day terms. Region-clustered CV3 (eight informative regions) already reported. Reproduce numerical point estimate from the frozen input without changing its response, lag, radius, unit or covariates.
2. **Between sites, raw/descriptive B0:** collapse to one row per source colony, taking equal means over the *observed* 2004–2010 site-seasons for both Y and P; fit one equal-colony-weight intercept+P regression across all 85 sites, with zero inference of habitat selection. This differs from Reaves chick-bone, which has distinct tissue, dates and polynya proxies.
3. **Between sites, primary region-adjusted B1:** on exactly those 85 site means, fit Y_mean ~ P_mean + source region effects + each site’s means of the original two summer covariates and two image-day terms + recorded mean season-year + source colony latitude. Keep all 85 original site records even if observed for one season. With report-region effects, the comparison is among colonies **within the same reporting region**; no physical interpretation of 10-point manipulation or inferred isotope baseline stability. No novel predictor variable except fixed source latitude and calendar-coverage mean. Equal colony weights.
4. **Design and coverage sensitivities fixed up front:** B0+region FE, and B1 on sites observed in at least three of the seven winters. A separately reported B2 first subtracts **reporting region×season arithmetic means** from each observation of Y, P and the four summer/timing covariates, then collapses the season-standardized residuals to equal-site means and runs the B1-style region+latitude model. This addresses incomplete site calendar support without using optical Y to choose any subset. These are diagnostics, not further independent hypotheses.
5. **Uncertainty and geography:** report all 11 regions and the number with more than one colony; use delete-one-informative-region refit jackknife CV3 on the *fixed* B1 between-site model with t(G−1) reference where G is the number of source regions with actual between-site residual P variance after the full B1 design. Report every omission point estimate and 95% interval. Quantify number of sites, unique years, source regions, latitudinal range, region-level sample dominance; no pixel or season pseudoreplication. Raw and within/ between slopes have different conditioning; do not equate them as an experimental effect decomposition.
6. **Calibration measurement sensitivity:** repeat B0/B1 only with the existing R1,000 mean response from the *same* original source pixel and lab samples; do not treat these as independent observations. No outcome transformations beyond site equal observed-year means.

## Stop/go criterion declared before seeing between-site coefficients

Only describe a **within-panel observed-scale sign reversal** when the *original-response primary B1* between-site slope is **positive**, its regional CV3 interval excludes zero in the positive direction (with no unestimable leave-region-out fit), its sign survives **every informative-region omission**, and its sign is also positive in the predeclared >=3-year and region×season-standardized B2 comparisons. The already known original within-site M_P slope must remain negative. If any condition fails, report that no new supported inversion was demonstrated; do not rescue it by selecting raw B0, a favourable geography, year subset, radius, lag, optical response, different reference df or outcome subset. Even passing these within-panel criteria does not prove biological species/krill diet effects or causality.

Reaves et al. (2025; MEPS 756:127–141, DOI 10.3354/meps14794) compared chick-bone δ15N at 15 Ross Sea colony locations (different bone tissue, ages, locations and polynya type). The publisher abstract/source dataset are evidence for a *spatial context*, **not** a measured positive coefficient on the same annual fraction or identical ecological estimand. Their sign shall never be inserted as a positive result for this dataset.

**Do not change current MEPS manuscript text or figures to claim inversion before these declared gates are met.** No post-result extra radii, new lags, split years, region boundaries, model formulas or significance thresholds; no submit, DOI, merge or licence assignment.
