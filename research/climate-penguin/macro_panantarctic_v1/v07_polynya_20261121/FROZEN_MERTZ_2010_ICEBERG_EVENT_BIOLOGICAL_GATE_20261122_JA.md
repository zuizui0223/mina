# 2010 Mertz–B-09B iceberg event: outcome-blind frozen diagnostic (2026-11-22)

**Freeze before looking up treated/control optical δ15N 2010 outcomes.** External event evidence and physical 2010 polynya exposure were already viewed. The prior broad 439-panel −1.003‰/10pp optical association was known. This same-source event check is neither independent ecological replication nor evidence of randomized polynya exposure.

## Event and time

- **February 2010**: B-09B iceberg collided with the Mertz Glacier Tongue, calving C-28 and changing the George V/Mertz polynya system (Tamura et al. 2012, Nature Communications, DOI 10.1038/ncomms1820). The associated winter is June–October 2010; optical response is November 2010–February 2011.
- No equivalent *pre-event* optical panel exists for Ross Island B-15A/C16: these already changed access in **2001**. Thus do not use 2004–2010 as an exogenous pre-2001 interrupted series.
- Iceberg impact changes more than polynya size (circulation, ice access, light, prey); no valid pure-polynya instrument, causal mediation or unconfounded experiment is claimed.

## Frozen tests and NO-GO gates

1. **Physical shock first stage**: equal-colony weighted D_P = (treated 2010 − treated mean 2006–09) − (controls 2010 − control mean 2006–09), in *percentage points*. Before this freeze the observed physical means were: treated 2006–09 10.2846%, 2010 4.1609%; controls 2006–09 9.7967%, 2010 9.2244%: D_P ≈ −5.55pp.
2. **Biological response primary falsification**: EXACT SAME equal-colony weighted D_Y on original optical δ15N per mil. Given the known broad negative slope and this negative physical change, event **sign concordance would require D_Y positive**. Repeat with original-author R1,000 optical calibration mean, without counting it as independent. Do not use known broad slope to predict numerical D_Y as a causal counterfactual.
3. **Prior-year placebo**: compute each 2006–09 treated-minus-control Y gap relative to the mean of the other three pre-period gaps. Compare their absolute values with 2010 D_Y. Compute identical placebo contrasts for P. If an earlier Y placebo is at least as large, event-specific timing is not stable.
4. **Regional/control-source sensitivity**: compute treated vs controls separately in Wilkes Land and Victoria Land, and with western control band lon 100–135°E versus eastern control band lon 150–180°E. PGEO has no within-region control; keep it in primary, exclude only in the explicitly predeclared within-region estimates. No post-result control selection.
5. **Source observation process**: report changes in number of observed Landsat image days, selected guano-positive pixels and optical Y quality summaries for each group, year, plus unique ocean wet cells/geographical clustering.
6. **Fixed-effects sensitivity only**: site + year fixed effects, treated×2010 slope on balanced 28 sites×5 seasons, optionally add source-original summer sea-ice concentration, temperature, the two timing terms, and predeclared salinity (measured after winter). Do not interpret these post-winter covariates as pretreatment confounders or estimate a total causal effect.
7. **Predeclared uptake**: A *descriptive event-level sign concordance* requires D_P<0; D_Y>0 for both original optical and original-author R1,000; positive outcomes in BOTH region-specific comparisons; and |2010 D_Y| exceeding every 2006–09 placebo. If any fails: NO_GO_EVENT_BIOLOGICAL_RESPONSE. Even if all pass: NO_GO_CAUSAL_POLYNYA_MEDIATION and no claim of future climate or diet prediction.
