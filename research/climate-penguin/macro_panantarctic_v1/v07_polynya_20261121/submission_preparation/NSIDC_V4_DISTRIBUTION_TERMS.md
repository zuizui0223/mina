# G02202 Version 4 source reuse check

Access date: 2026-11-22. Scope: the historical NOAA/NSIDC Climate Data Record of Passive Microwave Sea Ice Concentration, G02202 Version 4, DOI `10.7265/efmz-2t65`. The review package carries derived November–February, 150-km colony-season sea-ice concentration values in joined tables.

**Finding.** NOAA CoastWatch reports “No constraints on data access or use” in the dataset-level metadata of its Southern Hemisphere daily and monthly Version 4 distributions. Both records identify `v04r00` and link to the exact NSIDC Version 4 product page. This establishes a version-specific distributor statement. A named licence or express redistribution term for the original NSIDC release at `10.7265/efmz-2t65` remains unverified. NSIDC requires citation of that release.

**DOI discrepancy.** Both NOAA CoastWatch distributions give `NC_GLOBAL.id` as `10.7265/sr8p-kc62`. The [DOI resolver](https://doi.org/10.7265/sr8p-kc62) and [DataCite lookup](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.7265/sr8p-kc62) returned HTTP 404. The NOAA product title, version and links identify the distributions as G02202 Version 4, but the alternate DOI could not be verified as equivalent to the NSIDC DOI. Keep the canonical citation and attribution to Meier, Fetterer, Windnagel and Stewart (2021), `10.7265/efmz-2t65`, and describe the derived subset. The distributor metadata and DOI discrepancy can accompany that citation in the source register.

Only public documentation and metadata were inspected. The audit establishes no new licence or author authorization.
