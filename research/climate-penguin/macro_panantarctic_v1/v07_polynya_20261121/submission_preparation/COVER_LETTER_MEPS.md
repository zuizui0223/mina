# Cover letter — author-completion draft

Dear Editors,

Please consider our Research Article, “Winter coastal-polynya area is associated with annual variation in Adélie penguin optical isotope proxies”, for Marine Ecology Progress Series.

We reanalyse published satellite guano reflectance to ask whether winter coastal-polynya area near Adélie colonies is associated with the following summer's optical nitrogen-isotope proxy. The panel comprises 439 colony-seasons at 85 sites, with eight informative regional clusters. Conditional on summer conditions, image timing and fixed effects, a ten-percentage-point increase in winter polynya fraction accompanies a −1.003‰ proxy difference (95% CV3 interval: −1.675 to −0.331‰). The estimate remains negative after salinity adjustment. However, the preceding-minus-following-winter contrast is uncertain (−0.688‰; interval: −1.470 to +0.095‰). The negative direction contradicted the original positive hypothesis and is explicitly exploratory; adding exposure did not improve forward prediction. The contribution is a defined winter metric linked to within-colony annual variation in an existing optical record. It does not establish dietary switching or causality. This coastal environmental association and its limits are relevant to MEPS readers studying seasonal food webs and predator indicators.

The data-derived figures and reproducibility materials accompany the manuscript. AI assistance with substantive drafting, code, checks and figure scripts is disclosed for author review.
