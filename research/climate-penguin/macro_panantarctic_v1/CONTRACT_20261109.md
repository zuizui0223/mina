# Pan-Antarctic macroecology: original observed-colony master inventory

**2026-11-09 JST | Working branch `codex/climate-penguin-progress` (PR #206)**

## Why this replaces the 25-site satellite detour

The user wants *macroecology*, not more one-island image QC. The **denominator is the entire MAPPPD original observed colony database**, not the subset already having perfectly matched ice and vegetation. The original published site–species–year records should be the permanent biological atlas; climate and terrestrial products are separate layers to be expanded to it.

## One strict biological grain

Observed count `N[site,species,season,method]`, with observed site and original source region, latitude, longitude and original census year. **No missing site/species/year is changed to zero.** A site observed in 1892 cannot be treated as an absence in 2020. One site code is not necessarily independent island/colony or identical footprint over decades.

Write:
- `source_observed_2456.csv`: original **2456** count rows, no imputation, year/source/method.
- `panantarctic_595_site_inventory.csv`: one row per source site, count of focal species ever documented, observed years, source method/repeated-survey support, static plant-match, historical SIC-match.
- `panantarctic_18_region_inventory.csv`: number of sites by original **18 region labels**, per-species observed regional site coverage, observational effort year distribution.
- `observed_multi_species_same_year.csv`: **303** original site×year rows where at least 2 species have original counts under the selected method, of which **82** site×years at **13** distinct source sites have *all 3* species actually counted. Do not invent absence for a fourth or missing focal species. Count compositions are conditional on which species were surveyed and recorded.
- `macro_original_coverage.json`: fixed all-period and 1991–2020 source overlaps and limitations.

### No fake ecological inference

This is a large **observation inventory**, not randomly sampled sites across a mapped coastal background. It is macro in **geographic coverage** but species non-detections, standardized survey effort, historical season alignment and sea-ice/vegetation at the whole set are not yet measured. The current source climate intersection (40/595) is a **data-linkage bottleneck**, not evidence that only 40 penguin colonies occur.

**Next goal after this first evidence gate:** extend original monthly SIC/snow/temperature layers from their present 40 sites to the fixed full observed-site geographical footprint, retaining native environmental grid-cell identifiers, temporal support and Antarctic sector/region blocks; then estimate region-/distance-block-robust niche associations across species. Plant area is a separate contemporaneous static site context until independent year-specific vegetation is observed. The 2017–2023 static map must never be cloned into the 1892–2025 count series.

Stop using individual Landsat pixels to manufacture a multi-taxon macro sample; the correct replicated units are **regions, separately sampled colony site codes, original survey years and environmental grid cells**, not 77 polar-grid QA points.

**Prior-art relevance:** within-site and region-demeaned climate responses previously weakened, so a pooled large sample p value alone does not establish a continent-wide law. A truly new macro question should be *which scale and regions do observed warming/ice/land environments carry species-specific niche information, and when does that association fail to transfer*.

Keep PR #211 (GPS Circuitscape), #212 (Ross resight), #213 (small observed-count GAM and Landsat) separately as methodological/support lanes, not additional required manuscript endpoints.
