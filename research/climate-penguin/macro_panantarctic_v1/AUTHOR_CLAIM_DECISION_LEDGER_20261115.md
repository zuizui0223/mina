## New 27-interval source-year step audit

The original source-verified 588 colony×28y climate data were aggregated once per **378 true GLORYS wet cells** and tested across **every year-to-year change from 1994 to 2020**. The **2018→2019** change in the median original-cell salinity was **−0.42344**, the most negative of 27 intervals; **346/378 cells** declined and **188/378** had a change more than two historical 1994–2018 within-cell annual-difference SD below expectation. In 2020, the corresponding figures were −0.20505, 298/378 and 54/378. A regional sign alone is not unique to 2019: 2010 also showed **18/18 reporting regions** with a negative median cell step, but only 28/378 cells below −2SD and median step −0.20486. Thus the identifying property is the **exceptional magnitude of the 2019 source step**, not a novel common negative direction. [Full 27-step QA and independent recomputation](GLORYS_2019_YEAR_ON_YEAR_STEP_GATE_JA_20261115.md). This is *source-only temporal evidence*, not validated in-situ freshening or a proven ERA5 forcing artifact.

## Verified observed-colony spatial allocation sensitivity (2026-11-15)

- New *source-observed nested cohort* comparison: fixed three-decade colony roster **43 colony×species records**, including Adélie **19 sites across four source regions/six 250-km blocks**. Across 1990s→2010s, the sum of actual colonywise median observed Adélie nests rose **288,940→515,713.5 (+78.48%)**, while Hill q=2 effective colony sites fell **5.246→3.674 (−29.98%)**. This is an example of growing monitored source count totals with greater relative concentration in a fixed roster, not population-wide growth or a causal climate effect.
- The equally source-defined *endpoint-only strict* cohort includes **26 Adélie sites** and reverses the concentration inference: Hill q=2 **3.618→3.913 (+8.15%)**, with median-count sum +58.24%. The older original endpoint cohort with ≥1 observation per decade (35 sites) likewise yields **5.459→5.918 (+8.40%)**. The seven sites omitted from the 19 complete-decade group include the exceptionally large ADAR, with observed 1990s/2010s median nests of 318,110/445,440. **Missing intermediate-decade source support is not a recorded zero population.**
- The 19-site concentration decline reverses when the largest site CRZW is omitted (+8.64% remaining cohort); the 26-site increase reverses when ADAR is omitted (−31.43%). Even diversity order matters: for 26 sites, Hill q=1 is **6.736→6.648 (−1.30%)** while Hill q=2 increases. No universal Antarctic nesting concentration trend, guaranteed consequence of declining abundance, or shared climate mechanism is identified.
- A different 12-site chinstrap cohort showed sitewise median nest sum **−43.90%** but Hill q=2 **+14.04%**, while a separate 12-site gentoo cohort showed sum **+69.60%** and Hill q=2 **+3.88%**. Different source region support means **not** three spatially matched species response replicates.

## Geographic reweighting survives source-roster changes (2026-11-15)

**YES (observed-source geographical allocation, not distribution movement).** Within the original same **1991–2000 to 2011–2020** observation window, Adélie nesting-median-weighted latitude becomes more southerly under **19 (three-decade complete), 26 (strict endpoints), and 35 (weaker endpoints)** known-colony rosters: **+107.310/+88.411/+99.603 km**, respectively. All one-site, one-original-region and one-250km-block omissions retain the southward direction, including 26 strict original colonies +65.609 to +102.815 km when deleting one entire original reporting region. The size-dominance reduction to sqrt-count weights retains +87.484/+80.515/+81.878 km.

**NO (one universal concentration or sea-ice-niche-tracking mechanism).** Original Hill q=2 colony-effective number change switches **−29.98% (19 sites)** to **+8.15% (26)**; 19/26 original fixed-source nesting-weighted original nearshore SIC changes are just **+0.022/+0.625 pp**. Source region-between and within-region contributions differ with site inclusion. The 26-site source cohort is dominated by Victoria Land (19 site IDs, 16 with increased medians), while the six observed central-west Peninsula/South Shetlands Adélie sites all decline. Observation-cohort geography is not a biological absence, full Antarctic population tally, or proof of climate causal forcing.

## Author-facing decision

The supported unifying claim is that Antarctic coastal marine data span at least two distinguishable statistical physical dimensions, but the geography and time support of repeated breeding censuses are too narrow to establish a transferable climate response across Antarctic penguin communities. Local species-composition changes and monitored nesting totals are not zero-sum equivalents. The exceptional full-period salinity synchrony stays explicitly qualified by 2019–2020 reanalysis source-year sensitivity.

Keep one manuscript and four main figures. No new post-hoc p-value search, biological future forecast or climate forcing causal claim. Original main, Ecology Report and frozen Paper1/Paper2 are not changed.
