# Antarctic 18-region marine synchrony and unique-ocean-cell source test

**2026-11-12 JST | exploratory post-result source diagnostic in Draft PR #214 | no merge of frozen paper/main.**

## Single question

The 588 colony/18 region original 1993–2020 physically verified dataset showed PC1 thermal–sea-ice (60.365% of 3-variable spatial/temporal pooled data) and PC2 predominantly salinity (33.377%), with a PC1 decade-mean peak in 2001–2010 in 13 of 18 original regions. Is the apparent *shared 2000s timing* a genuinely common regional temporal mode, or due to colony sampling weights, duplicated GLORYS coastal cells and asynchronous regional histories?

This is a source-aware temporal coherence description, **not** a new test that anthropogenic warming caused a common peak, not a climate mechanism and not a colony demographic forecast. Previous 18-region results were observed when choosing this analysis; no independent preregistration.

## Frozen inputs / QA

- Reuse original successful GitHub Actions #42961084667, artifact 13829236358: 16,464 true original source 588 colony×28-year (1993–2020) complete 8–11-month SIC, GLORYS salinity/thetao with fixed native marine grid identities; 504 original source-region×year PC1 and variable means; 18 original region time trend outputs.
- Reconstruct original 3-variable standardized PCA from the full 16,464 3-dimensional physical values, with thermal–ice PC1 temp-positive and salinity PC2 salinity-positive, and verify each archived individual original PC1 score to <=1e−8; verify original source PC1 loadings/variances against reported previous result and original 18-region×28-year PC1 site-equal means <=1e−8. **Do not fit alternative physical axes based on temporal outcomes or biology.**
- Keep all 18 reporting regions AND a fixed geographic source-supported subset of **>=3 unique original breeding sites AND >=2 original 250km blocks AND >=2 distinct original GLORYS wet-grid cells per region** (expected 13 regions from previous source). Report both; no postresult choice of region/subset.
- Compute region×year PC1/PC2 in two observation-weighting regimes: (a) equal weight for each original observed known colony site in region (legacy), and (b) equal weight for each **unique original GLORYS wet-grid cell within region/year**, first averaging its source colony SIC/thermal+salinity scores (so duplicate marine source cells do not gain extra vote). Even in (b) some grid cells can be present in multiple source-region labels; retain and report cross-region overlap. No change to cohort site/year eligibility.

## Temporal coherence metrics

For each original region, subtract its entire 1993–2020 mean from annual PC scores and divide by its actual original 28-year temporal SD; region/year is one equal-weight column. Do **not** recenter each region separately per decade. For both axes, both source weightings, and both region sets, compute:

1. The year-common score across regions and the **fraction of total region-standardized annual anomaly sum of squares explained by the simple common year mean**. This is an equal-direction shared fluctuation descriptor, not a fitted climate-forcing coefficient.
2. Full 28-year×R region standardized year anomaly EOF/PCA with region columns; fraction of anomaly variance in EOF1, signed EOF loadings oriented so their mean is positive if nonzero, and count of positive/negative loadings. An EOF may contain anti-synchronous regions; EOF1 is **not** automatically an all-region coherent response.
3. Off-diagonal pairwise region anomaly correlations (median, first/third quartile, positive/negative count); pairwise regions are spatially correlated and are not independent n=R(R-1)/2 observations.
4. A reproducible **phase-offset diagnostic**: 1,000 independent circular rotations of each regional standardized real 28-year anomaly (fixed RNG seed 20261112). This preserves each region's 28-year values and cyclic order (within-series shape) but destroys true alignment of calendar years. Compare the observed common-year fraction and EOF1 fraction to the phase-offset median, 2.5–97.5% envelope, and fraction of offsets >= observed. **These are descriptive source phase-reference scores, not inferential p-values, climate null hypotheses, or established statistical significance**, because natural Southern Ocean climate autocorrelation and teleconnection structure are not completely represented by artificial periodic rotation.
5. Count 1993–2000 vs 2001–2010 vs 2011–2020 fixed original PC1/PC2 regional mean peaks (middle greater than both endpoints), for site-equal and ocean-cell-equal, and explicitly list any region whose classification changes. The 1993–2000 period has only 8 years versus the later two 10-year periods.
6. Report original regional physical PC1–salinity PC2 *within-region annual* correlation, as well as correlation of their cross-region common-year means, showing that globally orthogonal PCA spatial axes need not be temporally independent.
7. Report all-18 vs source-supported 13-region results side by side. If the middle peak or the common mode collapses under ocean-cell weighting / source eligibility, state the original 13/18 result was geography-weight dependent. If stable, call it broad *observed marine-time alignment*, never identical environmental change rate or causal warming.

**Required output**: per-region/year observed original fixed-PC1/PC2 scores under both weightings, exact 18-region middle peak audit, 4-pair (PC1/PC2 × all18/source-supported13) synchrony summaries including fixed-seed source phase references, full source receipt and honest inference boundaries. No species response coefficients, static vegetation years, alternative lag/threshold searches or new papers in this step.


## Explicit post-observed-result temporal-trend sensitivity (NOT retrospective frozen hypothesis)

After the first source computation reported ~52% cross-region salinity-PC2 common-year fraction and ~20% thermal–ice-PC1 fraction (all 18 regions), an additional essential **interpretability** check was appended. Mean-centering each region removes its physical geographic baseline, but **does not remove its 1993–2020 linear temporal trend**. A common salinity long-term trend can thus create apparent year-by-year synchronization even when short-term residual variations differ. For the SAME source PC scores, same sites, same 18/13 region definitions and same original marine cell weightings, remove each region's linear least-squares 1993–2020 trend separately (intercept and slope) BEFORE standardizing its 28 residual annual values. Recompute common-year variance fraction, EOF1 variance, positive/negative EOF1 region loadings, regional pair correlations, and 1,000 fixed phase-offset envelopes. This extra test is explicitly post-result, not an outcome-independent preregistered analysis. Report both mean-only and detrended values side by side; **do not replace the primary estimand with whichever version is larger**. If salinity coherence weakens after detrending, attribute part of the apparent synchronization to co-trending rather than independent interannual anomalies. Detected trend structure is not automatically attributable to greenhouse gases, surface freshwater or assimilation changes.
