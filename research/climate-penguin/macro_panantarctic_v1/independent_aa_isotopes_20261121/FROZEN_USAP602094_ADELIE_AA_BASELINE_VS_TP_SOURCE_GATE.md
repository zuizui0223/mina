# Independent amino-acid stable isotope Adélie source gate — frozen before source Y opened

**Frozen on 2026-11-21 JST** in Draft PR #214. Discovery: Michelson et al. 2026, `USAP-DC dataset DOI:10.15784/602094`, public 2026-09, *bulk and amino-acid δ15N in modern 2000–2016 and historical 1841–1995 Adélie penguin feathers from Ross Sea and Antarctic Peninsula*.

**Question:** Does an association with the local winter polynya environment, if time-supported by actual feather formation year/season, correspond primarily to **baseline-corrected trophic position (TP)**, **source-AA phenylalanine δ15N baseline**, or only **bulk δ15N**? The earlier negative coastal polynya association applies to summer guano-derived *optical* δ15N, and is exploratory and a **different tissue and possible time window**. No repeated tests of radii, lags, or sites chosen by outcome.

## Explicit source-before-response gates

1. Download **unmodified USAP-DC record 602094 raw CSV** `Michelson_2026_Adelie_Feathers_SIA_CSIA_Data.csv` via official public [datafiles API](https://www.usap-dc.org/api/v2.0/doc) GET `/datafiles/602094/{filename}`. Publisher MD5 `f9a91c9d9bf3dba9607bf0767df8c206`; reject HTML/auth/redirect substitutes and any other content. Log original source SHA256 and column schema.
2. Inventory **without analyzing isotope outcome values**: sample IDs, sample type, location, lat/lon, known collection date, **true year of feather synthesis/moult** (not just museum accession date), "modern" vs "historical", AA source and trophic metric field availability. All feather samples from a given site and year form a **single ecological unit**, not independent birds.
3. **Time-origin gate:** only winters 2004–2010 or 2013–2016 with known provenance and a **biologically defensible unique prior winter relative to the sampled feather** can be combined with DEEP-AA prior 6–10-month 100km polynya; if sampling date is not feather-synthesis time and no unique mapping, **do not fit**. Seasonal year cannot be fabricated from historical vs modern period labels.
4. **Spatial/sampling gate:** at least 20 independent **site×true feather synthesis years**, at least **5 actual sites**, at least **3 nonoverlapping ecological marine regions**, and **≥2 years per 3 sites**, with adequate original daily DEEP-AA source coverage and no coordinates extrapolated from broad region. Otherwise status **NO_GO_INDEPENDENT_TP_POLYNYA_IDENTIFICATION**. This is conservative and protects a pan-Antarctic claim; not a test of absent ecological relationship.
5. If both gates pass, fixed responses: published **AA baseline-corrected TP** (primary), **phenylalanine δ15N** (baseline channel), and **bulk feather δ15N** (secondary); same complete site×season sample and true metadata. If the published data do not contain all three, do not invent a phenylalanine label or impute TP. Effects estimated as site-level annual deviations from site means (FE) with source region as cluster. If only spatial contrasts, report those as *descriptive* and do not interpret time-varying polynya effects. Primary exposure 100km winter Jun–Oct native DEEP-AA coastal polynya fraction with correct marine mask; no radius or season tuning to obtain a sign. If fewer than 6 informative regions, no whole-Antarctic regional-precision inference.
6. If measured AA TP does not vary in a direction that confirms guano optical δ15N, this does **not** refute earlier guano finding because tissue, isotope correction, period and colony may differ. Equally if it varies similarly, it is not immediate independent confirmation without same site×true exposure support.

**Source-only outcome firewall:** first commit the downloaded CSV metadata audit with source fingerprint, year/site/season column assessment, matched original source region names if any, and precise exclusions. Do not calculate or correlate isotope, TP, nitrate, krill fraction, or polynya slope until numbered gates are independently checked. No time-window or region substitutions after reading the label values. `main`, frozen v0.5/v0.6, Ecology Report and Paper2 remain untouched.
