# Two physical marine axes vs genuinely co-observed penguin breeding-count composition: exploratory source contract

2026-11-11 JST, continuing the **same single Antarctic macroecology paper** in Draft PR #214, no main/frozen Paper1/Paper2 edits. Source cases and endpoints already observed, so this is *post-result exploratory*, not a prospective preregistration or a new confirmatory test.

## Central, falsifiable question
The original 18-region physical PCA (1993–2020, 588 known colonies, 16,464 site×year source-complete rows) identified **PC1 warm/low-ice** (60.365%, -0.707 SIC / -0.038 salinity / +0.706 thetao) and **PC2 mostly salinity** (33.377%, +0.998 salinity). Does either independent physical coordinate describe the observed **within-colony** change in the **same-site, same-year counted** relative number of nests between two penguin species, or is this relationship chiefly a between-colony/geographic association?

- Additional source-temporal guard: correlations from all years are subject to common climate year effects (ENSO/SAM) and unbalanced survey effort; no p-value testing, no Gaussian CIs based on pseudoreplicate siteyears, no post-hoc optimizing lags/thresholds or expanding biological cohorts outside the source exact co-census.

GLORYS12V1 salinity/thetao are an assimilative ocean reanalysis; OSI SAF SIC is a publisher-verified satellite product, both source local and spatially smoothed.
- Scientific transfer gate: a claimed transferable response would need >2 independent original reporting regions and >2 polar250km blocks of repeatedly dual-counted pairs, consistent sign of the two-PC within-site coefficient under all region/block deletions, and an independent held-out era/geographic replicate. The present dataset's support may fail; report NO_GO honestly.
