# Original EUMETSAT 60%-SIC marine-front meridional diagnostic (2026-11-10, before new physical output)

This new exploratory analysis is part of Draft PR #214. Original data and all choices below were frozen before examining full-grid front outputs. It is not a change to mina frozen Paper 1/2.

## Estimand
Quantify the geographic **meridional** displacement of the 60% marine pack-ice concentration isoline from gridded EUMETSAT observations. This is **not** coastline-following climate velocity and cannot estimate true range-edge, dispersal, ecological climatic debt, or biological habitat suitability.

## Source and prechosen extraction
1. Original EUMETSAT OSI SAF OSI-450-a1 v3.1 monthly 25-km Southern EASE2 NetCDF, DOI 10.15770/EUM_SAF_OSI_0023, from publisher thredds.met.no. All 120 August–November monthly files for 1991–2020, source URL/SHA256 and native geolocation identity recorded.
2. Source-pinned Sept 1993 valid ocean pixel mask from source status_flag (bitwise 3 equals 0) and 0–100 original SIC. Each month's marine pixel also requires current month's status flag and physically valid SIC; missing is not zero.
3. Primary climatologies: early 1991–2000, intermediate 2001–2010, late 2011–2020. Within each decade compute Aug–Nov seasonal mean per fixed ocean cell, requiring at least 36 of 40 original monthly values. For early–late compare only paired pixels passing that same requirement. A fixed September-only sensitivity requires 9 of 10 annual monthly values within each decade. No new lag, month or thresholds chosen from results.
4. Threshold: **60% SIC fixed as a physical contour only**, never a biologically calibrated risk boundary. Extract genuine interpolated original EASE2 marine SIC 60% contour with skimage.measure.find_contours and a paired valid mask. Only a geographically continuous candidate with >=100 vertices AND coverage of >=24 of 36 fixed 10-degree longitude sectors is called a circumpolar front. No such front → source geometry not identified.
5. Sector latitudes are the median interpolated latitude of the chosen marine contour in each of 36 sectors fixed to [-180,-170), ..., [170,180). Longitude interpolation uses circular sin/cos to avoid discontinuities. Require >=3 contour vertices per sector and decade. Positive 111.195*(latitude_early-latitude_late) km means southward/poleward meridional displacement. Report sectors with valid endpoints, sign counts, median, spread, and source coverage; sectors are spatially autocorrelated, not independent experiments.

## Original source cross-check and biology
6. Compare original Sept 1993 100-km colony ocean-pixel means against already verified original 595-site source monthly means using exact source native pixel IDs, accepting only absolute mismatch <=0.0002 pp. Both map geometry and source arrays must be identical. Without a pass, no physical/biological join is allowed.
7. Biological companion is the original same-colony 1991–2000 vs 2011–2020 pair recorded in PR #214, with >=2 real count observations in each endpoint decade per site/species. Original support is Adélie 26, chinstrap 12, gentoo 12, but actual ice-edge support is to be audited rather than assumed. Join by source longitude to supported 10-degree marine ice sectors. Within supported sectors only, recompute each species' source-count-weighted colony latitude displacement and, separately, baseline-count-weighted physical marine-front meridional displacement. For an interpreted comparison require >=3 supported sites, >=2 sectors, >=2 source regions; else no support.
8. Comparing these dissimilar geographic quantities yields a **descriptive meridional contrast** only: not along-coast tracking, climate debt, individual migration, settlement, or causal warming. Source survey effort and positive-colony-only sampling prevent estimation of true distribution edges. Report area/season biases and source-region dependence, including Ross vs Antarctic Peninsula opposing trends. Do not calibrate future SSP breeding counts from this diagnostic.

## Required final verdict
Source coverage and 60%-contour identifiability; regional nonuniform ice-front movement; species-overlap sample sizes; appropriately restricted descriptive comparison. A genuine **coastline-parallel** climatic velocity needs independent Antarctic coastline transects / SCAR ADD and a 2D ecological accessibility model, so remain NO-GO for a headline climate-debt ratio.
