# Marine exposure of observed Adelie nest redistribution

Fixed before computing new exposure contrasts, 2026-11-20.

Question: does southward reweighting of observed nests preserve sea-ice exposure but change salinity and coastal-polynya exposure? This is a descriptive decomposition, not a causal test of migration or diet.

Primary comparison: 1993–2000 versus 2011–2020, because GLORYS begins in 1993. Secondary three-environment comparison: 2004–2010 versus 2013–2020, because DEEP-AA winter coverage excludes 2003, 2011 and 2012. These estimate different historical changes and will not be pooled.

Use the existing method-selected observed census source, ADPE only, at least two observed census seasons per site per window, and a fixed site roster across windows. Recheck uniqueness and method consistency. Census summaries are medians of observed counts, not synchronous population totals. Unrecorded counts are never zero. Environment is the equal-year mean across each complete window: Aug–Nov monthly SIC, GLORYS surface salinity and temperature; Jun–Oct DEEP-AA coastal-polynya fraction within 100 km. Every environmental year must be available, with all months passing coverage; no temporal interpolation. 25 km polynya is a spatial sensitivity. Save all exclusions/support and actual census years.

For normalized count weights w and environment E, total change is sum(w1 E1-w0 E0); local-environment component is sum((w0+w1)/2 * (E1-E0)); redistribution component is sum((w1-w0)*(E0+E1)/2). These sum exactly. The symmetric definition splits the interaction equally. Report physical units, both components, latitude shift, and omissions by site, reporting region and shared GLORYS cell. Omission ranges are sensitivity ranges, not confidence intervals. Reproduce published 19/26/35-site geographical results separately; do not reuse their 1991–2000 census medians as matched 1993–2000 observations.

No significance search, environmental index selection, biological forecast, or claim that isotope associations identify a diet mechanism. If the environmental components have opposite signs, describe compensation in the sampled roster; robustness requires reporting region omissions. Counts can reflect recruitment, survival, attendance or redistribution and do not track individuals.
