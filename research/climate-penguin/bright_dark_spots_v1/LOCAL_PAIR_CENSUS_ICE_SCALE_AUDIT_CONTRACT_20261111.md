# Original 25km ocean-cell spatial-overlap and local-colony contrast audit

**2026-11-11: postselection diagnostic, not a prospective test.** Main question still ONE: where do observed Antarctic colony count changes diverge relative to peers? Investigate the broad source-supported original 147 same-species, same-region, same-fixed-250km-block pairs that share >=5 original census years over >=10 years. This is not a new independent paper or marine-front causal model.

## Why needed

Earlier successfully reconstructed original source results: 100 site×species long count series (1991–2020), 90 region-comparable, 25 trend/survey-density robust, 9 common-year-sign-supported, 2 with external blocks. An all-pairs source-matched local SIC audit identified 147 eligible local same-block pairs, 131 <=100km separation, all 147 with valid same-year 100km SIC means; among original two strongest opposite-sign candidate sites one pair Cape Crozier west (CRZW) vs Cape Royds (ROYD), 74.79km apart, 25 identical aerial count seasons 1991–2019, original SIC mean 81.07% and 82.81%, mean absolute SIC difference 2.247pp, population log1p slopes +0.44593 and −0.05169/decade.

**Important pre-existing literature:** Lyver et al. (2014) PLoS ONE DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0091188 and Ainley et al. (2024) Antarctic Science DOI (verify from original source) describe Cape Royds vs Crozier/Bird contrasting response to mega-icebergs B15A/C16, McMurdo Sound polynya and ocean access. Thus the original source pair is NOT a newly discovered ecological mechanism; it can serve as a prior documented mechanism benchmark of a broader database search.
