# Antarctic penguin observed-colony bright/dark spot discovery v1

**Exploratory, outcome-blind implementation specification dated 2026-11-11.** This is an original-data descriptive follow-on to the 595-site source inventory and 100 long-term nest+ice time series in climate PR #206; it is not an independently preregistered ecological discovery. This branch makes no changes to Paper 1/2, main, or the frozen 2026-11-10 macro paper draft.

## Single focused question
Which already-monitored penguin colonies have source-method-consistent observed nest-count trends well above ("bright candidate") or below ("dark candidate") other sites of the **same species in the same original reporting region**? Is the discrepancy robust to repeated observation-year and estimation-method choices? Such contrasts are leads for external mechanistic work, **not yet conservation refuges or localized survival-rate effects**.

## Fixed provenance
- Reconstruct full 595-site / 18-region / 2,456 observed census site×species×year source (same method per site-species), using frozen original Git blobs and existing macro_panantarctic_v1/build.py.
- Reuse the independent pre-existing original 100-site×species 1991–2020 trend CSV from SUCCESS #42613076334 (artifact 13670229768), fitted on the 1991–2020 subset with >=5 true census years spanning >=10 years: Adelie 37, chinstrap 27, gentoo 36, 77 distinct original colony sites in 7 regions. Exact original source trend must be re-estimated from the 2,456-year census table and match to <=1e-8 log1p nests per decade. If the source drifted, STOP.
- Unrecorded census years and species are **missing, never zero**; original verified count zero stays zero. This is **observed nest count**, not demographic growth/fitness or colony extinction.
- Original sea-ice per-site trend remains contextual only, NOT fit as a causal predictor or p-value-optimized regressor. The published 595 original site cohort remains geographic denominator, not a fabricated 595-site longitudinal count cohort.

## Comparability and residual estimand
- Within each species×source-region group, a site has a region reference only if there are **at least five distinct site IDs in the group including focal site** and **at least two different source EPSG3031 250-km blocks in the group**. Others stay in full results with STATUS=NO_COMPARABLE_REGIONAL_PEERS; do not compare to another region or species as fallback.
- Site i observed response y_i = linear OLS time slope per 10 years of log(1 + original nest count) computed only at actual surveyed years 1991–2020 (existing frozen definition).
- Leave-one-site-out robust regional expectation \hat y_-i = median(y_j) of the **other sites in same species and region**. Relative observed performance residual B_i = y_i - \hat y_-i. Positive B_i = relative bright; negative = relative dark. Report ratio exp(B_i) for intuitive relative 1+nests decadal change **only as an arithmetic transform**, not a population survival multiplier or a predicted true growth rate.
- No within-source species pooling, extrapolation to all Antarctica or 2-SD significance cutoff; baseline use is explicitly descriptive, not a calibrated fit to climate or a population-level expected counterfactual. Rank the B_i raw signed values, but do not call every positive site a biologically exceptional refuge.
- A stricter **robust-source candidate** requires at least ten original census seasons spanning at least twenty years, AND unchanged residual sign under (i) a source-only Theil–Sen estimate computed on all original observed years versus leave-one-site-out median peer Theil–Sen slopes, (ii) removing earliest source census year, and (iii) removing latest source census year, with regional OLS reference baseline held constant; require each reduced time series retains >=5 actual years spanning >=10 years. If any diagnostic not computable, mark support incomplete, do not call confirmed.
- For comparability/interpretation show first/last original observed seasons, distinct census year count, counts at earliest/latest observed years (they are not endpoint decade population levels), method, original region and 250km block, and original site name. Preserve verified site ID.
- To avoid declaring same local block independent: for each suggested candidate, show number of peer sites and source 250km blocks, plus leave-one-spatial-block-out median baseline residual when at least 2 non-focal peer blocks remain. Missing independent blocks are not zero residual.
- Output all 100 sites, region×species support table, two ranked lists (eligible all and strict robust), and exact JSON with source checks. No p-values, no threshold tuning, no claim of true absence/colonization/causal refuge.
- A post-result map or geographic grouping is interpretive only; no second hypothesis/p-value search, no redefinition of the selected cohort.

## Expected biological scope
A "bright" colony can be declining absolutely but be less negative than its own regional peers. A "dark" site can be increasing absolutely but less than its own peers. This is why the response MUST include observed signed slopes and residuals; do not mislabel the signs. Multi-species records at the same site share survey processes and are not three independent site experiments.

## Cinner precedent, not an identical method
Cinner et al. 2016 Nature, DOI 10.1038/nature18607, used a richer hierarchical expectation for >2,500 coral reefs. Here the source support is far smaller and baseline only species+reporting region. Thus the study discovers **relative performance leads for investigation**, not Cinner-like causally explained conservation refugia.
