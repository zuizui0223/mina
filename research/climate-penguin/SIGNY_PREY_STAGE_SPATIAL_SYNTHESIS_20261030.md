# Signy島：雛生産・食性・繁殖空間の同時観測と将来の推定可能範囲

**2026-10-30 Source-backed exploratory synthesis.** Draft PR #206. No frozen paper modified. This is source-data reanalysis and measurement audit, NOT a calibrated future breeding population forecast.

## Empirically completed

1. **Three species / reproductive stage:** original BAS 2021 publisher CSVs, source SHA verified for Adélie / chinstrap / gentoo; **463 original site×species×season rows**, of which **399 have valid source pairs/chick stages**, **392 have native *reported pre-fledging* fledgling count/pairs with documented count-order** (Adélie147, Chinstrap223, Gentoo22). **22 common years** (1996, 1998–2009, 2011–2019); one original corrupt Gentoo label \`1998-8895\` quarantined without invented correction. See \`signy_trispecies_stage_source_v1\`.
2. **Three species / annual change synchrony:** source-colony annual medians for Adélie and chinstrap; published island-aggregate series for gentoo. Difference only between *consecutive* years: **19 shared differences**, with r(Δrecorded fledglings/pair) **Adélie×Chinstrap +0.7944**, **Adélie×Gentoo +0.7026**, **Chinstrap×Gentoo +0.7382**. Same direction all three in **12/19** differences. Largest-shock-year removal (2012, 2013, 2015, 2016) drops the 3 r values to **+0.6990, +0.5718, +0.3495**; weighted-sum alternative **+0.6701, +0.5901, +0.8130**. Exact phase diagnostic (11 and 8 contiguous differences, 7744 two-block relative placements) returns observed mean r=+0.7451 versus phase 2.5–97.5% [-0.3038,+0.4628], empirical tail 1/7744. This is **post-exposure temporal association**, with no independent ocean-climate field effect or causal p-value. [source-backed v2 CI](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina/actions/runs/41056721425) SUCCESS, 5 tests.
3. **Prior publication / novelty ceiling:** Dunn et al. (2016), *PLoS ONE* 11:e0164025, DOI [10.1371/journal.pone.0164025](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0164025), already reported same Signy chinstrap×gentoo *annual-level breeding success* correlation **r=0.73** and divergent long-term population trajectories. We must NOT market "same-island success synchrony despite different population trajectories" as a new ecological discovery. This analysis adds first-difference, 3-species, source-schema and longer observation-window auditing of previously available observations, NOT independent reproduction data.
4. **Diet / food-intermediate layer:** original BAS summary CSVs, Adelie639, Chinstrap653, Gentoo74 entries. One original sample = one stomach-lavage record, NOT one independent ocean environmental replicate. Source verified usable krill fraction per stomach: **Adélie636**, **Chinstrap653**, **Gentoo73**. These reduce to **45 matched species-years** with native fledgling stage (18/18/9). Three-species same-year diet+stage overlap is **2001–2009**, 9 seasons only. Mean equal-stomach-sample krill fraction across matched years: Adélie **0.9492**, Chinstrap **0.9865**, Gentoo **0.6223**; the first two are near saturation, so ratio is a weak proxy for changes in ambient krill biomass. Original diet source ZIP used published \`TOTAL_MASS_g\` and \`EUPHAUSIA_SUPERBA_MASS_g\` with actual units g. [source coupled CI](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mina/actions/runs/41057594354) SUCCESS, 4 tests.
5. **Diet–fledgling descriptive correlations:** Same-season stomach krill fraction vs source reported fledglings/pair r is Adélie **+0.1504** (18 species-years), Chinstrap **+0.1742** (18), Gentoo **−0.1655** (9). For 16/16/8 consecutive year differences the r values are **+0.3815, −0.0680, −0.3396**. These are posthoc source correlations, not significant effects. The fish/krill stomach fraction is **realized consumed diet**, not independent krill stock biomass, foraging availability, causal salinity response or colony-wide prey supply. Critically all diet sampling strictly **precedes all source fledgling counts** only in 2, 6, and 4 species-years, respectively. 16/12/0 species-years have mixed/uncertain within-season chronology; Gentoo remaining 5 years sampled entirely after stage counts. Thus there is insufficient **pre-outcome** evidence to fit a valid diet-driven fledgling forecast; same-season association cannot be a causal mediator.
6. **Overlap with breeding-space state:** Original \`research/climate-penguin/outputs/signy_exploration/annual_metrics.csv\` (Git blob \`730a68878201838e194d951210ef539cc5712248\`) gives fixed-roster spatial effective component number for 2012–2019. Match source annual-stage medians to that **8-year window** for Adélie and Chinstrap only. Their annual simultaneous Pearson r(stage, N_eff) are **−0.0755 / −0.5143**; Pearson r(stage_t, N_eff_{t+1}−N_eff_t) for **seven year transitions** are **+0.1040 / +0.2254**. These are tiny single-island retrospective opportunities, heterogeneous source colony supports vs frozen N_eff rosters, no error calibration; cannot be taken as real climate-responsive redistribution or a transfer coefficient. Gentoo colony-level N_eff is not available in this historical panel.

## Main integrated interpretation

**Regional shared annual breeding productivity signals can coexist with local, species-specific demographic/spatial organization, but the observed synchrony is not itself new and does not identify a common ice/salinity/krill causal mechanism.** For a proper climate-to-future-breeding model, distinguish:

\`CMIP6 ice/temp/salinity → observed ocean prey availability (not yet measured here) → diet/meal delivery and chick-stage output → recruitment/survival → total nest pairs → spatial allocation N_eff and patch occupancy → local vegetation feedback\`.

The actual scientific gates stop at the following points:

- Environmental forcing is CMIP6 conditional scenarios, spatially coarse, models disagree about salinity; not pre-calibrated nesting microclimate.
- Prey inputs are *predator-derived gut contents*, not direct krill biomass; source field response to local ice/salinity remains untested.
- Only 2/6/4 diet species-years have strict temporal precedence over fledgling counts, and many species-stage outcomes share field survey support.
- Common Signy productivity is 1-island population-level temporal structure (22 seasons), not 22 independent geographic replicates or proof of prey mediation.
- Signy tracked colony rosters and source diets differ in grain and there are only seven transitions for N_eff linking; previous independent Signy lag2 performance-redistribution primary test did **not** replicate (p=0.279 in frozen source), so retrospective positives cannot rescue causal transfer.
- Neither climate→future nest abundance, habitat/vegetation time-change, colonization/recolonization, nor age-specific recruitment has passed external-region/time validation. **Do not give a percent decline in 2050/2100 nest abundance or vegetation.**
