# Palmer–Signy external diet comparison: analysis specification

2026-11-06. Exploratory external-region comparison following the existing Signy result; this is not a preregistration. Written before calculating the Palmer ice–diet association in this extension. The published Palmer food percentages and the existing Signy result have already been seen.

1. **Question.** Does the previously observed positive relationship between antecedent sea ice and gentoo–Adélie dietary separation also occur in an external region, and can the Signy relation predict the level of that separation there?
2. **Units and cohorts.** Signy: the existing 2001/02–2009/10 nine-season cohort, using only the gentoo–Adélie pair. Palmer: the six seasons 2009/10–2014/15 in Pickett et al. (2018), Table 1, DOI 10.1002/ece3.4445. The year is the season's starting calendar year. These are two regions, not 15 independent islands; 2009 is shared in calendar time. Palmer species originate primarily from different colonies, so species and colony contrasts remain confounded.
3. **Food response.** Primary: absolute difference between species' equal-stomach mean fish fractions among recovered food. Reconstruct Signy food mass from fish + crustaceans + cephalopods + other food, without adding krill subcategories again or including non-food. Retain the existing source quality mask and disclose missing food components; never replace missing with zero. Palmer uses the published means of per-stomach wet-mass percentages, divided by 100. Do not calculate a ratio of annual means and call it a mean of individual ratios. Retain Signy F/TOTAL_MASS and the legacy mean F/(F+C) as denominator checks; these do not replace the frozen three-species endpoint.
4. **Exposure.** The existing hash-pinned OSI SAF monthly dataset, 0–100 km band, all four complete August–November months equally weighted. Signy uses GOPT. Palmer primary uses equal weights for BISC and LITC proxies; show each proxy separately as a geographic-support check, not independent climate replication. Require unique site × year × month × band keys and all four months. Do not impute missing months. The coordinates are existing regional proxies, not observed feeding locations.
5. **Estimation.** Report each region separately: mean separation, Pearson r, OLS slope per 10 SIC percentage points, HC3 coefficient interval, all n! year permutations as exchangeability-conditional diagnostics, leave-one-season range, detrending and circular-shift diagnostics. These are descriptive sensitivities with six/nine seasons and no project-wide multiplicity correction. No pooled significance test of 15 independent climate replicates.
6. **Transport.** Fit the primary two-species model once on all nine Signy seasons, then apply its unaltered slope/intercept to Palmer. Compare absolute errors against the frozen Signy mean, not against a mean estimated on the Palmer test outcomes. Flag climate values outside Signy's observed range and give shared-support scores separately. Do not clip invalid predictions. This is a geographically external, retrospective diagnostic with a published target cohort, not prospective forecast validation or an independent causal test.
7. **Claims and preservation.** Keep the Signy three-species r=0.784 result intact. A weak or different Palmer association limits generality and is not a refutation of Signy's local observation. Differences between the two regions may reflect ecology, years, sampling stage, colonies or methods. No competition, prey capture, or 2050/2100 abundance prediction is inferred. Publish code, annual data, source hashes, numerical checks and an updated narrative on PR #206 only; frozen Paper 1/Paper 2 remain unchanged.
