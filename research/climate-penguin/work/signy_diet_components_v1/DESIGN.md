# Signy diet components: the content of the sea-ice association

2026-11-06. Exploratory explanation of an already observed association, not a preregistration. The original species attribution and Palmer external comparison are known. This specification precedes calculating component–SIC associations in this extension.

1. **Question and primary cohort.** Does the nine-season Signy gentoo mean fish-fraction association reflect variation in the fraction of stomachs with recorded positive fish wet mass, variation in fish fraction conditional on such mass, or their covariation? Use the same 2001/02–2009/10 cohort, 73 valid gentoo stomachs, and the exact source/QC/food denominator used in `palmer_signy_diet_external_v1`. Gentoo is chosen because the existing species decomposition identified its mean fish fraction as carrying the original association, not because of new component results. Adélie annual values are a support table, not additional tests.
2. **Definitions.** For each valid stomach, q = fish / (fish + crustaceans + cephalopods + other food). Preserve explicit fish zeroes, exclude missing components, and never count krill twice. For each season, p = n(fish mass > 0)/n; c = mean(q | fish mass > 0); m = mean(q) = p c. The event is recorded positive wet mass, not true dietary absence/presence or a tracked individual's switching. If n-positive is zero, c is undefined, retained as NA, and not assigned zero. Require all nine gentoo c values for the specified primary component analysis; do not silently change its cohort.
3. **Exposure and inference.** Use the pinned GOPT 0–100 km August–November equal-month mean for the season's starting year. Fit each of p and c separately to SIC with intercept, nine equally weighted seasons, slope per 10 SIC percentage points, HC3 t intervals (df=7), Pearson r, one-season omission, detrending and circular shifts. Enumerate the same 9! SIC permutations for both components and use max absolute r to adjust the two-component family. A constant component has no defined correlation test. This adjustment does not cover prior project-wide exploration or establish exchangeable climate years.
4. **Exact accounting of the mean-response slope.** Set P = mean(p) and C = mean(c), with seasons equally weighted. Decompose m into constant P C, frequency term C(p−P), conditional-composition term P(c−C), and covariation term (p−P)(c−C). Regress all three varying terms on the identical SIC design and verify their slopes sum to the m slope. Report signed allocations and one-season-omission sensitivity without interpreting them as causal contributions. For centred plots, subtract the covariation term's own mean. Do not substitute mean(p)mean(c) for mean(p c).
5. **Recovered masses and external reference.** Report mean recovered fish, crustacean and total food mass in grams by season, and their descriptive SIC correlations to show whether composition alone obscures changes in recovered material. These are one-time recovered meals, not intake rates or prey availability. Palmer Table 1 reported occurrence is not established to equal positive fish mass; ear bones and other traces were recorded. Its mean/occurrence ratio may be shown only as a separately labelled, assumption-dependent reported-detection conditional fraction, with rounding bounds. Do not compare Palmer occurrence or derived conditional fractions to Signy's positive-mass components as a harmonized regional effect, and do not add Palmer component–SIC tests.

Retain the original three-species result and the completed regional transfer result. Report the new decomposition as the observable content of the local association, not proof of warming, competition, individual diet switching or regional biological mechanisms. Integrate code, annual values, figure, report and manuscript additions on Draft PR #206; main and frozen Paper 1/Paper 2 stay unchanged.
