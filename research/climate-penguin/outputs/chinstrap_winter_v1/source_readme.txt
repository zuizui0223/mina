Raw ARGOS tracking data for chinstrap penguins are held here. 
The following headers are provivded:

PTT - the ID# of the tag	
LOC_DATE - time and date of the location estimate	
LOC_CLASS - standard ARGOS alpha-numeric code relating to error in in location estimate
LATITUDE - estimated latitude in decimal degrees	
LONGITUDE - estimated longitude in decimal degress
STAGE- code indicationg adult or juvenile 
COLONY - the colony of origination


Available nest census data are held here. 
The followng headers are provided:
YEAR - The year of the nest census.  Here, 2017 is used to denote the 2017-18 austral summer breeding season. 
ADB - Admiralty Bay colony	
CAS - Cape Shirreff colony	
CRV - Cierva Cove colony
