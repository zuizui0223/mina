# Emperor moving fast-ice colony footprint: original-source observability gate

LaRue et al. 2024 original satellite/aerial survey XLSX files (Git commit 13f71112da43c1fd082273677757b41c550457ed) are downloaded and exact Git blob verified by CI. The schema of every original worksheet is logged, with candidate per-observation latitude, longitude, observation year and quality metadata. If per-image satellite scene coordinates are present, they are not necessarily the actual colony centroid or colony relocation. Only if positions are ground-truth colony footprints with repeated same-year/next-year support can the analysis estimate kilometer-scale movement. Static site attributes and modeled abundance N_mean do not encode annual colony displacement. No extinction, movement or substrate-causality claim until that check passes.
