# Emperor (ice) versus Pygoscelis (terrestrial) allocation benchmark

**Original source:** LaRue et al. 2024 (Proc R Soc B), DOI 10.1098/rspb.2023.2067; Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.m63xsj48v; public authors' GitHub davidiles/EMPE_Global pinned commit `13f71112da43c1fd082273677757b41c550457ed`. Three author CSVs are vendored unchanged with original Git SHA. The 50 emperor colonies have modelled *adult count index* posterior marginal means/medians for each of 2009–2018. The full joint fitted posterior samples are NOT in those CSVs.

**Critical estimator distinctions:** The sum of individual colony medians falls by 12.38%, but the actual posterior of the **global sum** has median change near −9.5% (as published). Inverse Simpson effective number computed from individual posterior marginal means falls 10.39%; from marginal medians 12.77%. Neither is the posterior median or posterior 95% interval of N_eff. These are inspectable point-estimate allocation indices, not a calibrated cross-species significance test.

**Decomposition:** 2009 and 2018 had 47 colonies with modeled index positive both years; two positive→modeled zero (BBAY, UMBE) and one modeled zero→positive (LEDD). Setting just the two lost colonies' **2009** index to zero changes N_eff by only −0.61%, whereas persistent 47 colony N_eff falls by 10.51%. Complete physical colony extinction and annual colony geographic displacement are not measured by these data. Emperor source is observationally corrected with a Bayesian model, with transient blinking/non-occupancy.

**Arithmetic fixed-composition null:** All initial colony weights multiplied by T2018/T2009 leave N_eff exactly unchanged; this is the same mathematical null for ice and terrestrial, but NOT the Palmer/Signy measurement-error-aware fixed-composition test. Model uncertainty, posterior dependence, spatial grain and occupancy detection cannot be assumed equivalent. No p-value is computed from only marginal posterior summaries.

**Terrestrial data:** Original mina MAPPPD site/season/method one-row census, 2009 and 2018, positive at same site and same method. Only five species-region-method panels have at least two matched sites, largest ADPE Victoria Land is 6 source *sectors* on ONE Ross Island, not six independent colonies across different land islands; selection precludes testing actual extirpations. The Ross district comparator aggregates six sectors into Cape Bird, Cape Crozier and Cape Royds, one island. The modelled emperor 50 independent named colonies span Antarctica; no spatially matched estimator of substrate effect is available.

**Interpretation:** Avoid claims substrate mobility itself causes abrupt loss, that two named emperor zero states are permanent extinction, or that island breeding nests spread/contract at the colony *location* level solely from site-count N_eff. Extrapolation to movement (>2km), site persistence, and demographic response to warming requires distinct detection-corrected yearly site coordinates and multiscale breeding-area maps. Original Palmer/Signy within-colony concentration results remain independently valid.
