# BAS original modelled krill density rasters — 2011–2020 original-source gate

Source metadata (publicly published 2025): Freer JJ et al. (2025), **Modelled spatial predictions of the distribution and density of Antarctic krill in the South Scotia Sea between 2011–2020**, BAS UK Polar Data Centre, DOI [10.5285/4fd0a1bf-da1a-4021-82eb-2fc513910e32](https://doi.org/10.5285/4fd0a1bf-da1a-4021-82eb-2fc513910e32). Data storage: 50 year-specific GeoTIFF files (annual_krill_rasters) and 6 decadal raster files; documented raster product values from binomial occurrence GAM, Gaussian conditional density GAM and their product, with mean and cross-validation SD. Associated code: https://github.com/jjfreer/krill-48.2. License: UK OGL v3.0.

This is an *entirely different* data layer from the previously linked IMR Table 2 region-wide acoustic biomass series: BAS rasters **are spatial predictions from a previously published model**, not independently acquired site-level acoustic soundings. Furthermore the model uses annual environmental inputs including salinity, ice-edge distance, surface temperature, mixed-layer depth and spatial geomorphology, so treating predicted krill and its model-input salinity as independent covariates would be statistical circularity.

The source-only gate uses the publisher's public ZIP archive and records SHA, all native raster filenames, year completeness, common coordinate reference, pixel resolution, bounds, nodata and data type. **No correlated penguin outcome is opened in this step**. Only if all genuine rasters and spatial validity pass do we consider a fixed original Signy-colony-centred distance-band extraction, strictly separating land/ocean/NoData and identical native grid cells, then a prospective season-aligned (not causal) comparison.

For any later cross-layer calculation: match original model year T acoustic survey Jan-Feb T before breeding season T, not by arbitrary calendar offsets; delineate marine areas around the verified BAS Signy colony GPS (not arbitrary 0 km distance from land cells); annual raster pixels in the model domain are not independently observed years or penguin local foraging tracks. Access to raw Norwegian 2011–2020 acoustic soundings remains by collaborator request; the BAS raster is **already modelled**.

Do not integrate with future CMIP6 nesting abundance or vegetation as a validated mechanism. Frozen Paper1 PR189, Paper2 PR199 and 216 family unchanged.
