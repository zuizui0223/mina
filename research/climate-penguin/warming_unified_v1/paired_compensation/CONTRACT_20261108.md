# Paired-count demographic compensation audit — analysis contract (2026-11-08)
Status: **exploratory, hypothesis generated after seeing first/last values from v0.4; NOT independently preregistered**. This file fixes the six-site test and interpretation before examining *all-year* summed-count slopes. This extends the accepted PR #206 warming paper, not Paper 1/2 or main.

## Exact research question
At six strictly co-breeding sites with both species directly counted in the same original years and comparable observation method, does a rise in Gentoo while Adélie declines correspond to a stable/increasing **two-species counted-nest subtotal** (demographic compensation) or does the combined count decline? Could such a subtotal conceal the loss of Adélie nests? This is **not a claim about total penguin community abundance**, because Chinstrap or other penguins can be present but are not part of this two-species subtotal. It is also not a test of individual movement, temperature causality, demographic vital rates, or competitive replacement.

## Pre-specified calculations (within six sites only)
1. For each paired year: `A` = directly counted Adélie nests; `G` = directly counted Gentoo nests; `P=A+G` = two-species subtotal; `q=G/(A+G)`; `log_ratio=log(G/A)`; `log_P=log(A+G)`. All exactly positive and real, and all interpreted as observed nests, not all breeding pairs on an island.
2. For each site: first to last same-year changes `A_last-A_first`, `G_last-G_first`, `P_last-P_first`, percent change `100(P_last/P_first-1)`, and signed `compensation=(G_last-G_first)/max(1,A_first-A_last)` solely when A loss >0. The compensation ratio measures arithmetic offset in the **two species**; it does not demonstrate immigrating Adélie were replaced by Gentoo.
3. Across *all original common years*, fit unweighted OLS slope against actual calendar year for `log_P`, `log(A)`, `log(G)`, `log_ratio`, converted per decade. The count series differ in length/time window; no unstandardized pooled slope, and six sites are not independent region-scale experiments.
4. Fixed robustness: recompute subtotal `log_P` site slope after dropping each site's first year and after dropping each site's last year (only when >=5 years remain). Calculate site-level Spearman correlation of actual year and `log_P`. Summarize individual sign consistency, do not choose a subset by result.
5. Descriptive site-equal medians and ranges for first/last P change and ratio fold, plus count of sites with A negative, G positive and P positive, and count with positive all-year P slope and endpoint-sensitivity agreement. No p-value from six sites or environmental units. No historical null claim or novel universal law without independent data.
6. A secondary source support audit records whether the **Chinstrap** records are observed at the same six site-years using one known comparable method per site: do not add incomplete Chinstrap as an assumed zero or convert a two-species subtotal to a whole-community claim. A missing Chinstrap count is missing, not absence.
