# Frozen support-first sensitivity: three penguin species on identical census years

**2026-11-08 JST.** Status: outcome-aware exploratory follow-up after manuscript v0.5, not independent preregistration. The question was fixed after seeing two-species subtotal results but before opening complete-case Chinstrap counts for the new site-level trajectories. This is the *one prioritized robustness test* of the central positive result, not a new free-ranging search.

## One biological question

Does the finding that gentoo increases can numerically mask Adélie nest losses survive counting **Adélie, gentoo, and chinstrap** together on precisely the **same site-years and census method**? This concerns a *three-species subtotal*, not all penguin species, individual movements, resource competition, future nesting forecasts, or the causal effect of Antarctic warming.

Exactly ARDL, BISC, LION, LLAN, PCHA and PETE. Retain their original exact `common_years` lists, no interpolation, no site dropping based on the response.
- Original uniquely resolved MAPPPD site×species×season records: `outputs/comparison_audit/mapppd_unambiguous_site_seasons.csv`, Git blob `fc134e364cf38699fa7611d7dee5fbbbcb370c0a`. Confirm A and G positive nest counts, exact original year and count method, and C (CHPE) nonnegative recorded nest count in that year **with the same known method**. A source CHPE count of zero is a real zero and remains; unobserved CHPE is **missing**, not zero.
- Outcome-blind source support already audited: 54 complete triplets in **five sites** (ARDL 21, LION 7, LLAN 15, PCHA 6, PETE 5) spanning 10–25 years; BISC has no eligible CHPE years, so is explicitly unavailable for this sensitivity, not a negative case. There are 13 original two-species years with no matched CHPE input; these are never imputed.
- Support gate: all 54 paired triple years and all five supported sites reconstructed without duplicates; each site has at least 5 recorded years and 10-year first-to-last span. If it fails, stop with a source-only report; no post hoc change of matching rules.

## Fixed calculations; full same-years comparison

For each of the 54 site-year tuples, derive:
- `P2 = ADPE + GEPE`; `P3 = ADPE + GEPE + CHPE`, with direct source nonnegative counts and no pseudo-counts in subtotals.
- `share_ADPE = ADPE/P3`, `share_GEPE=GEPE/P3`, `share_CHPE=CHPE/P3` (the composition of these three counted species only).
- Site-specific first-to-last absolute and percentage change in `P2`, `P3`, ADPE, GEPE and CHPE on these **same years**.
- Site-specific unweighted `log(P2)` and `log(P3)` linear slopes per decade against actual calendar years, and `log1p(CHPE)` descriptive slope (zero-safe). No pooled site×year OLS or fabricated confidence interval.
- For sites with >=6 distinct triplet years, repeat `log(P2)` and `log(P3)` slope after dropping the first or last observed year; for PETE with only 5 triplet years, report endpoint deletion as **not eligible**, rather than claiming robustness.
- Per-site sign concordance `sign(ΔP2)=sign(ΔP3)` and `sign(slope(P2))=sign(slope(P3))`. Also report whether `P3` increases when ADPE declines and GEPE increases; never call it caused by the GEPE increase.
- Site-equal median/range of P3 first-to-last percentage change, not a pooled total across heterogeneous census years. Site-equal counts of positive/negative P3 endpoint and slopes; list site-wise signs and identify any changes relative to the exactly same-year P2 baseline.

## Fixed reporting rules

If at least four of five sites have P3 endpoint increase *and* the P3 slope is positive in at least four, say **'three-species subtotal increase recurrent in this selected complete-case five-site cohort'**. If not, say **'the two-species subtotal result is not robust to completing the three-species count'**. Only use 'endpoint-robust' for sites with >=6 actual years and both drop-first and drop-last slopes retaining the original sign. Any result remains exploratory (site selection, only two regions, shared climate cells, unequal spans), **not a general macroecological law**.

Explicitly distinguish between:
(a) original two-species result 5/6 from 67 site-years;
(b) two-species restricted to 54 complete triplet site-years in 5 sites;
(c) three-species subtotal on those same 54 site-years.

No restoration of excluded BISC or missing CHPE years using zeros, site totals, nearby locations or different methods. No causal warming inference, individual dispersal/competition claims, or numeric future population prediction. Preserve frozen Paper1/Paper2 and `main`; target only existing warming working PR #206 after verification.
