- Dunn et al. 2016 (10.1371/journal.pone.0164025) already explicitly proposes fur seal competition at Signy; annual chinstrap–gentoo success is positively correlated (r=0.73). Novelty is a direct adjusted species contrast, not the competition idea.
- Dunn et al. 2025 (10.1111/gcb.70290) already tests seal–ice links. Signy fur seal abundance declined ~47% 1977–2024; no migration front inferred.
- Cannone et al. 2022 (10.1016/j.cub.2022.01.074) records plant surveys in 1960, 2009, 2018, with marine vertebrate disturbance categories in recent surveys. This is not a 50-year annual vegetation panel. Seal trampling/nutrient mechanisms are already documented (10.1007/s13280-022-01782-4). No vegetation mediation fit with three survey periods. Palmer static co-location is not longitudinal damage.

## Primary estimand
Common 1996/97–2019/20 CEMP-era breeding seasons, season_start t. February t+1 island-wide adult Antarctic fur seal haul-out count is an index of seasonal presence, not foraging abundance or a timed exposure. Do not sum island totals and zones; require all six nonoverlapping surveyed zones or explicitly complete official island total, no missing-to-zero. Preserve count dates and zone roster. Other seal taxa secondary only. Use previous winter sea-ice duration (days) with unambiguous preceding temporal support; if source year ambiguous, stop. One season is one independent observational unit before autocorrelation. Source-only receipt precedes manual explicit schema mapping; no guessed seal/ice columns.

Outcome is recorded creched/expected-to-fledge chicks per breeding pair, not actual individual survival. Use existing original stage parser and SHA pins. On common years choose persistent literal source colony labels with valid paired chick/nest counts and nest-before-chick dates in all retained seasons for Adélie and chinstrap (>=3 labels each, no overlapping composite units); annual median ratio across fixed labels; gentoo source island aggregate ratio. For roster eligibility use only support and dates, not ratio magnitude; record exclusions and common years; no optimizing window. If fixed roster fails, stop primary and report all-observed as exploratory only. Preserve grain mismatch. Minimum 20 complete common seasons, >=6 fixed four-calendar-year blocks (anchored at 1996), full-rank design. No interpolation, no duplication, no automated outlier rejection. The normalized analysis input is one row per complete year and must come with reviewed mapping, source hashes and calendar/roster receipt.

Primary Y = chinstrap chicks/pair minus gentoo chicks/pair. OLS Y ~ standardized log1p(Feb fur seal count) + standardized preceding sea ice days + centered linear year. Estimate raw chicks/pair difference per 1 SD log-seal index; calendar-gap-aware HAC (Bartlett, lag 2) t interval, plus leave-one-calendar-block-out coefficients. Primary negative contrast supported only if interval upper bound <0 and every leave-one-block-out slope <0; chinstrap absolute slope must also be negative to describe reduced chinstrap success (otherwise only relative contrast). Positive or inconclusive equally reported. Adélie vs gentoo and absolute species slopes descriptive, no rescue endpoints. First difference on adjacent seasons only and previous-February seal association are fixed sensitivity checks, not interchangeable primary hypotheses. February counts may postdate Adélie/Gentoo chick counts; even the primary association is retrospective, not causal. Lagged seal index does not measure actual current exposure; daily pre-endpoint base observations from 1992 may enable a separate timing-aware analysis after source access.

Competing explanations: negative relative slope consistent with differential competition but could reflect different climate/prey sensitivity; positive association consistent with common prey/resource response, not proof of facilitation. Need temporally prior seal use, independent krill measures and foraging overlap to attribute competition. No whole-island demographic or vegetation effect claim. No southward shift claim. No forecasts.
