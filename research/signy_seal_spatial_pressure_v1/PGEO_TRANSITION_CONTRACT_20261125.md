# PGEO relative colony redistribution: pre-effect contract, 2026-11-25 JST

Source: Méheust et al. 2024 DOI 10.1007/s00442-024-05523-0, original v1 DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.22341229.v1 artifact 14987514463 SHA256 d67a47fa9c280da82ace6d471f1ea42a63fdc402f4b6aa6c0734ca68317632ab. Original paper already reports breeding success versus next-season colony growth; do not claim novel discovery of that association. Here estimate within-colony, within-island-year conditional relative change and a matched forward/backward contrast. Descriptive, no individual movement or causal public-information claim.

Structure-only audit executed before contract: 3068 rows, 83 merged colonies, 1258 strict paired colony-seasons across all years (1154 in prior common-adult-year subset). Source README adults = breeding pairs, chicks = creched chicks, year = November start. Strict footprint coverage as previous scripts: union literal hyphen-separated components within merged code, every constituent counted exactly once for each outcome, exclude ambiguous P45/PF10. Require positive current pairs, observed chicks 0..2*pairs, observed next pairs >=0 with identical complete footprint. Join exactly t+1, no bridging missing years. 1035 transitions, 79 units, 18 start years, four measured next zero counts. Use all eligible island-year strata; missing island-year not imputed. 

Primary OLS equal row weight: log1p(next pairs) on log1p(current pairs), current chicks per pair, colony fixed effects and island×start-year fixed effects. Interpret productivity coefficient as conditional future relative abundance, not actual tracked movement. Report partial R² against same-sample baseline removing productivity; all leave-one-year, leave-one-island and leave-one-colony fits (require identifiable regressors). Sensitivities only: equal total island-year weight; add squared log1p(current pairs); strictly positive endpoints log(next pairs), log(current pairs). Report estimates not p-values/CI; shared exposures and serial/spatial dependence. No alternative windows or variable selection. 

Matched temporal diagnostic: restrict to same t with complete observed t-1 pair count >=0. Estimate both forward log1p(next pairs) and backward log1p(previous pairs) with identical primary predictors, FE and matched sample. Report forward minus backward coefficient (equivalently regression of forward-minus-backward outcome) and all leave-year/island fits. This tests temporal asymmetry, not a valid randomized placebo. Coupling through current count, measurement errors, density dependence, habitat time-varying factors may still confound both. Directional support requires positive primary coefficient and all year/island deletions positive. Stronger asymmetry requires positive matched forward-minus-backward difference and all year/island deletions positive. If fails report lack of distinction. Do not promote secondary fits to primary. Freeze before effect fitting. No climate or recruitment pathway estimation yet. Existing manuscripts unchanged.
