# Signy haul-out spatial redistribution, V1 (2026-11-24)

New descriptive source analysis, distinct from closed seal–penguin competition tests and frozen Ecology Report. No joint penguin/vegetation outcomes, no environmental covariate search. Prior biological trends known from Dunn et al. (2025), DOI 10.1111/gcb.70290: the decline became pronounced around 2015. Waluda et al. (2010), DOI 10.1007/s00300-009-0706-2, described earlier island-wide haul-out abundance; local counts and whole-island counts are known to correlate (Favero-Longo et al. 2011 supplementary material). Novelty of this new comparison is not established; the target is whether the six-zone allocation changes across the published recent decline, independently of changes in total. Site-specific presence is a candidate index of pressure, not measured vegetation damage, land-only density, animal-days or competition.

Source: Dunn et al. (2025), https://doi.org/10.5285/8e910ac8-6f52-4548-bbcb-78474b559294; original CSV member SHA256 58f87d83afdd78f30daf5489f45f4221af30b1e413090c3d9a5493e602eacb3a. The publisher reports unchanged six-zone boundaries, whole-zone census and adult counts on land and adjacent sea ice. Raw area labels are not verified polygons. Source-only structural receipts inspected previously (run 45356876730); joint numerical outcome not examined in designing this analysis. Study uses recent protocol, reference 2010–2014 (2010 publication to before reported decline), late 2016–2024, excludes transition year 2015. No searching for alternative breakpoints. Every year of these fixed windows is audited even if unobserved.

Gate: all rows within six explicit zones, no ambiguous aggregates, no missing/negative/nonintegral adult fur counts, no exact duplicated area or overlapping numerical composite labels within zone, no ambiguous mixed numeric/text labels (unless solely numeric comma/dash ranges), no explicit incompleteness in comments; allow unique nonnumeric place names per official subarea census protocol, flag named subareas and audit strict numeric-only sensitivity separately. Require explicit February month somewhere and no other nonblank month; blank month on individual rows allowed under published protocol. Explicitly zero is zero only in otherwise complete zones; no missing-to-zero. Zone boundary continuity from source, not geometry. Report all excluded years and reasons. Minimum 4 eligible years per period; stop if insufficient. Companion elephant and Weddell counts on identical eligible years, only if all valid; these use different habitats and are descriptive comparators, not controls.

Primary descriptive effect: equal-year mean difference (late minus reference) in unbiased pairwise concentration H=sum n_i(n_i-1)/(N(N-1)), conditional on annual N>=2 (no finite-count increase under fixed composition in multinomial expectation). Report annual inverse-Simpson effective zones separately, period mean totals, per-zone mean count and mean share, and late zone counts relative to expected late total times reference equal-year mean shares. The latter is a descriptive counterfactual allocation, not forecast. Report leave-one-year-out range for primary contrast and zone count ratios; no p-value or independent-zone inference. Show whether any zone has nondecreasing mean count despite falling total, and whether relative concentration rises. Named-area exclusion sensitivity requires at least four years in each period, report blocked if not possible. Equal-year weighting fixed; no model substitution. A positive difference is spatial concentration of recorded haul-out counts, not proof of density dependence, warming, vegetation impact or permanent movement. No inference of terrestrial-only pressure or actual vegetation response. No new ecological result promoted until actual source panel and receipts reviewed.

Execution: separate branch from main, new source acquisition + reproducibility code, no edits to frozen manuscripts or blocked PR218. Synthetic tests check grouping, missingness, composite overlap, annual counts/finite-size identity. Output annual zone counts and audited result only.
