# Independent nutrient-composition check: Kerguelen fur seal scats (2026-11-24)

Status: measured concentration reaggregation, not a Signy calibration or a new independent test of the original paper's conclusions.

Gilbert et al. (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.scitotenv.2025.179195, explicitly measured variation in 20 elements and found colony-specific nutrient contributions. The raw composition and sample metadata workbooks are available in the authors' public repository https://github.com/Lola-san/Agazella.nutrient.release.Ker (data DOI 10.48579/PRO/9PR5YU). For this targeted illustration P and Fe were selected because the paper identifies contrasting terrestrial/marine biogeochemical roles; not an exhaustive search for favorable elemental results. GitHub source blobs verified: res_compo_scats.xlsx f68fff27dcf408170c5ffe626db4eff92c49f65d; data_scats.xlsx adef3decc6c154b055d81062e9f7539eff1c894b. Original compiled and metadata worksheets decoded directly from connector-returned XLSX bytes, with ZIP member sizes and CRC32 checked, using an independent JavaScript reader. No original files or individual records republished. 60 unique matching sample IDs; exclude CN12 (the authors' stated potential contamination rule in _targets.R), leaving 28 Cap Noir and 31 Pointe Suzanne samples. Both P and Fe are strictly positive numerical measurements, no censoring substitution needed. P and Fe in micrograms per gram dry matter; 1,000 micrograms per gram = 1 gram per kilogram. Samples need not represent distinct individuals. Two colonies, no spatial replication. This is a descriptive derivative of a known study, not new proof of nutrient limitation or a causal relationship.

Equal-dry-mass P is lower at Cap Noir under all 59 deletions; Fe direction at equal dry mass is NOT robust. The ratio of P/Fe concentration ratios is 0.717518. The pup-suspicion exclusion is a descriptive check, not sex- or age-standardization. No p-value, CI or generality claim.

Let q = total dry fecal mass at Cap Noir divided by Pointe Suzanne; assume sample mean concentrations represent respective exported dry fecal mass, no retention/volatilization or other pathway. Relative P output = 0.780447 q; relative Fe output = 1.087703 q. Opposite rankings (CN lower P, higher Fe) are possible for 0.919368 < q < 1.281317, and the full and every single-scat-deletion concentration reaggregation share the narrower sufficient interval 1.057086251 < q < 1.211819340. These bounds are arithmetic scenarios, not measured mass ratios or actual site-level fluxes. No data on nitrogen excretion from these workbooks and no elephant/Weddell comparison.
