# PGEO land–sea interaction: pre-effect contract (2026-11-25 JST)

Question: Does local topographic wetness modify the association between common winter coastal polynya exposure and breeding success? Hypothesis: if higher winter P100 is associated with better breeding conditions, the negative relative performance of wetter (poorly drained candidate) colonies may be amplified at lower P100. Direction of P100 × topographic wetness is predicted positive, not a proven flooding mechanism. TWI is static topography, not observed snow, water or runoff. Existing Méheust et al. Oecologia (2024) model examines spatial variability in mean-year-adjusted breeding success and local environments; this new estimand concerns temporal variation of spatial contrast, not first land habitat effect. Same geographic region, not a causal or independent regional replication. Do not test alternative habitat variables or radii to obtain desired signs.
