## Fixed literature comparison
Smykla et al. (2015), DOI 10.1016/j.catena.2015.04.011, Table 1, original institutional PDF https://www.iop.krakow.pl/pobierz-publikacje%2C915 (printed p61). Primary classes active (5 samples), relict (5), barren fellfield (6); upper 0–10 cm; means and SD. Total N, total P, extractable P in mg/g. Compare relict/active ratio for each; compare (P total/N total) of arithmetic class means, also separately the published mean sample N/P (not interchangeable), and extractable/total P ratio of class means. Barren background as descriptive anchor and background-subtracted relict/active contrasts only where both excesses are positive. Literature describes regional relict occupation ca 1630–1220 BP but no exact age by sampled soil. Do not fit decay, convert concentrations into stocks, or infer plant limitation.
