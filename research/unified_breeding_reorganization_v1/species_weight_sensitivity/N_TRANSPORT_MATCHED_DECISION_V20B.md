## Integration and next gate
This is an independent measurement system relative to the Palmer census and vegetation archive, but only one station and season, and a secondary comparison of previously published data. It is not an independent biological replication of the published Marambio finding. It supports a bounded quantified before/after difference in detected concentration; seasonal persistence is prior art. No multi-region longitudinal plant/animal bridge exists in the sources examined. Cape Hallett spatial nutrient data lack repeated departure series; Windmill 2000–2022 archive currently not public; published 2019 multi-region N transects and later cryptogam compilation lack verified repeated animal–plant support (V15). Do not infer long-term abandonment persistence from seasonal observations or combine with V18 as a measured mass budget. 
The next biological question is whether changes in nutrient *source persistence and spatial footprint* follow repeated breeding losses; requires independent synchronized multi-season NH3/deposition and animal observations, or matched repeated plant/soil records with measured exposure. No more exploratory sector/window/threshold tuning. Preserve evidence boundaries.
