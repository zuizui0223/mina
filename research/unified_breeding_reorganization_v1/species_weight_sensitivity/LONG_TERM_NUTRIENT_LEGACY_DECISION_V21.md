## Next decision
The existing evidence motivates differential chemical legacies, not a new nutrient-loss claim. A new macroecological question is whether independently measured present-day vegetation patterns relate to documented former breeding geography after controlling modern colonies and local moisture/topography. Koerich Dryad provides SDM predictions, not original occurrences, and predicted moss distributions already incorporate current seabird distance; do not use predictions as independent plant outcomes. Plantarctica original static map could support a spatial descriptive comparison after validated coordinates and same-land geomorphic reference support; it cannot measure greening. With only four relict localities and two comparative regions the Wood dataset alone cannot identify a general ecological effect; expand independently dated localities first (USAP 600145, 601212, 601327, 601374 and original published chronology tables) without assigning region centroid coordinates or latest dated tissue as exact abandonment. For decay rate specifically obtain genuinely paired dated surface-soil nutrient observations. Xu Mendeley record contains carcass photographs rather than matched chemistry. No repeated surrogate correlation search; no main/frozen manuscript modifications.
