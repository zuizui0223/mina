## Fixed estimands
For every year use positive total observed monitored nests N; p_i=count_i/N. Fixed q_i=map union footprint_i/sum footprint; a_i=published physical land_i/sum land; f_i=map union footprint_i/physical land_i.
1. Primary count-vs-map allocation mismatch TV(p,q)=0.5 sum |p_i-q_i|; overlap=sum min(p_i,q_i)=1-TV. Also count-weighted fixed mapped-cover fraction E=sum p_i f_i. These are different spatial quantities; neither is measured penguin-plant contact or vegetation change. Compare first/last fixed years 1991/2017; report all annual results, not a selected maximum.
2. Count concentration H=sum p_i², effective islands 1/H, largest island share; area-normalized relative intensity p_i/a_i, concentration relative to area D=sum p_i²/a_i (1=equal density across whole mapped land), and observed counts/whole mapped km². All hypothetical loading shares are identical to counts under constant within-species per-nest emission; no new nutrient-flux evidence. Report actual endpoint total/density changes separately from normalized shares.
3. Exact proportional decline reference for 2017 holds 1991 shares fixed with same observed 2017 total. Compute island differences and aggregate E/TV/H/D. No noise model or significance, only deterministic composition contrast. Report sum of excess nest equivalents = deficit and exact redistribution index; not movement.
