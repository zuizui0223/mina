## Estimands
For counts n0, n1 of each species, d=n1-n0, G=sum positive d, L=sum absolute negative d. Total delta G-L. Gross change G+L. Hidden/cancelled fraction 2min(G,L)/(G+L), undefined if gross=0. The exact minimum and maximum of a hypothetical additive species-weighted nest index change over time-invariant relative weights w_s in [1,R] are G-RL and RG-L. For nonzero total delta and mixed signs, strict sign reversal is possible exactly when R>max(G,L)/min(G,L); equality allows zero. One-sided changes are sign-invariant for all strictly positive weights; zero-total mixed changes permit both signs for any R>1. This is an identification/sensitivity statement for a hypothetical linear index, not an estimate of an ecological function or nutrient flux. Actual weights are unobserved. Do not use cross-sectional soil concentrations as weights, even provisionally. Weights vary by species but are fixed within each comparison; no claim of a common weight that reverses all sites.
