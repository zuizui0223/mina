# V19 decision — spatial allocation versus fixed mapped vegetation
26 November 2026. Executed on original-source-derived Palmer five-island annual monitored counts and original SCAR-clipped Plantarctica map receipts; not independent replication. No new raw data download or new Actions run. No merge or manuscript modification.

## Results
- Five-island monitored Adélie totals fell 6689 to 140 (−97.91%), 1991–2017. All five island groups declined. Under constant within-species nutrient output per nest, potential source shares equal nest shares, but actual emissions/retention are unmeasured.
- Litchfield: 497 to zero monitored nests, 7.43% to 0% count share, versus 78.95% of the later mapped vegetation-plus-lichen union. Humble: 1085 to 68 nests, 16.22% to 48.57% share, versus 15.76% of the mapped union. These are not evidence of historical vegetation establishment or observed inter-island transport.
- Count-weighted fixed mapped cover E increased 8.256% to 14.495% (+6.239 pp). In contrast the total variation distance between count shares and fixed mapped vegetation-plus-lichen area shares rose 0.71522 to 0.78952 (+0.07430); equivalently allocation overlap fell 28.48% to 21.05%. These distinct metrics show why an increase in average mapped cover at the islands carrying the remaining nests does not imply improved spatial correspondence across the network. Neither is actual nest-plant contact.
- Under proportional decline with the same total of 140 nests, preserving 1991 shares predicts 22.71 nests at Humble versus observed 68, and 63.12 at Torgersen versus observed 22. The sum of positive residuals is 54.04 nest-equivalents, 38.60% of 2017 total (equals sum absolute negative residuals). This is composition accounting, not measured migration or a fitted null distribution.
- Endpoint effective island count 3.449 to 3.079 and area-normalized relative density concentration D 2.506 to 2.947. But the five-year mean sensitivity reverses both concentration directions (ΔNeff +0.093, ΔD −0.244). Do not claim persistent regional concentration; composition shift and decline are distinct. Whole-land mean monitored nest density fell 8255 to 173/km²; no measured increase in nutrient loading or nesting density at any island.
- Fixed sensitivity 1991–95 vs 2013–17 means: count-vs-union TV +0.07573, weighted mapped cover +3.407 pp. Leave-Litchfield-out changes ΔTV from +0.07430 to −0.31049; leave-Humble-out changes ΔE from +6.239 to −2.497 pp. Thus the network mismatch and weighted cover change are driven by different islands. The three groups with invariant reported component counts (COR/HUM/LIT) retain ΔTV +0.21962 but only +0.151 pp in E. Stable component count is not verified component identity. Vegetation-only TV rises +0.07430; lichen-only TV falls −0.03119. No general increase in mismatch across all cover classes.
- Cumulative 66,237 monitored nest-season counts (not distinct nests or cumulative guano) give TV 0.72404 to the same later mapped cover. No historical cover observations.
