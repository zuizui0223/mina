# Main figure captions (working draft)
1. Accounting routes to increased concentration. Synthetic examples only, not observed data. N is total; ΔH is change in uncorrected Simpson concentration; C is the replacement component of Bray–Curtis. The examples distinguish differential attrition, differential amplification and opposing absolute changes; not individual movement.
2. Species-equal changes in concentration (ΔU) and signed squared share distance (D²), full 10 common taxa and exact-year seven common taxa. Grey segments: 5th–95th percentiles under the fixed-total gamma CV20 conditional reference. Observed markers and network-bootstrap 95% intervals only where the prespecified valid-draw gate passes. Strict bootstrap intervals withheld. Exact-year cohort is nested, not replication.
3. Panel-wise frequency of opposing local changes and positive-to-zero counts, and equal-species mean C and material C≥0.05. Grey intervals are fixed-total gamma CV20 simulation ranges, not field-calibrated error. Observed full cohort intervals are network bootstrap intervals; exact-year intervals withheld. A recorded zero is not verified extirpation.
4. Six selected Antarctic fixed-roster panels, same-start and same-end lag comparisons. Different axis scales in A–F (Litchfield effect is much larger). G–I summarize the 71 overlapping same-start comparisons at each lag; five local panels were selected for previous decline. No independent per-window inference or duration causal claim.
5. Ross illustrative disturbance and rebound (1999, 2001, 2002). Total counts, six-component composition, absolute loss and regain by component, and TV distance from pre-shock composition at two spatial grains. Restoration = 1−TV(pre,rebound)/TV(pre,shock). All six components remained occupied. One disturbance case; no general recovery law or adult survival inference.
