# V2 matched temporal-scale diagnostic, fixed before new scores (2026-11-25)
Question: does net breeding composition change and signed concentration accumulate over longer calendar intervals when the same starting years are compared? Previous Ross lag results and the five declining Palmer/Signy trajectories are known. This is a post-result diagnostic, NOT a fresh confirmation of concentration or a representative population sample.
Exactly six previously frozen local panels: Palmer COR/HUM/LIT (official ERDDAP SHA256 b4ef04e2275ea779fc8fe54fa13528dc2052d37dd88a60c811d54c7601f67b16), Signy Adélie and chinstrap (publisher CSV SHA256 as original figure builder), Ross six components (external frozen CSV Git blob eaf47bf87158ef219d75b1be9eb8e50d0703ebaa on commit 58fd7f4d0246c19344098dc5ce309343514346d2). Reuse original figure-builder roster functions from main pinned commit 94fadb8ddd62e2ea6ac53b4d9605fdab61b21ff1, verify original endpoint effective numbers and, for Signy, all annual values. No new roster selection. Positive total >=2, integer counts only; no missing imputation. Litchfield full extinction is outside conditional-share estimand, not absence of effect.
Primary matched START: exact calendar y,y+1,y+5,y+10 all present. Secondary matched END: y,y-1,y-5,y-10 all present. No requirement for intervening missing years; endpoint only. At least 3 anchors for a panel-specific summary; otherwise report support only. Fixed 1,5,10 years; 20 years omitted from common primary because several local series are short. For each panel and lag compute median signed count-corrected Simpson delta U, median signed two-sample D2, median TV, median log(total later/earlier). Report 10-minus-1 median within-anchor differences, fraction positive; D2 negative retained. Report abundance strata using prior whole-interval 10% cutoff, but no extra tests on sparse cells. Matched end checks avoid privileging common starting years; both remain confounded with chronological drift and abundance. No p-values treating overlapping starts, species, or Palmer islands as independent replicates.
Mechanistic boundary: a positive lag contrast can follow long-term directional trends and is not an intrinsic time-scale law. Five panels selected previously as declining and already known to concentrate. Ross is recovery comparator, not independent random sample. No statement of globally increasing concentration without unselected long series; JNCC different data grain and time spans. No new environmental regression; no changes to frozen prior manuscripts.
