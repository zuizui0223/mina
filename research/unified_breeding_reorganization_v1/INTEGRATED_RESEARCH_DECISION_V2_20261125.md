# Integrated research decision V2: 25 Nov 2026
## Candidate central claim
**Spatial reorganization of monitored breeding networks occurs beyond population decline; the long-term concentration pattern is not restricted to declining networks.** Distinguish amount N, signed concentration U, and reorganization D2/TV. An independent geographic data source (JNCC) supplies a comparative all-direction test, not an independent replication of the earlier JNCC decline analysis.
## New source-executed evidence
- Matched local time-scale diagnostic: official Palmer and BAS source hashes and original frozen N_eff endpoints checked, all 44 Signy annual N_eff values checked; Ross pinned source CSV hash checked. Six local panels in three geographic systems. Same starts 71 anchors (64/71 deltaU10−deltaU1>0, panel-median difference >0 in all six); same ends 72 (66/72 and all six). TV and D2 10−1 median positive in all six for both schemes. Ross 10-year median concentration +0.04856 with median log(total ratio) +0.11870 for same starts. Five panels previously selected for decline/concentration; local lag contrasts are descriptive, overlapping and may reflect secular drift rather than causal duration. No claim of population-level independence or general temporal scaling. Source run 45583915094, artifact 15008250718.
- JNCC strict within-network same census year at both epochs: all 663 panels (389 country-master networks), decline/stable/growth n=457/47/159. Seven of prior ten common species retained with >=3 observations in each class. Equal-species mean deltaU +0.06282/+0.05246/+0.05759, signed D2 +0.12335/+0.04314/+0.08582. All above the 5–95% conditional model range under multinomial and gamma-CV20 assumptions, but stable D2 only narrowly above gamma q95 0.04195. Network-block bootstrap has 1833/1999 (91.7%) valid draws, below 95% gate: all bootstrap intervals suppressed, no difference inference. Decline-growth point difference +0.00523 is not equivalence. Actual survey coverage, correlated errors, adjusted count protocols and changed boundaries remain unresolved. Same-source sensitivity not a new external replication. Source run 45584082355, artifact 15007197974. Census elapsed medians 19/18/18 yr; time still varies across networks.

## Next single analysis
Use the already acquired exact JNCC count vectors to distinguish differential attrition from heterogeneous growth and simultaneous absolute gains/losses, conditional on actual totals; test whether concentration is accompanied by gain at a subset and loss elsewhere, rather than calling a change in shares movement. Compare all-direction full vs exact-year, with fixed source roster and explicit null. No outcome-dependent endpoint search. This identifies accounting patterns, not individual causal mechanisms. No additional bibliography search or new source acquisition until this core question is resolved.
