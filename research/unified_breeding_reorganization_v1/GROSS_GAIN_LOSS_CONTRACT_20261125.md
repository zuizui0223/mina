# V3 gross gain–loss accounting of breeding-site reorganization (2026-11-25)
New question: does local opposing change occur even when total breeding counts increase, decline, or stay approximately stable, beyond a fixed-composition observation model? Previous all-direction concentration and exact-year results known; no prospective external replication. No search for favorable thresholds or alternative endpoints after scoring.

For each panel d_i=n1_i-n0_i, G=sum positive changes, L=sum absolute negative changes; G−L=N1−N0. Primary counterflow index C=2 min(G,L)/(N0+N1), nonnegative, independent of units within panel; report mean species-equally, median panelwise, and group fractions C>0 and C>=0.05 (at least 5% of mean observed total in opposing changes). In growing networks, C measures observed losses, in declining networks observed gains, in exact stable networks each. C is an accounting metric, not movement, recruitment or mortality. Secondary absolute local outcomes: any local decline, any local gain, observed positive-to-zero and zero-to-positive labels; proportion of baseline occupied sites recording zero later; counts NOT confirmed extirpation/colonization, source effort/boundary uncertainty remains. Split observed positive concentration deltaU into mixed (G,L>0) versus unidirectional; distinguish concentration from counterflow (mathematically possible without counterflow), do not interpret conditional association as causal. Report number of panels with positive deltaU in both modes. 
Null: 999 pooled two-epoch independent multinomial draws per panel fixed actual N0,N1; same second model independent gamma CV20 weights before multinomial, pooled shares p=(n0+n1)/(N0+N1), fixed totals. Simulate C and material event C>=0.05, compare species-equal observed statistics to model 5–95% intervals and tail fractions; positive outcome requires both metrics above q95 in both models per class. The full panel is the primary, exact-year subset nested sensitivity; no claim of actual survey error calibration, correlated effort, causal movements. Compare within-class only; C near stability can be larger mechanically, do not interpret class differences causally. 1999 whole Country×Master network bootstrap, same fixed species, retain only draws all species × strata present; report 95% intervals if >=95% valid, else suppress. Count units heterogeneous; never sum raw birds across panels, do not treat overlapping networks as independent islands.
