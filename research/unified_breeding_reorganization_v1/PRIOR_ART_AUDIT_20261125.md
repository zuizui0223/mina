No new biological data or outcomes opened. Sources checked during this review; DOI or official links are the reference anchors.

## Decision
The defensible candidate empirical contribution is a large matched, within-taxon breeding-network comparison across declining, near-stable and growing **observed totals**, conditional on the recorded totals, with both concentration and the existing replacement component measured, exact-year sensitivity, and selected Antarctic time series illustrating different arithmetic routes. This is original secondary-data macroecology, not a meta-analysis of published effect sizes, a new diversity index, or an independently collected field dataset. No "first ever" assertion. The searches cannot exclude a prior equivalent JNCC analysis, unpublished or in book chapters. Before submission inspect full census methodology/species chapters, especially Herring Gull and Lesser Black-backed Gull (official low comparability at national trend level), and independently audit changes in Site.ID boundaries and effort. A common year and count unit do not resolve all these issues.

## Manuscript naming
Use "countervailing local change" or "replacement component of Bray–Curtis" for C; no new named index. Use "concentration of monitored breeding counts" not physical breeding area. Use "network of monitored components" rather than metapopulation unless connectivity is independently known. Use "recorded zero" rather than extinction. "Near-stable" describes two measured totals, not temporal stability. No causal or temporal-synchrony inference from two censuses.
