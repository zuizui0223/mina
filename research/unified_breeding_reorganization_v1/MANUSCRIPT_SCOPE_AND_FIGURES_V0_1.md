## Figure plan (not yet rendered)
1. Concept: exact existing Bray–Curtis decomposition plus relative share metrics. Explicit examples of differential attrition, differential amplification and opposing changes, not individual movement.
2. JNCC concentration and signed squared share distance: three observed-total classes, full common 10 species, exact-year common 7 and conditional model ranges. Distinguish descriptive counts from species-equal estimates and nested samples.
3. Countervailing local change: full/strict recorded opposing-change frequency, positive-to-zero frequency, C and 5% event rate vs both reference models. Full bootstrap intervals only; strict withheld.
4. Matched local start/end time-scale contrast plus mixed/nonmixed concentration counts. Litchfield large effect should be visible, not allowed to obscure other panels; exact same anchors, no pooled independent p-value.
5. Ross illustrative pre-shock / shock / rebound counts and shares; both six-component and three-colony grains, correct state restoration definition.
