# Antarctic v0.6 revised figure 3
The six main-figure structure remains unchanged; Fig. 3 is replaced by the aligned historical/projected SIC and geographic coverage comparison, and the previous full-scenario SIC/SOS Fig. 3 becomes Supplementary Figure S1 (unchanged from v0.3). Figs. 1,2,4–6 are unchanged. No new ecological fit.
Fig. 3. Change in count-weighted physical sea-ice anomaly under the same fixed early and recent allocations. A, historical change fields (1993–2000 vs 2011–2020 for long rosters, 2004–10 vs 2013–20 for short) and projected change field (SSP5-8.5, 2081–2100 minus 1995–2014). Open symbols show three selected climate models (triangle up ACCESS-CM2, square BCC-CSM2-MR, triangle down CESM2), vertical ranges are their spread, not confidence intervals, filled symbols means. B, exact geographic between/within terms and sum (diamond); projection is model mean. C, share of recorded noncontemporaneous site-median sums held by the six sites lacking historical hydrographic coverage. D, parent minus complete contrast, with retained renormalization, excluded within-group change and between-coverage-group terms. The four rosters are nested and not independent. Positive signs do not imply fitness benefits, migration or climate adaptation. No biological forecast. All variables and alternative scenarios remain reported in V9/V10 tables and Supplementary Fig. S1.
