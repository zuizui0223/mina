## Scientific question
How does the changing marine environment alter the distribution of exposure, biological responses, and land-use footprint of Antarctic marine vertebrates? The same organizing distinction is amount versus composition versus location. Do not equate concentration with geographic reweighting or infer a causal chain from different samples.
Candidate title: **Reorganization of marine exposure and terrestrial ecological footprints in Antarctic colonial vertebrates** (provisional; terrestrial footprint is observed use/overlap, not measured ecosystem effect).
For a fixed roster define N=sum(n_i), p_i=n_i/N, E=sum(p_i*x_i). For an environmental variable x, ΔE = Σ mean(p_i)Δx_i + Σ mean(x_i)Δp_i. The first term describes environmental change at fixed locations; the second describes the changing distribution of recorded breeding counts. These are established accounting identities, not a new index or a behavioral inference. Relative composition TV, concentration H, and the replacement component of Bray–Curtis C are complementary. No multiplication of fitted coefficients from different datasets.
