## Results
Five same named site / date blocks, seven overlapping pairwise contrasts: Biscoe Point ADPE/GEPE; Kopaitic Island all three; Penguin Island ADPE/CHPE; George Point CHPE/GEPE; Hanna Point CHPE/GEPE. Distinct species nominal sampling coordinates separated 4.76–217.41 m; identical site and date do not establish same substrate, density or local history. No additional matched sites, no pooled unpaired species effect. Full six-outcome summary table in PYGOSCELIS_SOIL_PAIRED_SUMMARY_V16A.csv.

NH4-N ratio (geometric mean of equal-site ratios): CHPE/ADPE 2.64678 (higher 2/2); GEPE/ADPE 0.59055 (lower 2/2); GEPE/CHPE 0.62321 (lower 3/3). Individual site NH4-N ratios rounded: Biscoe GE/AD .6534; George GE/CH .9127; Hanna GE/CH .7451; Kopaitic CH/AD 1.4997, GE/AD .5338, GE/CH .3559; Penguin CH/AD 4.6713. Available P CH/AD 2.33335 across 2 sites, GE/CH .63451 across 2 sites, GE/AD 1.30480 from only 1 site. Directions differ by nutrient; no uniform "less fertile" species ordering. Water and organic C covary, not controlled; no inferential statistics because 2–3 paired sites per contrast and shared sites. The new result is an observational comparison of source-derived means, not an estimate of species-specific guano release, plant response, or a temporal species-replacement effect. 

## Integration
The earlier same-site species turnover results motivate a new testable hypothesis: species identity and spatial replacement may change the terrestrial nutrient environment even where summed nest counts do not fall. Current observed soil contrasts are only initial evidence for non-interchangeable *soil contexts*, not calibrated species weights. A biological conclusion about replacement requires repeated comparable soil sampling during actual species turnover, including occupancy history, substrate, microclimate, density/area and deposition/uptake measurements. No multiplying soil concentrations by nest counts or future marine coefficients. Direct native plant response, Cape Hallett GIS and UAV dependencies remain unresolved. Do not tune covariates or significance to rescue this small dataset. Preserve manuscript v0.6, other branches and main; no submission or merge.
