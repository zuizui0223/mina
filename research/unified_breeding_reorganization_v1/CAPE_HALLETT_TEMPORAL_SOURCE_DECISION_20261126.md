## Executed support and measurement audit
10,374 records = 3,458 original grid IDs × 3 years. Each grid ID has stable local x/y, no duplicated grid-year. All three functional group blanks coincide; missing != no cover. Nonmissing 3,343 (1962), 2,057 (2004), 3,458 (2018); 2,057 common cells all three. This establishes actual repeated vegetation support, not a novel result from the original paper. Ground-truth class comparisons, using 174 original author comparison records per group (not independent validation): exact four-class agreement moss 140/174, algae 115/174, lichen 102/174; binary true positives among actual-presence records 117/125, 118/132, 9/74, respectively. Lichen predictions contain no patchy/heavy classes and 65/74 actual presences are recorded as no cover; caution against inferring true disappearance from RGB classification. The grid-scale temporal response could be analyzed on matched nonmissing cells, but published vegetation transitions alone are not a new animal effect.
## Animal and geometry support
CHAL strict original 2456 table: 18 aerial nest-count seasons, no exact vegetation-year match; UAV 2023 match only. Original v5 5487-row CSV: 28 literal numeric Adélie nest records in 25 seasons; 2018 is the one exact vegetation survey season with two conflicting aerial records, 43688 from manaakiwhenua2021aerial and 46991 from kpri2020report6. Do not average, replace, or treat as no penguins. 1961 record not exact 1962 vegetation label; published 1961/62 timing needs source-specific date verification. Counts are colony-wide, not 1m-grid local exposure.
Jeong et al. 2026 [Scientific Data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-08053-x), six UAV flights in November 2018, 2021–25. [KOPRI older source record](https://kpdc.kopri.re.kr/search/8ef94868-4924-4363-a862-b9d34678e86f) provides EPSG:32759 bbox, 66.38 GB orthomosaics and reference marker/residual files, marked Request required; latest version exists but direct access status not confirmed in current retrieval. No automatic data request or giant download. Published 2018 plant and UAV flights are 28 and 25 Nov. The literal NW corner and all four metadata corners project inside the reported rectangle, but the literal four-corner area is 8,848.49 m², inconsistent with the 3,458 m² grid (120×30 envelope). Side lengths ~205,265,21,95 m, not the declared rectangular grid. No ad hoc coordinate correction. Need original 2018 georeferenced crop or surveyed plot control points and actual UAV valid-data masks, not just a bounding box. The 2.02–2.38m published median co-registration residual already exceeds the plant 1m grid; use resolution and registration sensitivity only after verified crosswalk. [2023 station recolonization study](https://doi.org/10.3390/d15010051) is separate anthropogenic disturbance footprint, not verified same vegetation plot.
## Decision
New usable independent temporal plant archive is obtained. Local plant–animal temporal link remains unverified. Next distinct work: recover the small plot georeferencing/crop and UAV footprint/control point metadata and, if permitted, crop 2018 and later imagery to a verified common footprint. Classifier validation must distinguish vegetation functional groups, snow and guano, and retain ground truth; no extrapolation from 174 original photos to all years. Do not multiply colony counts by grid plant cover or infer direct interactions. No new ecological coefficient in this step; manuscript v0.6 and prior drafts remain unchanged.
