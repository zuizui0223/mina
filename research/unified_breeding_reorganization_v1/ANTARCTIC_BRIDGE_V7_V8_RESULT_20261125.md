## Decision
The core common-roster physical accounting is now historically and prospectively aligned without a biological prediction. Historical positive ice reweighting and future physical anomaly exposure are different estimands; neither guarantees adaptation. V7 and V6 failed direct temporal bridge gates. Keep all existing terrestrial evidence: Palmer seal-detection/vegetation overlap and Shirley mapped relic footprint as spatial contrasts, not causal plant change; Signy opposite concentration changes and seal numerical noncompensation as allocation contrasts, not direct interaction or nutrient-flux measurements. Do not tune periods, radii or support thresholds. Broader Antarctic draft v0.2 remains author-review synthesis with separate evidence modules; narrow v0.2 and earlier frozen PRs unchanged.
