For fixed field f, geographic share P_tg and conditional field mean μ_tg, A=Σ ΔP_g mean(μ_g)+Σ mean(P_g)Δμ_g. These are exact between-region and within-region terms. Group and site contributions in full JSON use a common parent equal-site field reference where the parent field exists; otherwise complete-cohort reference. Individual contributions depend on centering; aggregate terms do not. A second decomposition uses the six excluded and retained sites as groups, preserving their original counts. Parent-minus-complete equals retained-within minus complete contrast + excluded-within + between-group share change. This is accounting, not an ecological causal mechanism.

## Why hydrographic complete-case coverage changes the result
Six excluded Victoria Land sites account for 49.4112% early and 60.0729% recent median nest-count sums in long parent23; 73.8733% and 78.0207% in short parent15. They are excluded from historical complete hydrography, not censored by effect sign. Their CMIP6 fields exist. Long future parent-minus-complete SIC = -1.606267 pp = -0.802358 (retained re-normalization) -0.092149 (excluded-group within change) -0.711760 (between coverage groups). Historical long difference +0.487578 = +0.064188 +0.065090 +0.358300. Short future difference -0.632943 = -0.356821 -0.044333 -0.231789. Thus complete-case results are not interchangeable with parent results. Missing historical salinity at six sites cannot be filled by a future-model anomaly.
