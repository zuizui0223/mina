## Scientific decision
The plausible distinctive contribution is the explicit historical-vs-projected physical-change-field comparison on identical monitored breeding-site rosters, alongside separate multivariable exposure-level accounting. It is an original comparative application and source-joining study, not a new decomposition, general theory of climate tracking, first demonstration of marine–land coupling, or calibrated biological forecast. Historical R and projected F are different quantities; comparing their signs as if they measured the same process is invalid. V9 calculates the missing historical change-field analogue A=Δp·(x1−x0). For SIC the late-century high-emissions three-model mean is opposite to A for all four overlapping rosters; across all scenarios/periods 4/16 same-sign means, 0/16 agreement with all models. Salinity 4/8 same-sign means, 1/8 all-model sign agreement. This is not a significance test or independent validation. Past observed and future modeled anomaly intervals differ. The comparison identifies arithmetic direction dependence, not biological benefit; historical absolute concentration and change fields are distinct.

## Publication boundary
Broad manuscript is an author-review candidate for a multi-source synthesis, not yet one causal empirical study. Main Figs. 1–3 are coherent; Figs. 4–6 are bounded separate systems. Cannot claim more novelty merely by combining old results. The earlier narrowly focused empirical manuscripts and this version share data and findings; they cannot be submitted with overlapping substantive results absent explicit author decision, citations and journal disclosure. Full systematic search, publication-specific citation/rights check, and authorship approval remain pending. No submission/merge.
